# Mixed-Troop Versatility Equipment

## Overview
Commanders with Integration/Versatility specialties (able to lead any troop type — e.g., Mulan, Hector, Charlemagne, Julius Caesar) generally do not have a dedicated named Legendary equipment line the way single-troop-type specialists do. Instead, community guidance recommends building around balanced attack/defense/health stat lines rather than a troop-type-locked set.

## Statistics
Pending research — no specific named Legendary "mixed-troop" set was identified in available sources for this pass.

## Recommended Usage
Equip balanced stat-line gear (attack/defense/health spread evenly) rather than troop-type-specific pieces, since these commanders' value comes from flexibility across compositions.

## Best Users
Integration/Versatility-specialty commanders leading mixed Infantry/Cavalry/Archer marches.

## F2P Alternatives
Standard Gold/Purple balanced-stat equipment from the Blacksmith is generally sufficient — there is little reason to chase troop-type-locked Legendary gear for a commander who isn't troop-type-locked.

## Strategy Tips
When running a mixed-troop march, equip gear matching whichever troop type makes up the majority of the march rather than trying to average across types.
