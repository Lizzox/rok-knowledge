# The Bard's Pendant

## Overview
The Bard's Pendant is the best-regarded gathering accessory in the game, granting a flat bonus to resources gained from gathering. Notably, there is no Legendary-rarity gathering gear in Rise of Kingdoms — gathering equipment tops out below the Legendary tier, which simplifies investment decisions for this category.

## Stats
> Exact percentage not independently verified this pass — commonly cited around +5% extra resources gathered; confirm via in-game item preview.

- Slot: Necklace (accessory)
- Rarity: Gold (top gathering-equipment tier — no Legendary gathering gear exists)

## Best Users
Dedicated gathering-march commanders (Cleopatra VII, Seondeok, Casimir III, Pepin III, Ishida Mitsunari).

## Best Pairings
Combine with other gathering-focused equipment pieces crafted from Animal Bone (the material type exclusive to farming/gathering gear, separate from Iron Ore/Leather/Ebony used for combat gear).

## Crafting Priority
High priority for accounts running dedicated gathering marches, since gathering equipment doesn't compete with combat-gear materials (Animal Bone is gathering-exclusive).

## F2P Value
Excellent — gathering gear is comparatively cheap since it caps below Legendary rarity, and Animal Bone doesn't compete with combat equipment material pools.

## Endgame Evaluation
Since gold can be gathered faster with better farming gear, and materials don't cross over with combat equipment, this is a good investment for any account actively running gathering marches, F2P or whale alike.
