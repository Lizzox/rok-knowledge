# Ring of Doom

## Overview
Ring of Doom provides a proc chance to significantly increase normal-attack damage for a short duration, making it a strong choice for commanders whose kits emphasize normal attacks over active-skill damage (e.g., Takeda Shingen, William Wallace's Smite-based kit).
