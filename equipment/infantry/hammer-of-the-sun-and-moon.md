# Hammer of the Sun and Moon

## Overview
The Hammer of the Sun and Moon is a Legendary weapon-slot piece obtained as a Season of Conquest KvK reward, widely recommended as the primary Infantry attack weapon for endgame builds.

## Stats
> Exact base stat values and special-effect percentages were not independently verified in this pass — check the in-game Blacksmith preview or a current build calculator (e.g., RokHub's Equipment Builder) before finalizing a build around specific numbers.

- Slot: Weapon
- Rarity: Legendary
- Primary contribution: Infantry attack

## Best Users
Infantry-specialty commanders serving as rally leaders or garrison primaries (e.g., Richard I, Charles Martel, Guan Yu, Liu Che).

## Best Pairings
- Paired with **Conqueror's Helm** (Infantry variant) and pieces from the **Eternal Knight** set for a full Infantry build emphasizing health and defense alongside this weapon's attack contribution.
- **Karuak's Humility** recommended for the leg slot in the same build.

## Crafting Priority
Per general equipment prioritization: weapon first (largest single-stat contribution, unlocks 2-piece set bonuses), then helmet (activates set bonus), then chest, gloves, and boots last.

## F2P Alternatives
Lower-rarity (Gold/Purple) Infantry weapons from standard Blacksmith crafting; prioritize completing a full lower-rarity set before chasing this specific Legendary piece, since set bonuses often outweigh a single strong item without its set partners.

## Endgame Evaluation
Considered a top-tier Infantry weapon choice for high-spender and whale accounts building around KvK Season of Conquest content; F2P/low-spend accounts should treat it as a long-term target rather than an early priority given its KvK-reward acquisition path.
