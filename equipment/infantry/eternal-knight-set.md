# Eternal Knight Set

## Overview
The Eternal Knight set is a Legendary Infantry equipment line valued for its health and defense bonuses, forming the durability backbone of high-spender Infantry builds alongside the Hammer of the Sun and Moon and Conqueror's Helm.

## Stats
> Exact stat values and set-bonus thresholds not independently verified this pass.

- Primary slot referenced: Chestplate (full set spans multiple slots per standard equipment structure)
- Rarity: Legendary
- Focus: Health, Defense

## Best Users
Infantry tanks and garrison defenders prioritizing survivability over raw damage.

## Best Pairings
- **Hammer of the Sun and Moon** (weapon) for damage, **Conqueror's Helm** (Infantry) for the set-activating helmet.
- **Karuak's Humility** (legs) to round out the build.

## Crafting Priority
Chestplate typically ranks third in general slot priority (after weapon and helmet).

## F2P Alternatives
Gold/Purple defense-focused Infantry chestplates; the durability gain from a complete lower-rarity set often outweighs a single unmatched Legendary piece.

## Endgame Evaluation
A cornerstone of the current top-tier Infantry defensive build for high-spender/whale accounts; F2P players should treat it as a long-term goal reached through steady blueprint/material accumulation rather than a rushed purchase.
