# Formations

## Overview
Formations are unlockable presets (via Formation books) that boost stats for a specific tactical purpose: attacking marches, defending garrisons, or reinforcing allies. Only one formation of each category can be active at a time and formations must be manually switched depending on your current action.

## Recommended Usage
- **March/Attack Formation** — increases march attack/health stats, use before sending an attacking march or joining a rally.
- **Garrison/Defense Formation** — boosts defending troop stats inside your own city, switch to this when not actively attacking.
- **Reinforcement Formation** — boosts stats of troops sent to reinforce an ally's city or a Fort.
- Forgetting to switch formations is a common and costly mistake — always check your active formation before initiating rallies, defending, or reinforcing.
