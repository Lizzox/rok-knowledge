# Talent Trees

## Overview
Talents are passive bonuses arranged in branching trees. Each commander can invest in trees matching their specialties (e.g., a Cavalry/Skill commander can build Cavalry or Skill talents, not Infantry).

## Statistics
- Talent points are earned by consuming Talent Books (Universal or type-specific).
- Trees typically top out around 60–70+ points depending on commander/update.
- Nodes provide flat or percentage bonuses to: attack, defense, health, marching speed, rage generation, skill damage, healing, deceleration/stun/silence resistance, garrison bonuses, gathering speed, and more.
- Keystone/final nodes usually grant a unique powerful passive (e.g., extra skill damage on crit, immunity chance, rage refund on kill).

## Recommended Usage
- **Rally leader builds** favor rage generation + skill damage + defensive survivability keystones.
- **Rally joiner (secondary) builds** often favor pure damage/attack keystones since they don't need to survive as the front.
- **Garrison builds** prioritize defense, healing, and damage-reduction nodes.
- **Gathering/farming builds** prioritize load capacity and gathering speed nodes (rare dedicated trees on gathering-specialist commanders).
- **Open-field solo builds** balance survivability keystones with damage.

## Strategy Tips
- Reset cost is free in RoK (unlike many mobile games) — experiment between rally and open-field builds depending on your immediate task.
- Always match talent tree choice to the specialty that lines up with the troop type you're actually marching (an Infantry talent tree does nothing if you march Cavalry).
- Prioritize the keystone (final) node math over spreading points thin — most keystones outperform several minor nodes combined.
