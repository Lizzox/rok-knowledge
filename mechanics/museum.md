# Museum System

## Overview
The Museum is a building where players donate/deposit collected relics and artifacts in exchange for permanent, account-wide passive bonuses (e.g., troop attack, resource production, research speed).

## Recommended Usage
- Prioritize donating artifacts that boost your current governor focus (economy early, combat mid/late game).
- Museum bonuses are permanent and account-wide, so incremental donations are always worth doing during downtime.

## Note on Completeness
Exact artifact lists and bonus values change with content updates; **verify current artifact effects in-game**.
