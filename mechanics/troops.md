# Troop Types

## Overview
Rise of Kingdoms has four troop types, each with a rock-paper-scissors-style damage relationship, plus a fifth "Leadership" pseudo-type used by support commanders.

## Statistics
- **Infantry** — high defense/health, tanks the front line, deals bonus damage to Cavalry, weak to Archers.
- **Cavalry** — high attack and marching speed, best for rallies and burst damage, deals bonus damage to Archers, weak to Infantry.
- **Archers** — high ranged attack, deals bonus damage to Infantry, weak to Cavalry, low health.
- **Siege** — slow, used mainly for razing structures/forts in KvK, not commander-led in the same way.
- **Leadership** — a virtual "troop type" for pure support/Leadership-specialty commanders (e.g., healers) who don't have a preferred combat troop but boost whichever troops they lead.

## Troop Tiers (T1–T5, and beyond)
Troops are trained in tiers (Warrior → Scout → ... up to Tier 5 in most kingdoms, higher tiers unlocked with Season/Kingdom progression). Higher tiers cost more resources/time but hit much harder and survive longer. Power score scales heavily with troop tier.

## Recommended Usage
- Match troop type to commander specialty and to the field composition you expect to face.
- Cavalry dominates rallies and open-field due to speed and burst.
- Infantry anchors garrisons and absorbs incoming rally damage.
- Archers punish infantry-heavy garrisons/forts.
- Mixed marches (e.g., majority Infantry with minority Archer) are common for balanced power/damage output.
