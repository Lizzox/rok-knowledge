# Armaments System

## Overview
Armaments are individual-account stat-boost items attached to Formations (see `mechanic-formations`), not a kingdom-wide or alliance-wide buff system as earlier assumed in this repository. Each Formation supports up to 4 Armaments simultaneously, and their stat impact can rival or exceed equipment bonuses — making Armaments a core endgame optimization system.

## Recommended Usage
- Obtain Armaments through daily **Travel** and **Dispatch** activities (State Forum) — Dispatch grants up to 6 chances per day at State Forum level 18, yielding Sage's Testimony, armaments, and commendations.
- Match Armament/Formation choice to your commander pairing's damage type: Pincer/Delta formations suit Smite (William Wallace, Bai Qi) and Combo (Arthur Pendragon) kits; Wedge/Hollow Square remain solid general-purpose defaults for skill-damage-focused pairings.
- Recycle unwanted Armaments for coins, which fund upgrade materials or formation-choice chests, rather than letting them sit unused.

## Strategy Tips
- Use Travel and Dispatch chances every day — they're a primarily free, F2P-friendly acquisition path, and most sources don't roll over, so missed days are lost value.
