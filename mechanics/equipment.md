# Equipment System

## Overview
Equipment provides commanders with additional stat bonuses and set effects, independent of skills/talents. There are 7 slots: Helmet, Chestplate, Weapon, Boots, Belt, Ring, Necklace.

## Statistics
- Rarity tiers: Green → Blue → Purple → Gold → Legendary.
- Each piece has base stats plus, at higher refinement/star level, a special set-bonus effect when 2 or 4 pieces of the same set are equipped.
- Equipment is crafted from materials obtained via events, Fort/Pass battles, and daily activities, then upgraded with Refinement Stones and Charms.

## Recommended Usage
- **Rally/skill-damage sets** boost skill damage and rage generation — best on rally leaders.
- **Tanking sets** boost defense/health — best on garrison and rally-joining secondary commanders.
- **Gathering sets** (e.g., resource-boosting gear) are farmed early to speed up City Hall progression.
- Legendary equipment sets are best-in-slot but resource-intensive; prioritize the set matching your commander's primary combat role before spreading investment.
