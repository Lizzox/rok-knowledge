# VIP System

## Overview
VIP is a persistent account progression track increased by consuming VIP Points (from VIP items, gem purchases, or events). Higher VIP levels unlock passive bonuses (resource production, troop capacity, building/research/training speed) and access to the VIP Shop, which sells speedups, resources, and Legendary commander sculptures/shards.

## Recommended Usage
- Treat VIP level as a long-term passive investment — bonuses are permanent and account-wide once reached.
- The VIP Shop is one of the most reliable (though gem-costly) sources of Legendary commander sculptures; refreshing it strategically around new commander releases is a common gem-spending strategy.
