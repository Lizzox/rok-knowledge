# Garrison Support Guide

## Overview
Reinforcing an ally's city or a contested structure is a distinct role from being the primary garrison defender — reinforcements add raw troop numbers and stats but don't control which commander leads the defense.

## Recommended Usage
- **Reinforce proactively**, not just reactively — send reinforcements to structures/allies likely to be attacked based on current war intelligence, rather than waiting for an attack to already be underway.
- **Match your march's troop type to the garrison's overall composition need** — a garrison lacking Archers benefits more from an Archer reinforcement than another Infantry march, even if Infantry is your personal specialty.
- **Equip for defense as a reinforcer**, since your stats contribute directly to the garrison's total effective health/defense pool rather than needing your own skill-damage output to carry the fight.

## Strategy Tips
- Coordinate with the structure/city owner or alliance leadership before committing reinforcement capacity, so reinforcement efforts aren't duplicated on an already-secure target while an undefended one goes unreinforced.
- Track your kingdom/alliance's most contested structures during active war windows and pre-position reinforcement-ready marches accordingly.

## Mistakes to Avoid
- Reinforcing an already heavily-defended target while a more vulnerable one goes unsupported.
- Sending a mismatched troop-type reinforcement when the garrison has a clear compositional gap.
