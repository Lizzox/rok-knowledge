# Rally Support Guide

## Overview
Most players in a large rally are joiners, not the leader — being an effective rally joiner is a distinct skill from leading, with different equipment/talent priorities and timing responsibilities.

## Recommended Usage
- **Join early.** Rally join windows are time-limited; joining late (or not at all) reduces the rally's total power for no good reason.
- **Match troop type to the rally leader's request.** Alliance leadership typically calls for a specific troop-type composition — bringing the wrong troop type reduces the rally's effectiveness even if your personal march is strong.
- **Equip for damage, not survivability, as a joiner.** Per [[guide-combat-talents]], rally-joining secondary builds can prioritize pure attack/skill-damage keystones since the rally leader's survivability carries the group.

## Strategy Tips
- Confirm rally timing and target with alliance leadership before committing march capacity — joining the wrong rally (or a rally that gets cancelled) wastes march availability during a critical war window.
- Keep a march ready and available during announced rally windows rather than being mid-gather or mid-cooldown when a call goes out.

## Mistakes to Avoid
- Joining with a mismatched troop type against explicit rally-leader instructions.
- Missing rally join windows due to inattention during active war periods.
