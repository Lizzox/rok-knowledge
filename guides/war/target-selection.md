# Target Selection Guide

## Overview
Target selection is the single highest-leverage tactical decision in competitive play — a well-chosen fight wins itself before troops even clash, while a poorly-chosen one loses regardless of individual march quality.

## Recommended Usage
1. **Scout first, always.** Confirm troop composition and rough power before committing — engaging blind is the top cause of avoidable losses at the endgame.
2. **Assess reinforcement range.** A target near their own garrison/city can call reinforcements mid-fight; an isolated target cannot.
3. **Check for bait patterns.** An unusually exposed, seemingly-easy target near enemy territory may be a deliberate trap — cross-reference with known enemy alliance tactics.
4. **Match troop-type counters.** Use the Infantry > Cavalry > Archer > Infantry relationship (see [[mechanic-troops]]) as a first-pass filter before considering commander/equipment quality.
5. **Confirm your own support.** Don't commit to a contested engagement without a rescue/reinforcement plan from nearby allies.

## Strategy Tips
- Rank potential targets by risk-adjusted value (likely reward vs. likely loss) rather than simply engaging the first available target.
- Communicate target selection to your alliance before committing during coordinated war windows, so allies can support or advise against a specific engagement.

## Mistakes to Avoid
- Engaging based on power score alone without checking actual troop composition and commander pairing.
- Chasing a retreating target into unscouted or reinforcement-heavy territory.
