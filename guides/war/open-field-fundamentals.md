# Open Field Fundamentals (War Guide)

## Overview
This guide extends [[guide-open-field-combat]] with advanced, KvK/war-context-specific principles for open-field engagements, where the stakes and opponent sophistication are typically much higher than standard early/midgame skirmishes.

## Target Selection
- Prioritize isolated, unsupported enemy marches over marches near enemy garrisons/reinforcement range — even a favorable 1v1 matchup can turn into a loss if enemy reinforcements arrive mid-fight.
- Watch for "bait" marches deliberately left exposed to lure an attack into a prepared ambush — cross-reference scouting reports with known enemy alliance behavior patterns before committing.

## Recommended Usage
- Coordinate open-field roaming with your alliance's overall war plan rather than freelancing — an uncoordinated kill, even a successful one, can expose your position or disrupt a larger planned operation.
- Maintain updated scouting on contested map zones continuously during active war windows, not just immediately before an individual engagement.

## Strategy Tips
- Keep a designated rescue/reinforcement plan with nearby allies before roaming deep into contested territory — open-field marches that overextend without support are a common source of avoidable losses in competitive play.
