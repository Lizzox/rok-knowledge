# Kill Event Strategy

## Overview
PvP-scored kill events (most prominently the Killing Enemies phase of [[mightiest-governor-event]]) reward efficient, low-risk kill accumulation over risky high-value engagements — the scoring math typically favors volume over individually impressive kills.

## Recommended Usage
- **Target weaker, isolated marches** for reliable, repeatable kills rather than contesting strong, defended positions that risk your own march for a single kill.
- **Coordinate with alliance mates** to avoid splitting kill credit on the same targets — uncoordinated overlap wastes potential points that could have come from spreading across more targets.
- **Time engagements to the scoring window precisely** — kills outside the active scoring phase (even by minutes) may not count, so confirm phase timing before committing.

## Strategy Tips
- Pre-scout a list of viable low-risk targets before the scoring phase opens, so you can execute immediately rather than spending scoring-window time searching.
- Balance kill-event participation with your Hospital capacity (see [[guide-hospital-management]]) — repeated aggressive engagement without adequate healing capacity converts short-term point gains into longer-term troop losses.

## Mistakes to Avoid
- Contesting the same high-value target as alliance mates instead of spreading across more available targets.
- Engaging targets outside the scoring window due to unclear phase-timing awareness.
