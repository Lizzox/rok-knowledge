# Hospital Management Guide

## Overview
Hospital capacity determines how many wounded troops can be recovered rather than permanently lost after a fight — a common and costly endgame mistake is having combat capability that outpaces Hospital capacity, converting recoverable wounded losses into permanent full losses.

## Recommended Usage
- Upgrade Hospital capacity and healing-speed research in step with your account's actual combat activity level, not just alongside City Hall level — a high-power account that fights often needs proportionally more Hospital capacity than a similarly-powered but combat-inactive account.
- Before committing to a major engagement (rally defense, contested structure fight), estimate expected wounded-troop volume against current Hospital capacity — if the estimate exceeds capacity, expect some wounded troops to convert to permanent losses regardless of the fight's outcome.

## Strategy Tips
- During active war windows (KvK conflict phases, Ark of Osiris, sustained garrison defense), monitor Hospital fill levels actively and consider healing-speed speedups a priority resource alongside construction/research/training speedups.
- Coordinate with alliance leadership on expected engagement volume ahead of major war windows so Hospital capacity can be pre-upgraded rather than discovered as a bottleneck mid-conflict.

## Mistakes to Avoid
- Assuming Hospital capacity automatically scales with combat power — it requires its own dedicated upgrade investment.
- Engaging in high-volume combat activity (repeated rally defense, aggressive open-field roaming) without first confirming Hospital capacity can absorb the expected wounded-troop volume.
