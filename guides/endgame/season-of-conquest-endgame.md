# Season of Conquest Endgame Guide

## Overview
Season of Conquest is the primary endgame content loop, layering Crystal Technology, Armaments, and large-scale cross-kingdom conflict on top of an account's base-game progression. Endgame accounts should treat each season as a semi-fresh strategic cycle rather than an extension of standard KvK.

## Recommended Usage
- Follow the phased Crystal Technology approach in [[crystal-tech-overview]], [[crystal-tech-beginner]], and [[crystal-tech-optimization]] — economy first, then war technology matched to your confirmed combat role.
- Coordinate Armament formation choices (see [[guide-armaments-endgame]]) with your current commander pairing and troop type, since armament bonuses can rival or exceed equipment bonuses in impact.
- Reassess alliance/kingdom-wide strategy at each season transition — Season of Conquest rules and metas are periodically revised between seasons.

## Strategy Tips
- Purchase the Premium Season Crystal Supply bundle on Day 1 if spending, since its value compounds daily from the purchase date.
- Track which phase of the season (honor/prep, conflict, climax) your kingdom is in and shift resource/speedup allocation accordingly, per general KvK-phase principles in [[guide-season-of-conquest]].

## Mistakes to Avoid
- Treating every season identically without checking for rule/meta changes.
- Neglecting Armament optimization in favor of equipment alone — endgame accounts should optimize both systems together, not one at the expense of the other.
