# Armaments and Formations Guide

## Overview
Armaments are attached to Formations and provide substantial stat boosts that can rival or exceed equipment bonuses — each formation supports up to 4 armaments simultaneously, making this one of the highest-impact endgame optimization systems.

## How It Works
- Each **Formation** (see [[mechanic-formations]] for the base concept) can hold up to 4 **Armaments**, each providing its own stat boost.
- Armaments are obtained through daily **Travel** and **Dispatch** activities (State Forum) — Travel yields Sage's Testimony and other armament-related resources; Dispatch (up to 6 chances/day at State Forum level 18) grants Sage's Testimony, armaments, and commendations.
- Unwanted armaments can be **recycled** for coins, which purchase upgrade materials or formation-choice chests — don't let unused armaments sit unrecycled.

## Best Upgrade Order
Use your daily Travel and Dispatch chances every single day without fail — these are the primary armament-acquisition sources and largely don't roll over, so missed days are lost value (the same discipline principle as Sunset Canyon and Lucerne Scrolls daily activities).

## Recommended Usage
- Match formation choice to your current meta commander pairing: with Smite-focused commanders (William Wallace, Bai Qi) and Combo-focused commanders (Arthur Pendragon) increasingly prominent in the meta, **Pincer** and **Delta** formations have become top choices for those specific kits.
- For most general-purpose builds, **Wedge Formation** and **Hollow Square Formation** remain the standard recommendations — but the correct choice ultimately depends on your specific commanders' skill types (skill-damage vs. normal-attack vs. Smite vs. Combo).

## F2P Strategy
Armaments are earned entirely through daily free activities (Travel, Dispatch) rather than gem/cash spending, making this one of the more F2P-friendly endgame systems — consistent daily engagement matters more than spending here.

## Strategy Tips
- Recycle armaments that don't match your current formation/commander build rather than hoarding them — the coin conversion funds targeted upgrades instead.
- Re-evaluate formation choice whenever you significantly change your commander pairing, since an armament set optimized for a skill-damage pairing may be wasted on a Smite- or Combo-focused pairing.
