# Fighting Efficiency Guide

## Overview
At the endgame, raw troop tier and commander investment matter less in isolation than *fighting efficiency* — the ratio of damage dealt/objectives achieved to troops lost. Efficient endgame accounts consistently outperform less disciplined accounts of similar nominal power.

## Recommended Usage
- **Scout before every engagement** — endgame marches represent enormous resource/time investment; engaging blind is the single most common source of avoidable losses.
- **Match formation to task** (see [[guide-armaments-endgame]] and `mechanic-formations`) — fighting in a Reinforcement or Garrison formation instead of an Attack formation (or vice versa) silently costs significant effective stats.
- **Retreat discipline** — endgame players should be willing to retreat a losing engagement early rather than committing fully, since T5 troop losses are extremely costly to replace.
- **Healing capacity planning** — ensure Hospital capacity and healing speed research can actually absorb your expected losses from planned engagements; healing bottlenecks force costly full losses instead of recoverable wounded troops.

## Strategy Tips
- Coordinate rally timing precisely with alliance leadership so marches arrive together — piecemeal arrivals are a top cause of inefficient losses in both rallying and defending.
- Track your kingdom/alliance's typical enemy patterns (common rally timings, favored troop-type compositions) to inform scouting and engagement decisions rather than reacting purely case-by-case.

## Mistakes to Avoid
- Committing full marches to contest structures/objectives without first confirming you can win the engagement.
- Neglecting Hospital capacity relative to your account's actual combat activity level, resulting in avoidable full troop losses when wounded-troop capacity is exceeded.
- Fighting in the wrong formation because it wasn't switched after the previous activity (attacking right after defending, or vice versa).
