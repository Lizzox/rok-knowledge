# T5 Players Guide

## Overview
Reaching Tier 5 (T5) troops (see [[guide-t5-progression]] for unlock requirements) marks the transition into true endgame play, where the limiting factor shifts from troop tier to commander investment, equipment refinement, and tactical execution.

## Recommended Usage
- With T5 troops unlocked, re-evaluate your commander pairing and equipment build — the damage/survivability math changes meaningfully at T5, and a build optimized around T3/T4 assumptions may no longer be optimal.
- Shift research/resource focus toward endgame systems: Legendary equipment refinement, Special Talent forging (see [[guide-endgame-equipment]]), and — during Season of Conquest — Crystal Technology.
- Reassess your account's combat role within your alliance now that you can field top-tier troops; T5 access often means taking on more demanding rally-leading or garrison-defense responsibilities.

## Strategy Tips
- T5 troop training is resource-intensive — plan training queues around your kingdom's actual combat calendar (upcoming KvK phases, alliance events) rather than training reactively.
- Coordinate T5 troop-type composition with your alliance's overall strategy rather than optimizing purely for your own preference — a kingdom benefits from complementary troop-type distribution across its top accounts.

## Mistakes to Avoid
- Training large T5 batches without a clear near-term use, burning resources that could be better timed around an actual combat window.
- Neglecting equipment/commander refinement after reaching T5, assuming troop tier alone is sufficient for endgame competitiveness.
