# Meta Commander Investment Guide

## Overview
At the endgame, commander investment decisions should be driven by current meta tier lists and your account's specific combat role rather than personal preference alone, since Legendary commander investment (skill books, Expertise materials) is one of the most resource-intensive systems in the game.

## Recommended Usage
- Cross-reference community tier lists (e.g., AllClash's periodically-updated rankings) against your account's role (rally-leading, garrison defense, open-field) before committing Expertise materials to a new commander.
- Prioritize completing Expertise on your established primary/secondary pairing before diversifying into additional Legendary commanders — a fully-Expertised top pairing generally outperforms several partially-invested Legendaries.
- Reassess your pairing periodically as new commanders release — per the pairing files in `pairings/`, top pairings do shift over time as new Legendaries are added.

## Strategy Tips
- Watch for "Prime" reworks of commanders you already own (e.g., Sun Tzu → Sun Tzu Prime, Joan of Arc → Joan of Arc Prime) — these frequently represent significant power upgrades over the base Epic version and may be worth prioritizing over an entirely new commander.
- Budget Expertise material investment across a season/quarter rather than trying to fully build multiple commanders simultaneously.

## Mistakes to Avoid
- Chasing every new commander release without finishing your current pairing's investment first.
- Ignoring `detail_status` flags on newer commanders in this knowledge base (several current-generation Legendaries are marked `stub-pending-research`) — verify exact numbers in-game before committing scarce Expertise materials based on unconfirmed data.
