# Lucerne Scroll (Sunset Canyon) Event

## Overview
Lucerne Scroll (part of the Sunset Canyon feature) is a team-based, instanced PvP mode where small squads fight for map-control points within a limited-time match, separate from a player's main city/kingdom.

## Recommended Usage
- Favor commander/troop comps optimized for short, skirmish-style fights (burst damage, mobility) over long-sustain rally comps, given match time limits.
- Coordinate squad composition with teammates ahead of time (a mix of tanky point-holders and burst-damage flex commanders tends to work well).
- Prioritize capturing and holding designated point-control zones over chasing kills, since scoring is typically zone-based.

## Note on Completeness
Specific scoring rules and map layouts vary by season/version; **verify current mode rules in-game**.
