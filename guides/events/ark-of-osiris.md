# Ark of Osiris Event

## Overview
Ark of Osiris is a large-scale, scheduled cross-kingdom team battle where two teams compete to capture, hold, and defend a central "Ark" objective across multiple rounds/days, with match-wide score determining the winner.

## Recommended Usage
- Assign clear roles ahead of the event: dedicated Ark-holders/garrison defenders versus roaming rally squads intercepting enemy pushes.
- Time your strongest rallies around the Ark's flip/capture windows rather than engaging continuously — timing discipline matters more than raw troop count.
- Scout the opposing team's composition and rally patterns between rounds to adjust your team's counter-strategy.

## Note on Completeness
Exact round structure and scoring mechanics vary by version; **verify current event rules in-game**.
