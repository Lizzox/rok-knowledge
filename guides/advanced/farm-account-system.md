# Farm Account System

## Overview
A "farm account" is a secondary, lower-investment account maintained specifically to generate resources, speedups, or specific materials that are then gifted or otherwise routed to a player's main account, common practice among dedicated players once time/attention allows managing multiple accounts.

## Recommended Usage
- Focus farm accounts entirely on gathering-commander investment (Cleopatra VII, Seondeok, Casimir III — see `equipment/gathering/` and `guides/equipment/gathering-equipment-optimization.md`) rather than combat, since a farm account's sole purpose is resource generation.
- Keep farm-account City Hall/building investment minimal beyond what's needed to unlock useful gathering-march capacity and troop tiers — over-investing in a farm account's combat infrastructure defeats its purpose.
- Respect your game's terms of service and your kingdom/alliance's specific rules regarding multi-account play before setting up a farm account — policies vary and this guide does not constitute permission or endorsement of any specific server's rules.

## Resource Management
Route farm-account resources to your main account through whatever legitimate in-game gifting/alliance-help mechanisms are available, prioritizing whichever resource type your main account currently needs most (commonly gold, given its lack of passive production).

## AP Management
Farm accounts benefit from the same AP-cost-reduction talent priorities as a main account's gathering marches — Peacekeeping-tree Insight and similar reduction talents matter disproportionately here since a farm account's entire value proposition is throughput.

## Strategy Tips
- Don't over-invest a farm account's own commander roster beyond gathering specialists — any combat investment on a farm account is likely wasted resource allocation relative to putting that same investment into the main account directly.
