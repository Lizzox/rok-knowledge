# Main Account Optimization

## Overview
A mature main account benefits from periodic systematic audits — checking that resource flow, Action Point (AP) usage, and speedup allocation all match current priorities rather than habits left over from earlier game phases.

## Resource Management
- Regularly review Warehouse protection caps versus actual stockpiles; resources sitting exposed above the cap are pure risk with no benefit.
- Audit gathering-march allocation periodically — as troop tiers and equipment improve, the optimal gathering-territory tier/distance may shift.

## AP Management
- Action Points gate barbarian/fort engagement volume — track your daily AP regeneration against your actual usage patterns (Karuak Ceremony, barbarian farming, fort rallies) to avoid either wasted overflow or chronic AP shortage relative to available reduction talents/equipment.
- Prioritize AP-cost-reduction talents (e.g., Peacekeeping-tree Insight) on commanders you actually use for PvE farming, since AP is often the binding constraint on farming throughput, not march availability.

## Speedup Management
- Categorize speedup stockpiles by type (construction, research, training, general) and track which category currently has the longest queue — general speedups should always flow to the longest-queued category for maximum elapsed-time saved.
- Reserve a portion of stockpiled speedups for known upcoming events (Mightiest Governor phases, KvK conflict windows) rather than spending everything reactively as it's earned.

## Strategy Tips
- Set a recurring personal schedule (e.g., weekly) to review these three systems together, since inefficiencies in one area (e.g., AP) often compound with inefficiencies in another (e.g., under-using gathering equipment).
