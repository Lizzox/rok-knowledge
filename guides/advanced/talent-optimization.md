# Advanced Talent and Build Optimization

## Overview
Once a commander has a complete base talent tree and equipment set, further optimization comes from matching builds tightly to specific opponents/scenarios and from expertise-tree investment.

## Recommended Usage
- Maintain scenario-specific talent presets (rally vs. garrison vs. open-field) since resets are free — don't settle for one "average" build.
- Invest Expertise materials into commanders you use most heavily across multiple game modes, since expertise bonuses stack with base talents for compounding returns.
- Re-evaluate pairings periodically as new commanders release — a top pairing today can be improved by a new amplifier/support commander later.

## Strategy Tips
- Track patch notes for rebalances; a commander's optimal role can shift meaningfully with skill/talent reworks even without a full "Prime" rerelease.
