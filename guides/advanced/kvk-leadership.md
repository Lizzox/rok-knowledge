# KvK Leadership Guide

## Overview
KvK leadership requires planning across the full season arc — preparation, active conflict, and post-season recovery — with clear role assignment and communication structures that individual members can't establish on their own.

## Recommended Usage
- **Pre-season:** assess member combat roles (rally-leading, garrison defense, gathering/economy support) and communicate expectations well before the conflict phase opens, per [[guide-midgame-kvk-preparation]].
- **Active conflict:** establish clear call-out procedures (who calls rally targets, who coordinates garrison reinforcement, who tracks enemy movement) so decision-making doesn't bottleneck through a single overloaded leader.
- **Post-season:** conduct an honest after-action review — what worked, what didn't, which members over/under-performed expectations — to improve the next season's planning.

## Strategy Tips
- Delegate specific KvK responsibilities to trusted officers (see [[guide-officer]]) rather than centralizing all decision-making with the alliance leader alone — bottlenecked decision-making costs critical response time during active conflict.
- Maintain scouting/intelligence-sharing discipline throughout the season, not just during major engagements — consistent map awareness compounds into better target selection and reinforcement timing.

## Mistakes to Avoid
- Waiting until the conflict phase opens to assign roles and establish communication procedures.
- Centralizing all tactical decisions through one person, creating a bottleneck during time-sensitive engagements.
- Skipping post-season review, repeating the same coordination mistakes every season.
