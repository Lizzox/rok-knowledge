# Alliance Officer Guide

## Overview
Alliance officers (commonly "R4" rank) bridge day-to-day member management and alliance-leader-level strategy — effective officers reduce the leader's workload while keeping members engaged and informed.

## Recommended Usage
- **Member management:** monitor member activity/inactivity, welcome new members, and flag consistently inactive members to leadership for potential removal (freeing alliance capacity for active players).
- **Event coordination:** organize participation for recurring events (barbarian-fort rallies, Ceroli Crisis teams, Alliance Mobilization task focus) so members aren't left to self-organize inconsistently.
- **Communication relay:** keep members informed of leadership decisions and relay member feedback/concerns back to the alliance leader — officers who only communicate downward (not upward) create blind spots for leadership.

## Strategy Tips
- Specialize officer responsibilities where the alliance has enough officers to do so (e.g., one focused on recruitment, one on event coordination, one on war/KvK planning) rather than every officer doing everything generically.
- Document recurring processes (recruitment criteria, event sign-up procedures) so alliance operations don't depend entirely on one officer's memory or availability.

## Mistakes to Avoid
- Making unilateral major decisions (member kicks, diplomacy commitments) without leadership alignment, damaging trust within the leadership structure.
- Neglecting the "relay member feedback upward" half of the communication role.
