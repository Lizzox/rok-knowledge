# Diplomacy Guide

## Overview
Diplomacy — managing relationships with other alliances (within-kingdom) and other kingdoms (cross-kingdom, especially during KvK matching) — is a core alliance-leadership responsibility that directly affects a kingdom's competitive standing.

## Recommended Usage
- **Within-kingdom diplomacy:** negotiate territory-sharing agreements and mutual non-aggression understandings with other strong alliances in your kingdom to avoid costly internal conflict that weakens the kingdom's overall KvK readiness.
- **Cross-kingdom diplomacy:** once matched for KvK, gather intelligence on the opposing kingdom's alliance structure and known strengths/weaknesses through scouting and, where possible, direct communication channels.
- **Alliance-to-alliance agreements:** formalize agreements (verbally or via written alliance rules) about territory boundaries, ceasefire terms, or mutual defense pacts, and hold to them consistently — a reputation for reliability strengthens future diplomatic leverage.

## Strategy Tips
- Diplomacy is most effective when backed by credible strength — a weak alliance's diplomatic offers carry less weight, so diplomacy and military/economic development should be pursued together, not as alternatives.
- Keep a small trusted group handling sensitive diplomatic communications rather than allowing uncoordinated member-level diplomacy that could contradict official alliance positions.

## Mistakes to Avoid
- Making diplomatic commitments without leadership alignment, then having them contradicted by other alliance actions.
- Neglecting diplomacy entirely and relying purely on military strength — this often leads to unnecessary two-front conflicts that better diplomacy could have avoided.
