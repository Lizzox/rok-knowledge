# Alliance Rules Creation Guide

## Overview
Clear, written alliance rules reduce ambiguity-driven conflict and give officers a consistent basis for enforcement decisions — vague or unwritten "understood" norms are a common source of member disputes and perceived unfairness.

## Recommended Usage
- **Cover the essentials explicitly:** activity/inactivity expectations, territory/gathering-zone etiquette, event-participation expectations, and rally/reinforcement call-out procedures.
- **Define consequences clearly:** specify what happens for rule violations (warnings, temporary restrictions, removal) so enforcement feels consistent and predictable rather than arbitrary.
- **Make rules accessible:** post rules somewhere all members can reference at any time (alliance description, pinned chat message, external document) rather than relying on rules being verbally communicated once.

## Strategy Tips
- Revisit and update alliance rules periodically (e.g., each KvK season transition) rather than treating them as permanently fixed — an alliance's needs change as it grows or as the game's meta shifts.
- Involve officers in rule-drafting so enforcement has buy-in across the leadership team, not just from the top alliance leader.

## Mistakes to Avoid
- Enforcing unwritten "understood" norms inconsistently across different members, creating a perception of favoritism.
- Writing overly rigid rules that don't allow for reasonable case-by-case judgment on genuinely ambiguous situations.
