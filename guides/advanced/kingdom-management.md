# Kingdom Management Guide

## Overview
Kingdom-wide management extends beyond a single alliance — top leaders across a kingdom's alliances need to coordinate territory control, KvK strategy, and internal conflict resolution to keep the kingdom competitive against other kingdoms.

## Recommended Usage
- **Cross-alliance coordination:** establish regular communication channels between top alliances in the kingdom (leader/officer councils) to align on territory control and KvK strategy rather than each alliance acting independently.
- **Territory planning:** coordinate which alliances hold which forts/passes/resource zones to avoid internal contest over the same territory while external threats go unaddressed.
- **Conflict resolution:** establish agreed-upon norms for resolving inter-alliance disputes within the kingdom (resource-zone disputes, member poaching) before they escalate into damaging internal conflict during critical war windows.

## Strategy Tips
- Build kingdom-wide coordination structures during peacetime (between KvK seasons) rather than trying to establish them reactively once conflict begins.
- Balance competitive rivalry between kingdom alliances (which can be healthy) against the need for unified response to external kingdom-vs-kingdom threats.

## Mistakes to Avoid
- Allowing internal alliance rivalry to fracture the kingdom's ability to respond to external KvK threats.
- Waiting until an active KvK season to establish cross-alliance communication norms.
