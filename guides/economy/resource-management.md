# Resource Management Basics

## Overview
Resources beyond your Warehouse's protected cap are vulnerable to being plundered if your city is attacked; efficient resource management means spending down surplus rather than hoarding it unprotected.

## Recommended Usage
- Upgrade the Warehouse to increase protected resource caps, especially before periods you expect to be offline longer than usual.
- Spend surplus resources on construction/research/troop training rather than letting them sit exposed above the Warehouse cap.
- Use Peace Shields during vulnerable stockpiling phases (e.g., saving for a big City Hall upgrade) rather than leaving resources unprotected.

## Strategy Tips
- Coordinate big resource spends (e.g., troop training pushes) around alliance/kingdom events that offer resource-cost or speedup discounts.
- If you anticipate being farmed, keep resources as low as practically possible above the protected cap — an empty-looking city is a less attractive target.
