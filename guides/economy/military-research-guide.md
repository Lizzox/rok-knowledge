# Military Research Guide

## Overview
Military research is organized by troop type (Infantry, Cavalry, Archer, Siege) plus a set of universal combat technologies. Efficient accounts specialize rather than spreading research evenly.

## Infantry Technologies
Prioritize Infantry Attack/Defense research if your account runs garrison defense or Infantry-led rallies as its primary combat role.

## Cavalry Technologies
Prioritize Cavalry Attack/Defense research if your account runs open-field roaming or Cavalry-led rallies — the most common primary specialization given Cavalry's rally/open-field dominance.

## Archer Technologies
Prioritize Archer Attack/Defense research if your account runs Archer-led skill-damage nuking marches.

## Siege Technologies
Lower priority for most accounts unless running dedicated Engineering-commander marches (Gonzalo de Córdoba, Gajah Mada, Babur, Stephen III); siege units serve niche ranged and structure-razing roles rather than general combat.

## T5 Requirements
Unlocking Tier 5 troops requires:
- City Hall level 25
- Academy level 25
- Every building type at level 25
- Military research maxed to the same standard as economic research
- Specific prerequisite research nodes to level 10: **Combat Tactics**, **Defensive Formation**, and **Herbal Medicine**
- A large gem/blueprint investment (community sources cite roughly 32,000 gems and 16 Master's Blueprints across the full unlock path)
