# T5 Progression Guide

## Overview
Tier 5 (T5) troops represent the current top combat tier in Rise of Kingdoms, requiring a substantial, multi-system investment across buildings, research, and gems. This guide covers the full requirement checklist, sequencing, and common pitfalls.

## Requirements
- **City Hall level 25**
- **Academy level 25**
- **Every building type at level 25** (not just City Hall and Academy — the full building roster)
- **Military research maxed** to the same standard as economic research
- **Prerequisite research to level 10:** Combat Tactics, Defensive Formation, Herbal Medicine
- **Gem/blueprint investment:** community sources cite roughly 32,000 gems and 16 Master's Blueprints across the full path

## Timeline
There is no fixed timeline — it depends heavily on speedup stockpiles, alliance support, and spending level. Treat T5 as a mid-to-late-game milestone reached through sustained building/research investment rather than a rushed early-game goal.

## Resource Planning
- Stockpile Master's Blueprints specifically, since they're a hard gate on the unlock (16 required per community sources) and aren't easily substituted with other resources.
- Plan building-upgrade order so that all building types reach level 25 roughly together, rather than rushing City Hall/Academy while other buildings lag — T5 requires the *entire* building roster at level 25, not just the headline buildings.

## Research Priorities
Follow the [[guide-military-research]] specialization approach (universal nodes → primary troop-type branch) while working toward the T5 prerequisite nodes (Combat Tactics, Defensive Formation, Herbal Medicine to level 10) specifically, since these gate the unlock regardless of your other research progress.

## Mistakes to Avoid
- Rushing City Hall to 25 while neglecting other buildings — T5 requires every building type at level 25, so an unbalanced building profile delays the unlock even with a maxed City Hall.
- Spending speedups reactively instead of stockpiling toward the specific T5 research/building thresholds.
- Underestimating the gem cost — 32,000 gems is a significant sum that benefits from being planned for in advance via Wheel of Fortune discipline, VIP progress, and event participation rather than scrambled together late.
