# Economic Research Guide

## Overview
Economic research compounds into every other progression system — faster gathering and production means faster access to the resources that fund military research, building upgrades, and troop training.

## Gathering Technologies
Complete gathering-speed research for all four resource types (food, wood, stone, gold) — unlike military research, there's little reason to specialize here since your account will gather all four resource types regardless of combat focus.

## Resource Production
Passive production research (food, wood, stone — gold has no passive production and relies entirely on gathering/events) should be completed early, since it provides compounding value for the rest of the account's life.

## Building Efficiency
Construction-speed and building-cost-reduction research pays for itself quickly given how many building upgrades a growing account performs — prioritize this alongside gathering research rather than deferring it in favor of pure military nodes.

## Recommended Usage
Complete the entire economic tree — community consensus is unambiguous that skipping economic nodes to rush military research is a false economy, since economic research accelerates the resource base that funds military research in the first place.
