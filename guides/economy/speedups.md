# Speedup Management

## Overview
Speedups (general and type-specific: construction, research, training, healing) accelerate the game's core timers. Efficient use means matching general speedups to whichever queue currently has the longest remaining timer, and saving type-specific speedups for that type's biggest single jobs.

## Recommended Usage
- Use **type-specific speedups first** on their matching queue (they can't be used elsewhere, so there's no opportunity cost).
- Save **general speedups** for whichever queue (construction/research/training) currently has the most time remaining, to maximize elapsed-time saved per speedup spent.
- Time major troop-training or research pushes to align with events offering speed bonuses, so speedups stretch further.
- Don't let free speedups from daily quests/events expire unused if you're near an inventory cap — spend down to make room before caps overflow.

## Strategy Tips
- Before a KvK phase transition or major event, front-load queues so speedups convert directly into readiness rather than being wasted mid-queue.
