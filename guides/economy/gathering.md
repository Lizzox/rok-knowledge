# Efficient Resource Gathering

## Overview
Gathering marches sent to resource tiles are one of the main resource sources beyond passive production, especially for gold, which has no passive-production building.

## Recommended Usage
- Send marches to the highest-tier resource tile your troops can efficiently gather (higher-tier tiles yield more per trip but may be farther/contested).
- Use commanders/talents/equipment with gathering-speed or load-capacity bonuses on dedicated gathering marches rather than combat commanders.
- Stagger march return times so you're not manually re-dispatching marches constantly — queue enough marches to cover your active play windows.

## Strategy Tips
- Gold tiles are typically the scarcest and most contested — prioritize gold gathering during low-contention hours or in secured territory.
- Watch alliance territory/scout reports before sending gathering marches into contested or enemy-adjacent zones; gathering marches are easy plunder targets.
- Use resource-boost items/events (double gathering speed events, etc.) to concentrate gathering activity into short high-efficiency windows rather than spreading it evenly.
