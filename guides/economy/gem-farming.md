# Gem Farming Strategies

## Overview
Gems are the premium currency, obtainable free through quests, events, exploring the map (chests), and alliance activities, or purchased. F2P players rely on consistent daily/weekly free sources rather than one-time windfalls.

## Recommended Usage
- **Daily/weekly quests and VIP points** are the most reliable recurring free gem source.
- **Exploring fog-of-war tiles and chests** on the map yields gems, especially early in a server's life or after a new Lost Kingdom migration.
- **Alliance/kingdom events** (e.g., Ark of Osiris, Kingdom vs Kingdom events) often distribute gems as participation/ranking rewards.
- Prioritize gem spending on **VIP levels early**, then on **commander sculptures/shards** for your core build, saving large lump sums for known high-value shop refreshes (e.g., when a commander you need appears in a limited shop).

## Strategy Tips
- Avoid impulsively spending gems on random shop refreshes — track which commanders/items you actually need before refreshing.
- New Lost Kingdom migrations tend to have fresh, easier map exploration rewards — a good time to focus on gem-farming via exploration if you migrate.

## Note on Completeness
Exact gem amounts per source change with updates/events — treat quantities as directional, not exact.
