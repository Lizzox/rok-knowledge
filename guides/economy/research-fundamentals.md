# Research Fundamentals

## Overview
Research at the Academy is organized into branches (Economic, Military, and — during Season of Conquest — Crystal Technology) and gates nearly every other progression system: troop tiers, building levels, and combat stat bonuses all depend on completed research.

## Research Priority
- **Universal nodes first:** stat bonuses that apply regardless of troop type or specialization (e.g., Troop Attack, Troop Defense, Troop Health at +25% each when maxed) are the highest-return completions in the tree, since they benefit every march you ever send.
- **Primary troop-type branch before secondary:** once universal nodes are done, max your account's primary troop-type branch (Cavalry Attack/Defense for cavalry-primary accounts, Archer for archer-primary, Infantry for garrison/rally-lead accounts) before splitting into secondary troop types. Spreading research evenly across all three troop types wastes completions on branches you don't actively use.
- **Economic research:** complete every technology in the economic tree — unlike military research, there's little reason to skip economic nodes since they compound into faster overall progression across every other system.

## Academy Progression
Academy level gates which research tiers are unlockable — keep Academy level paced with (or slightly ahead of) your City Hall level so research never becomes the progression bottleneck.

## Technology Planning
Plan research queues around upcoming needs (e.g., queuing expensive research before a Mightiest Governor Technology phase, or before a KvK combat window) rather than researching reactively.

## Avoiding Wasted Resources
- Do not spend universal speedups on building, healing, or training troops if research is behind — prioritize universal speedups on research specifically, since research time is often the single largest bottleneck to reaching T4/T5 troops.
- Avoid research resets/redirects mid-tree; RoK's research tree generally doesn't reward switching specializations partway through, unlike the free talent-reset system for commanders.

## Optimization Tips
- Civilization research-speed bonuses (e.g., Korea), alliance research-technology bonuses, kingdom title research-speed boosts, and rune-based research-time reduction all stack multiplicatively — combine as many of these bonuses as possible before committing speedups to expensive research nodes.
- Joining an alliance with maxed research technology and altar control meaningfully accelerates every member's research — prioritize alliance quality over solo optimization once you've capped your own low-hanging bonuses.
