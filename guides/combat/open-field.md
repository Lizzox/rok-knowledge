# Open Field Combat Fundamentals

## Overview
Open-field combat happens directly on the map outside garrisons/rallies — duels, ambushes, and roaming skirmishes. Winning consistently requires good scouting, favorable troop-type matchups, and correct commander/skill setup for the expected opponent.

## Recommended Usage
- **Scout before engaging** — check the enemy march's troop composition and rough power to judge the matchup (Cavalry beats Archer, Archer beats Infantry, Infantry beats Cavalry, as a baseline).
- **Match your active Formation** to "March/Attack" before initiating combat (see [[mechanic-formations]]).
- Use commanders whose active skills synergize with your troop type and expected opponent composition (e.g., AoE skill commanders against grouped/weaker marches, single-target burst against a lone strong march).

## Strategy Tips
- Roaming with a Cavalry-heavy march is generally the most flexible open-field approach due to speed advantages for both engaging and disengaging.
- Avoid open-field engagements against unscoutable or ambiguous enemy comps unless your march can afford to lose — retreat and regroup is often correct.
