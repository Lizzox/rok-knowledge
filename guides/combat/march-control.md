# March Control and Field Coordination

## Overview
"March control" refers to alliance-level coordination of where and when marches move — timing rallies, reinforcing threatened forts/cities, and avoiding overcommitting marches to a losing engagement.

## Recommended Usage
- Rally timers should be set so multiple joining marches arrive together rather than trickling in piecemeal and being picked off.
- Reinforce key strategic targets (forts, passes, allied cities under threat) proactively rather than reactively when possible.
- Track enemy alliance's typical rally patterns/timing to anticipate and counter-rally or reinforce in time.

## Strategy Tips
- Communication tools (voice chat, in-game alliance chat) are as important as any commander build for effective march control — coordinate call-outs for incoming threats.
- Avoid "trickle feeding" marches into an active enemy rally/kill zone; consolidate before committing.

## Note on Completeness
Specific coordination tactics vary heavily by kingdom/alliance culture and current KvK ruleset — treat this as general principle, not a rigid script.
