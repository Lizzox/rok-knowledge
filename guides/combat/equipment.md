# Equipping for Combat

## Overview
See [[mechanic-equipment]] for the base system. This guide covers *priority order* when gearing a combat commander.

## Recommended Usage
1. **Weapon and Chestplate** first — these typically carry the largest attack/defense stat weight.
2. **Set-bonus completion** next — a completed 2-piece or 4-piece set bonus often outperforms randomly upgrading unrelated slots.
3. **Role-matched special effects** — rally leaders want rage/skill-damage effects; garrison defenders want damage-reduction/health effects.
4. Refine and Charm gear incrementally rather than rushing one piece to max while others sit at base level — balanced refinement usually yields a higher combined stat total early on.

## Strategy Tips
- Don't split investment across too many different sets; concentrate on one set per commander role to unlock bonus effects sooner.
