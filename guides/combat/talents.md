# Talent Builds for Combat Roles

## Overview
See [[mechanic-talents]] for the base system. This guide covers how to pick a build by role.

## Recommended Usage
- **Rally leader (primary):** prioritize rage-generation and skill-damage nodes plus a survivability keystone — you need to both trigger skills often and survive to keep triggering them.
- **Rally joiner (secondary):** prioritize pure attack/skill-damage keystones since survivability is less critical when not leading.
- **Garrison defender:** prioritize defense, health, and damage-reduction nodes; rage generation matters less since defensive skills often trigger reliably regardless.
- **Farming/gathering commander:** prioritize load-capacity/gathering-speed nodes on commanders that have them, ignoring combat nodes entirely.

## Strategy Tips
- Because talent resets are free, keep at least two saved mental "profiles" (e.g., rally build vs. garrison build) for your main commanders and re-spec based on your immediate task rather than settling on one permanent build.
