# Midgame KvK Preparation

## Overview
A kingdom's first KvK season typically arrives during the midgame window for most accounts — preparation should begin well before the season's matching/conflict phase actually opens.

## Recommended Usage
- Confirm your account's combat role (rally-leading, garrison defense, open-field support, or economy/gathering support) with alliance leadership in advance, and direct commander/equipment/research investment accordingly.
- Push troop tier and military research (see [[guide-military-research]] and [[guide-research-during-kvk]]) as far as possible before the season's conflict phase opens — combat stat research applies immediately and compounds with commander/equipment investment.
- Assess alliance strength and fit honestly during this window — per [[guide-first-90-days]], this is the last practical point to migrate to a stronger alliance before KvK stakes make migration costlier.

## Strategy Tips
- Coordinate equipment/commander investment with your assigned combat role rather than optimizing generically — a garrison-defense-assigned account should prioritize defense gear over rally-leading skill-damage gear, for example.
- Stockpile speedups in the weeks before the conflict phase opens rather than spending them reactively, so you can burn them efficiently once the season's combat window begins.

## Mistakes to Avoid
- Waiting until the conflict phase opens to figure out your combat role — by then it's too late to redirect weeks of prior investment.
- Neglecting alliance-fit assessment until KvK stakes are already high.
