# Alliance Participation Guide

## Overview
Passive alliance membership (accepting help-speedups, occasionally joining rallies) is sufficient for early-game growth, but the midgame is when active, visible contribution starts to matter — both for personal benefit and for building the trust that shapes your role in your kingdom's first KvK.

## Recommended Usage
- Take on informal responsibilities: organizing barbarian-fort rallies, coordinating gathering-territory assignments, or helping newer members with questions.
- Participate consistently in alliance-scored events (Ceroli Crisis, Silk Road, Alliance Mobilization) rather than only the events that directly benefit you personally.
- Communicate proactively with alliance leadership about your account's strengths (combat focus, gathering focus, research focus) so you're assigned a role that matches your actual build once KvK planning begins.

## Strategy Tips
- Visible, consistent contribution during the midgame is what typically earns officer roles or priority consideration for high-value rally/garrison assignments during KvK — this isn't purely altruistic, it's a practical investment in your own KvK positioning.
- If your alliance is inactive or poorly organized, the midgame is the right time to consider migrating (see [[guide-first-90-days]]) before KvK stakes raise the cost of doing so.

## Mistakes to Avoid
- Remaining a purely passive member through the midgame and then expecting a strong KvK role without having built any track record or leadership trust.
- Contributing to alliance events only when personally rewarding, rather than when the alliance as a whole benefits.
