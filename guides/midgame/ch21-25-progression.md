# City Hall 21-25 Progression

## Overview
City Hall levels 21-25 constitute the "Feudal Age" stretch — a critical midgame phase that gates access to Tier 5 troops and requires a wide spread of supporting buildings to reach specific levels, not just City Hall itself.

## Recommended Usage
- **City Hall 21:** unlocks a meaningful jump in troop tier and dispatch queue capacity — treat 21 as a key milestone worth prioritizing speedups toward.
- **Final stretch to City Hall 25** requires elevating: Hospital to level 21, Farm/Lumber Mill/Quarry to level 23/23/24, and Siege Workshop to level 23 — plan speedup allocation across these supporting buildings, not just City Hall itself.
- **City Hall 25** requires a blueprint obtainable via 2,000 gems, and is the key gate for Tier 5 troop access (alongside the broader T5 requirements covered in [[guide-t5-progression]]).

## Strategy Tips
- Use universal speedups on City Hall specifically rather than other buildings — City Hall progression unlocks the *ability* to upgrade everything else, so it has outsized leverage.
- Focus on milestone levels (16, 21, 25) as speedup-priority checkpoints rather than treating every level as equally urgent — these specific levels unlock disproportionate value (mid-game troops, more dispatch queues, top-tier research access).

## Mistakes to Avoid
- Rushing City Hall alone while ignoring the supporting buildings (Hospital, resource buildings, Siege Workshop) needed for the final stretch to 25 — an unbalanced building profile stalls progress right before the finish line.
- Underestimating the 2,000-gem blueprint cost for City Hall 25 — budget for it in advance rather than being caught short.
