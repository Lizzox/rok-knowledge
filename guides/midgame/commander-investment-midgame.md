# Midgame Commander Investment

## Overview
By the midgame, most accounts have moved past the single-primary-commander phase and need a framework for prioritizing a growing roster — typically a primary, a strong secondary/pairing partner, and situational commanders for garrison/gathering/PvE roles.

## Recommended Usage
- **Primary commander:** continue prioritizing universal sculptures here until fully maxed (5-star, Expertise if available) before diverting investment elsewhere.
- **Pairing partner:** once your primary is well-developed, invest in whichever commander forms your best pairing (see `pairings/` for scenario-specific recommendations) — a strong pairing partner often provides more combat value per sculpture than further over-investing in an already-strong primary.
- **Role-specific commanders:** budget separate, smaller investment for garrison defense and gathering commanders — these don't need to compete with your primary/secondary combat investment since they serve entirely different roles.

## Strategy Tips
- Don't let "shiny new commander" releases distract from your established primary/secondary investment unless the new commander is a clear, verified upgrade for your specific build — see each commander's `detail_status` and community tier-list consensus before redirecting resources.
- Keep secondary commanders you're not actively pairing at a modest level (community sources suggest ~30) since their talent trees don't apply when used as secondary.

## Mistakes to Avoid
- Spreading sculptures across many commanders "just in case" instead of committing to a primary/secondary pairing.
- Over-investing in a commander whose role your account doesn't actually need (e.g., a rally-leading commander on an account that never leads rallies).
