# T4 Unlocking Guide

## Overview
Tier 4 (T4) troops represent the mid-to-late midgame troop tier, gated behind City Hall level and matching military research progress, serving as the direct stepping stone to Tier 5.

## Recommended Usage
- Push City Hall toward the milestone level that unlocks T4 access (per [[guide-ch21-25-progression]], milestone levels like 21 unlock significant troop-tier jumps) while keeping military research paced closely behind — a high City Hall level with lagging research doesn't actually grant T4 combat strength.
- Prioritize upgrading your account's primary troop-type research branch to T4-equivalent completion before diversifying into secondary troop types, consistent with the specialization principle in [[guide-military-research]].
- Begin disbanding/upgrading excess T3 troops into T4 once the tier unlocks, rather than maintaining a large T3 army indefinitely — T4 troops significantly outperform T3 per-population.

## Strategy Tips
- Treat T4 as a checkpoint to reassess your commander/equipment build, since higher troop tiers change the practical damage/survivability math that talent and equipment choices are based on.

## Mistakes to Avoid
- Unlocking T4 troop training without matching research completion, resulting in T4 troops that underperform their potential.
- Continuing to train large batches of T3 troops right before a T4 unlock — time training around the unlock instead.
