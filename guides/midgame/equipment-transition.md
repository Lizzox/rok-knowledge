# Equipment Transition Guide

## Overview
Early-game equipment is often acquired opportunistically (whatever blueprints/materials happen to drop); the midgame is when it's worth switching to a deliberate plan matching your confirmed primary commander and troop type.

## Recommended Usage
- Audit your current equipment against your now-confirmed primary commander's troop type — early-game pieces crafted before your build solidified are often mismatched (e.g., Archer pieces on an account that ended up Cavalry-primary).
- Follow the slot-priority order (weapon → helmet → chest → gloves → boots) from [[guide-midgame-equipment]] when re-committing materials to your confirmed troop type.
- Begin tracking blueprint/material income specifically toward your next planned piece rather than crafting reactively whenever materials happen to be available.

## Strategy Tips
- It's fine to "waste" some early mismatched equipment — don't let sunk-cost thinking delay committing to your confirmed build's correct troop-type gear.
- Coordinate with your alliance on blueprint-sharing norms, since midgame accounts often benefit from alliance-level blueprint gifting as they push toward their first full Purple set.

## Mistakes to Avoid
- Continuing to craft equipment for a troop type your build no longer uses, out of habit from the early game.
- Waiting for "the perfect piece" instead of completing a full lower-rarity set first, per the general equipment-guide principle that a complete Purple set outperforms scattered high-rarity pieces.
