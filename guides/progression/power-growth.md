# Power Growth Strategy

## Overview
Power is an aggregate score derived from troop count/tier, building levels, technology researched, and commander levels. It is a visibility metric (used for leaderboards and as a rough attack-target signal) more than a direct combat-strength number — two accounts with equal power can have very different actual combat strength.

## Recommended Usage
- **Balance "real strength" against "paper power."** Troops contribute heavily to visible power but also to real combat strength; buildings/tech contribute to power with less direct combat payoff per point, but are prerequisites for higher troop tiers.
- Avoid growing power faster than your actual defensive capability, especially in a kingdom still in an active KvK/conflict phase — high power with weak troops/talents makes you a visible, easy target.
- Time large troop-training pushes around events that grant training-speed or resource bonuses.

## Strategy Tips
- Many experienced governors intentionally slow power growth during vulnerable phases (e.g., right after a migration or at low troop tiers) to avoid being farmed by stronger kingdoms/alliances.
- Prioritize troop tier upgrades over raw troop count once a new tier unlocks — higher-tier troops usually outperform proportionally more lower-tier troops of the same total power.
