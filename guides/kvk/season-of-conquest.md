# Season of Conquest

## Overview
Season of Conquest is the umbrella endgame system organizing sequential KvK seasons, typically structured around honor/preparation phases, a main conflict phase with capturable objectives (passes, forts, resource points), and a climactic phase determining the season's winning kingdom(s)/alliance(s).

## Recommended Usage
- Track your kingdom's current phase and adjust posture accordingly: build economy and rosters during honor/prep phases, shift fully to military production and coordination once the conflict phase opens.
- Prioritize objectives that grant compounding advantages (buffs, strategic map control) over cosmetic point-scoring targets when both are contested simultaneously.

## Note on Completeness
Exact phase names, durations, and objective mechanics are periodically revised between seasons; **verify the current season's specific rules via in-game announcements** rather than relying solely on this document.
