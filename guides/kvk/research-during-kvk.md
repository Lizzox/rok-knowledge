# Research During KvK

## Overview
Research priorities shift across the three phases of a KvK cycle — preparation, active conflict, and post-season recovery — and mistiming these shifts wastes speedups and leaves an account under-optimized when it matters most.

## Before KvK
- Finish or push as far as possible into your primary troop-type combat research branch before the season's matching/conflict phase begins — combat stat bonuses from research apply immediately and compound with commander talents and equipment.
- Stockpile speedups rather than spending them on lower-priority research, so you can burn them efficiently once high-value combat nodes are queued.

## During KvK
- Shift any remaining speedup stockpile toward whatever research most directly increases current combat effectiveness (troop attack/defense/health nodes, primary troop-type nodes) rather than economic research, which matters less during active conflict.
- If your kingdom is in a Season of Conquest-style KvK, prioritize Crystal Technology nodes relevant to your immediate combat needs (see the Crystal Tech guides) over base-game research during this window.

## After KvK
- Return focus to economic research and building-efficiency nodes once active conflict winds down, rebuilding the resource base that active KvK combat typically depletes.
- Use the post-season lull to fill in any base-game research gaps that were deprioritized during the conflict phase.
