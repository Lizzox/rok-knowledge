# KvK Season 2 Overview

## Overview
KvK 2 generally follows a kingdom's continued matchmaking progression after KvK 1, typically against comparably-strengthened kingdoms, with higher average power/troop tiers across the board. Detailed current-season mechanics are not verified for this repository — treat as a stub pending research against current official rules.
