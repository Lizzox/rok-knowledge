# KvK Season 1 Overview

## Overview
"KvK" (Kingdom vs Kingdom) content pits multiple kingdoms against each other for map control, culminating in objectives tied to the current Season of Conquest ruleset. KvK 1 generally refers to a kingdom's first matched season after a home-kingdom "honor" period.

## Recommended Usage
- Build combat-focused commander rosters and troop tiers ahead of the KvK matching date — economic focus should shift toward military production as the season approaches.
- Establish clear alliance leadership roles for march control, target calling, and diplomacy before the season begins, not after.
- Scout the matched kingdom(s) via early skirmishes to gauge relative strength before committing to major objectives.

## Note on Completeness
Specific Season of Conquest rules, phases, and objectives (e.g., city-holding points, boss fights, sub-phases) are updated periodically and can differ between server generations; **verify the current season ruleset in-game or via your kingdom's official announcements** before planning around specific mechanics.
