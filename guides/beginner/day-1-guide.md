# Day 1 Guide

## Overview
Your first session sets the foundation for everything that follows — civilization choice and initial commander/building decisions compound over the account's entire lifetime, so it's worth a few extra minutes of care even on Day 1.

## Civilization Choice
Community guides frequently cite **China** as the strongest beginner pick: it grants +5% building speed and comes with **Sun Tzu** as a free starting commander, widely regarded as the strongest Epic commander in the game. That said, per [[guide-civilization-choice]], the permanent bonus difference between civilizations is a minor long-term factor — don't agonize over this choice if China isn't appealing thematically.

## Recommended Usage
1. **Complete the full tutorial** — it grants substantial free resources and guides you through core UI/mechanics.
2. **Set your civilization** deliberately rather than randomly, per the guidance above.
3. **Start the City Hall upgrade queue immediately** and keep it running continuously from this point forward.
4. **Join an alliance today, not later** — even a small first-server alliance provides shields, help-speedups, and barbarian-fort access (needed later for Books of Covenants, a City Hall 25 requirement).
5. Do not train large amounts of low-tier troops on Day 1 — you'll disband/upgrade them soon, wasting resources.

## Mistakes to Avoid
- Skipping or rushing the tutorial to "get to the real game" — the resources and guidance it provides are worth the time investment.
- Delaying alliance-joining to "look around first" — every day without alliance help-speedups is lost progression.
