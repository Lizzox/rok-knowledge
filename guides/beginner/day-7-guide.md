# Day 7 Guide

## Overview
By the end of your first week, your account should have a clear primary commander direction, an active alliance relationship, and steady City Hall progression — Day 7 is a good checkpoint to verify you haven't drifted from the fast-progression path.

## Recommended Usage
- **Commander focus:** identify your account's primary commander (often your starting free commander, e.g., Sun Tzu if you chose China) and concentrate universal sculptures on that one commander rather than spreading them thin. Keep secondary commanders around level 30 — their talent trees don't function when used as secondary, so higher levels are wasted investment early on.
- **Alliance engagement:** you should be actively receiving alliance help-speedups and participating in any alliance-organized barbarian-fort rallies by this point, since forts are the source of Books of Covenants (a City Hall 25 requirement).
- **VIP progress:** continue spending gems on VIP level rather than commander sculptures during this window — VIP compounds account-wide.
- **Resource discipline:** avoid stockpiling resources above your Warehouse's protected cap; spend down into construction/research/training.

## Benchmarks
- City Hall should be progressing steadily with no multi-day stalls.
- You should have joined and be actively participating in an alliance (not just nominally a member).
- You should have a clear idea of which single commander you're building around.

## Mistakes to Avoid
- Splitting sculpture investment across multiple commanders instead of committing to one primary.
- Leveling secondary commanders past ~30 this early — their talents don't apply as secondary, so it's wasted resource allocation.
