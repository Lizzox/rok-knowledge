# Your First Days in Rise of Kingdoms

## Overview
The first week determines how efficiently you snowball through the early game. Priorities: rush City Hall to a reasonable level, don't overbuild troops you'll disband, and start collecting VIP points immediately.

## Statistics
No hard numeric benchmarks here — priorities depend on your kingdom's age, but the *order of operations* below is stable across servers.

## Recommended Usage
1. **Complete the tutorial fully** — it grants substantial early resources and a free commander.
2. **Rush City Hall upgrades** using the in-game "recommended construction" queue; don't build economy buildings past what City Hall level requires yet.
3. **Join an active alliance immediately** for shields, gifts, and help-speedups on construction/research.
4. **Spend gems on VIP** in the first days rather than on commander sculptures — VIP unlocks compounding account-wide bonuses.
5. **Don't train large amounts of low-tier troops** — they'll need to be disbanded/upgraded later, wasting resources.
6. **Use free-tier commanders** (whatever you're given) until you can commit to a real Epic/Legendary investment.

## Strategy Tips
- Keep a Peace Shield active while City Hall is low-level; only drop it once you have real troops and can request alliance help.
- Prioritize research paths that unlock economic bonuses first (gathering, construction speed) before military tech.
