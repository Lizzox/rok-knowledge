# Choosing Your Civilization

## Overview
At account creation, you pick one of 15 civilizations (China, Rome, Britain, Germany, Arabia, Japan, Korea, Spain, Ottoman, Byzantium, Vikings, France, Egypt, Maya, Greece — see `civilizations/` for a dedicated file per civilization with sourced bonus values). This grants small permanent starting bonuses (economy, construction, research, or troop-type bonuses depending on civilization) but does **not** lock you out of using commanders or troops from other civilizations later.

## Recommended Usage
- New players should not overthink this choice — the permanent civilization bonus is a minor long-term modifier, not a build-defining decision. That said, **China** is the most commonly recommended default for brand-new players: +5% building speed, +5% action point recovery, and Sun Tzu (the strongest Epic commander) as a free starting commander.
- If choosing based on strategy rather than defaulting to China, match the civilization to your intended role:
  - **Rally leaders:** Arabia (+5% rallied army damage) is the top pick for offensive KvK rallying.
  - **Garrison/structure defense:** Vikings or Rome.
  - **Archer-primary builds:** Britain, Ottoman (rally-focused), Korea, or Maya, depending on secondary priorities.
  - **Cavalry-primary builds:** Germany or Byzantium.
  - **Open-field/long-war sustain:** France, thanks to its large hospital healing-speed bonus.
  - **Gathering/economy focus:** Spain (+20% resource production) or Maya (+10% gold gathering).
- See the individual `civilizations/{name}.md` files for exact bonus text and role fit per civilization.

## Strategy Tips
- Prioritize joining a strong, active kingdom/alliance over optimizing civilization choice — social factors matter far more to long-term success than the starting civilization bonus.
- Civilization choice is effectively permanent per account, so if you already know your intended long-term role (rally leader, garrison defender, gatherer), it's worth a few minutes to pick accordingly rather than defaulting blindly to China.
