# Choosing Your Civilization

## Overview
At account creation, you pick a civilization (e.g., China, Rome, Britain, Germany, Arabia, Japan, Korea, Spain, Ottoman, Byzantium, Vikings, France, and others added over time). This grants small permanent starting bonuses (economy, construction, research, or troop-type bonuses depending on civilization) but does **not** lock you out of using commanders or troops from other civilizations later.

## Recommended Usage
- New players should not overthink this choice — the permanent civilization bonus is a minor long-term modifier, not a build-defining decision.
- If choosing based on strategy, civilizations generally lean toward either an economic bonus (good for growth-focused/farming accounts) or a military bonus (good for combat-focused accounts); check the specific bonus text in-game since it is periodically rebalanced.

## Strategy Tips
- Prioritize joining a strong, active kingdom/alliance over optimizing civilization choice — social factors matter far more to long-term success than the starting civilization bonus.

## Note on Completeness
Exact current bonus values per civilization should be checked in-game, as they are occasionally adjusted and new civilizations have been added over time.
