# First 90 Days Roadmap

## Overview
Community consensus is direct: the first 60-90 days of a kingdom's life determine territory control, competitive hierarchy, and resource access for every season that follows. This is the window where kingdoms establish their relative strength ranking against other kingdoms and internally among alliances.

## Roadmap by Phase

### Days 1-7 — Foundation
See [[guide-day-1]] and [[guide-day-7]]: civilization choice, tutorial completion, alliance join, primary commander identification.

### Days 8-30 — Compounding
See [[guide-day-30]]: City Hall milestone progression (16, 21, 25), alliance embedding, consistent event participation, committed commander investment.

### Days 31-60 — Consolidation
- Push toward City Hall 25 and T4 troop unlocks if not already reached.
- Deepen alliance participation — take on informal responsibilities (barbarian-fort rally organization, resource sharing) that build trust ahead of KvK.
- Begin equipment investment in earnest now that a stable commander build is in place (see [[guide-beginner-equipment]] and [[guide-midgame-equipment]]).

### Days 61-90 — Competitive Positioning
- Kingdom-wide territory control (forts, passes, resource points) is typically being contested by this point — active participation here shapes your kingdom's standing heading into its first KvK matches.
- Assess whether your alliance is positioned competitively for the kingdom's first KvK season; this is the last practical window to migrate to a stronger alliance before KvK stakes make migration costlier.

## Mistakes to Avoid
- Treating the first 90 days as "just leveling up" rather than recognizing it as the window that sets competitive trajectory — passive play here is expensive to correct later.
- Waiting until KvK actually begins to assess alliance strength/fit — by then, migration options are far more limited.
