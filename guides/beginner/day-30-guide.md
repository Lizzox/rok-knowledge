# Day 30 Guide

## Overview
Community guides consistently describe the first 30 days as the **highest-return investment window of the entire game** — civilization, commander priorities, event participation, and alliance choice during this window set a compounding trajectory that's difficult to fully correct later.

## Recommended Usage
- **City Hall:** should be well into progression toward the milestone levels (16, 21, 25) that unlock mid-game troops, more dispatch queues, and top-tier research. Use universal speedups on City Hall specifically over other buildings.
- **Alliance:** by day 30, you should be embedded in an active, powerful alliance — if your current alliance is inactive or small, this is the point to consider migrating to a stronger one before your account grows large enough that migration becomes costlier.
- **Commander roster:** your primary commander should be meaningfully invested (skills, talents) with a clear secondary pairing identified, per the pairing principles in `pairings/`.
- **Events:** you should have a consistent daily/weekly participation habit for Lucerne Scrolls, Sunset Canyon, Wheel of Fortune (spending discipline), and any recurring alliance events (Ceroli Crisis, Silk Road) available in your kingdom's current age.

## Benchmarks
- Steady, uninterrupted City Hall progression with no long stalls.
- An active alliance relationship with regular help-speedup exchange.
- A committed primary commander build rather than scattered investment.

## Mistakes to Avoid
- Staying in an inactive or weak alliance out of inertia — the cost of migrating grows over time, so decide early.
- Continuing to spread sculpture/resource investment across multiple commanders instead of committing.
- Neglecting recurring events — missed daily/weekly participation in month one is difficult to fully recover.
