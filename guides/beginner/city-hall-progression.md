# City Hall Progression Priorities

## Overview
City Hall level gates almost everything else in the game (building levels, troop tiers, research tiers), so efficient progression means rushing City Hall while building only the minimum supporting infrastructure it requires.

## Recommended Usage
- Use the in-game "Construction Advisor"/recommended-queue feature as a baseline, but understand *why* it suggests what it does: it sequences buildings to satisfy City Hall's prerequisite requirements with minimal waste.
- Don't max out resource buildings (farms, etc.) early — build just enough to sustain the queue, then return to them once City Hall growth slows.
- Use speedups (free ones from quests/events first, then paid ones) on the construction queue, not on troop training, during the early rush phase.

## Strategy Tips
- Governors aiming for a competitive kingdom position typically rush City Hall to the "power spike" thresholds their kingdom age calls for (e.g., unlocking a new troop tier) rather than growing evenly across all buildings.
- Coordinate big City Hall pushes with alliance help requests — the speedup contribution from help clicks is one of the largest free time-savers in the game.
