# Infantry Equipment Optimization

## Overview
Infantry equipment optimization depends heavily on whether the march is defending (garrison) or actively brawling (open field) — the two roles call for different stat priorities despite using the same base gear pool.

## Recommended Usage
- **Garrison defense:** prioritize health and defense stat lines (Eternal Knight-style pieces); damage is secondary to durability since garrison Infantry wins by attrition.
- **Open-field tanking:** balance health/defense with enough attack to actually threaten the enemy — pure defense stacking without damage lets fights stall indefinitely without a decisive outcome.
- **Rally-leading Infantry:** prioritize the weapon slot (Hammer of the Sun and Moon-tier) for damage output, since a rally leader's Infantry march is expected to actually kill the target, not just survive.

## Strategy Tips
- Match Special Talent (if present) to the Infantry talent tree specifically — the 30% attribute bonus only applies with a matching commander talent tree.
- For F2P/low-spend accounts, a fully refined Purple Infantry set reliably outperforms a partial, unrefined Legendary set — refine before you upgrade rarity tier.
