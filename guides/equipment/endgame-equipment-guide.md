# Endgame Equipment Guide

## Overview
At the endgame, equipment strategy shifts from "get to Legendary" toward refinement, Special Talent optimization, and matching gear precisely to your account's primary commander pairings.

## Legendary Equipment Priorities
- **Cavalry-primary accounts:** Pride of the Khan (weapon) + KvK Conqueror's Helm (Cavalry) + Hope Cloak (chest) + Navar's Control (gloves) + Ash of the Dawn (legs).
- **Infantry-primary accounts:** Hammer of the Sun and Moon (weapon) + Conqueror's Helm (Infantry) + Eternal Knight set (chest/health-defense) + Karuak's Humility (legs).
- **Archer-primary accounts:** Full 4-piece Legendary Archer set + a Special Talent weapon, prioritized over the Cavalry set specifically if Archer is your primary march type (e.g., running Yi Seong-Gye, Zhuge Liang, or Hermann Prime).

## Refinement Strategy
- Refinement raises a piece's base stats within its rarity tier — refine your weapon and helmet first since they carry the most impactful base stats.
- Refined Level 0 equipment can be refined up to a high cap (community sources cite up to 100) and — notably — refinement itself can also be used to **grant a Special Talent** to pieces that haven't procced one through forging, giving F2P/low-spend players a guaranteed (if slow) path to Special Talent gear.

## Special Talent Strategy
- Special Talents come from one of five talent categories (Infantry, Cavalry, Archer, Integration, Leadership) and proc randomly when forging.
- When equipment with a matching Special Talent is worn by a commander with that same talent tree, the item's attribute bonuses increase by **30%** — always match Special Talent category to your primary commander's specialty.
- If a forge doesn't produce the desired Special Talent, you can either re-forge (consuming more materials) or use the refinement system to guarantee one over time — refinement is the more resource-predictable (if slower) route for F2P/low-spend accounts.

## Meta Equipment Sets
See the per-troop-type entries in `equipment/{infantry,cavalry,archer}/` for the current named top-tier Legendary pieces cited by community high-spender guides. Most are tied to Season of Conquest KvK rewards or recurring PvE events (e.g., The Karuak Ceremony), so plan long-term acquisition around your kingdom's KvK schedule and your own event participation consistency rather than the cash shop alone.
