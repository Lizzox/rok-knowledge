# Cavalry Equipment Optimization

## Overview
Cavalry equipment should emphasize attack and rage-generation stat lines given Cavalry's role as the game's primary burst-damage troop type, with march speed as a secondary but meaningful consideration for open-field roaming.

## Recommended Usage
- **Open-field roaming:** prioritize the weapon slot (e.g., Pride of the Khan-tier) and rage-generation accessories (Horn of Fury) to maximize skill-cycling frequency during extended roams.
- **Rally leadership:** prioritize the full recommended Cavalry set (weapon, helmet, chest, gloves, legs) for set-bonus completion, since rally leaders benefit from sustained stat bonuses across a longer engagement.
- **Hit-and-run/secondary Cavalry (e.g., Belisarius Prime-style kits):** march-speed and rage-denial accessories matter more than raw attack, since the play pattern relies on mobility and disruption rather than prolonged brawling.

## Strategy Tips
- Ring of Doom (normal-attack proc) pairs well with normal-attack-focused Cavalry kits (e.g., Takeda Shingen); pure skill-damage Cavalry nukers benefit more from Horn of Fury and skill-damage accessories.
- Complete the full named endgame Cavalry set (see `equipment/cavalry/`) before diversifying into accessory-only upgrades — set bonuses generally outweigh a single strong standalone piece.
