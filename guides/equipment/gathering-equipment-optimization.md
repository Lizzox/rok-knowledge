# Gathering Equipment Optimization

## Overview
Gathering equipment is a comparatively low-risk investment: it uses Animal Bone, a material exclusive to gathering gear that doesn't compete with combat-equipment materials (Iron Ore, Leather, Ebony), and gathering gear caps below Legendary rarity — so there's no risk of "wasting" a scarce Legendary craft on the wrong slot.

## Recommended Usage
- Equip dedicated gathering-march commanders (Cleopatra VII, Seondeok, Casimir III, Pepin III, Ishida Mitsunari) with a full gathering-focused set: gathering-speed and load-capacity stat lines, plus **The Bard's Pendant** for the flat resource bonus.
- Prioritize whichever resource type your kingdom currently needs most (commonly gold in the late game, since gold lacks passive production) when choosing between gathering-speed talent/equipment allocations.

## F2P Value
Excellent — since materials don't cross over with combat gear, there's no opportunity cost to building a solid gathering set even on a combat-focused account, as long as you're actively sending gathering marches.

## Strategy Tips
- Don't neglect gathering equipment just because it "isn't PvP gear" — faster gathering compounds into faster combat-gear crafting overall, since gold and other resources feed every other progression system.
- Refresh gathering equipment periodically as troop tiers increase — higher-tier troops carry more load, and matching equipment should scale accordingly.
