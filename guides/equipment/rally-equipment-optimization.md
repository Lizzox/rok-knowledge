# Rally Equipment Optimization

## Overview
Rally-leader equipment must balance three needs simultaneously: enough damage to actually threaten the target, enough survivability to outlast the engagement, and enough rage generation to keep the active skill firing throughout a long fight.

## Recommended Usage
- **Weapon and helmet:** always prioritized first regardless of troop type, since they carry the largest stat contributions and activate set bonuses.
- **Accessories:** Horn of Fury (rage generation) and Ring of Doom (normal-attack proc) are the highest-return accessory picks for offensive rally marches per community guides.
- **Full named set completion:** matters more for rally leaders than for solo open-field roamers, since rallies are longer, sustained engagements where full set bonuses have time to matter.

## Strategy Tips
- Coordinate with alliance leadership on which commander/troop-type combination will lead major rallies before investing heavily — re-gearing after committing to the wrong primary wastes scarce Legendary materials.
- For KvK and Ark of Osiris specifically, prioritize survivability slightly more than in standard open-field rallies, since these modes often involve prolonged multi-wave engagements rather than a single decisive exchange.
