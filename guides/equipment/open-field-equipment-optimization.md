# Open Field Equipment Optimization

## Overview
Open-field marches need a different equipment balance than rally leaders or garrison defenders: enough burst damage to win quick skirmishes, enough survivability to avoid being deleted by a surprise counter-rally, and enough mobility to disengage or reposition.

## Recommended Usage
- Prioritize the weapon slot for damage, but avoid pure glass-cannon builds — open-field marches face unpredictable engagements and need a baseline of defense/health to survive a bad matchup.
- March-speed-boosting pieces/accessories matter more here than in rally or garrison contexts, since open-field marches rely on repositioning and disengaging from unfavorable fights.
- Rage-generation accessories (Horn of Fury) help skill-damage commanders win skirmishes faster before reinforcements arrive.

## Strategy Tips
- Scout before committing to a fight — no equipment build fully compensates for engaging a much stronger march blind.
- For hit-and-run specialist commanders (e.g., Belisarius Prime), prioritize mobility/rage-denial accessories over pure attack, since the play pattern is built around picking off weakened targets rather than sustained brawling.
