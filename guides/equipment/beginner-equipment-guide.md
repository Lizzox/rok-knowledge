# Beginner Equipment Guide

## Overview
Equipment is crafted at the Blacksmith using four material types (Iron Ore, Leather, Animal Bone, Ebony) plus blueprints, across five rarity tiers (Normal, Advanced, Elite, Epic, Legendary). New players should focus narrowly rather than spreading materials thin.

## First Equipment to Craft
- Craft **one full set matching your main march's troop type** (Infantry, Cavalry, or Archer) rather than splitting materials across multiple troop types.
- Prioritize the **weapon slot first** — it carries the largest single-stat contribution and is required to unlock 2-piece set bonuses.
- Follow with the **helmet**, which typically activates the set bonus, then chest, gloves, and boots last.

## What NOT to Craft
- Do not craft equipment for a troop type you aren't actively running — materials and blueprints don't cross over between troop types, so this is wasted investment.
- Do not craft gathering equipment (Animal Bone-based) at the expense of combat gear if you're still building your core fighting march — though note gathering and combat materials don't compete directly (gathering gear specifically uses Animal Bone), so a small early investment in gathering gear is reasonable if you actively send gathering marches.
- Avoid spreading a single rarity tier's materials across many pieces — finish upgrading fewer pieces to a higher tier rather than half-upgrading everything.

## Material Management
- The four materials (Iron Ore, Leather, Animal Bone, Ebony) are earned through gathering, events, alliance help, and VIP chests.
- Track which material your next planned piece needs and prioritize gathering that specific type rather than collecting generically.

## Blueprint Priorities
- Blueprints are obtained from Tavern chests, special events, VIP chests, Sunset Canyon, Ceroli Crisis, and Lost Kingdom activities.
- Crafting a piece requires accumulating a full set of identical blueprints (commonly cited as needing many copies per piece at higher rarities) — prioritize consistent, small blueprint acquisition over waiting for a single lucky spike.
