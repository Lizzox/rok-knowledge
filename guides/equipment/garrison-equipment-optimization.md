# Garrison Equipment Optimization

## Overview
Garrison defense equipment should prioritize damage mitigation and counterattack value over raw offense, since a garrison's win condition is usually outlasting the attacking rally rather than winning a fast trade.

## Recommended Usage
- **Accessories:** Scolas' Lucky Coin (shield proc) and Vengeance (counterattack damage boost) are the priority accessory picks for garrison-defense builds per community guides.
- **Armor slots (helmet/chest/legs):** prioritize defense and health stat lines; the weapon slot still matters but contributes less to a garrison's overall survival than it does for an offensive march.
- **Special Talent match:** pair Infantry-talent Special Talent gear with Infantry garrison tanks (Richard I, Charles Martel, Zenobia) for the 30% attribute bonus.

## Strategy Tips
- Don't neglect the weapon slot entirely — a garrison with zero offensive capability can stall indefinitely without ever actually repelling an attack; balance mitigation with enough damage to make attackers pay a real cost.
- Coordinate gear choices with your garrison-defense commander pairing (e.g., Zenobia + Yi Sun-Sin) so equipment amplifies the pairing's existing strengths rather than working against them.
