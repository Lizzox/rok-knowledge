# Midgame Equipment Guide

## Overview
By the midgame, most players have a functioning Purple-rarity set for their main troop type and face the decision of when to start investing in Legendary pieces versus fully maximizing Purple gear first.

## Purple vs. Legendary Equipment
- A **fully refined, fully starred Purple set** frequently outperforms a **partial, unrefined Legendary set** — don't chase Legendary rarity at the expense of finishing your current tier.
- Legendary equipment's main advantages are higher stat ceilings and eligibility for stronger Special Talent procs — both of which matter more once you're already running a complete, refined lower-tier set.

## When to Upgrade
- Upgrade to Legendary once your Purple set is fully refined AND you have a reliable material/blueprint pipeline for the next tier (from events, alliance help, or KvK rewards) — don't start a Legendary piece you can't realistically finish within a reasonable timeframe.
- Prioritize upgrading the **weapon slot to Legendary first**, since it has the largest stat impact and enables set-bonus scaling.

## Efficient Crafting
- Craft in slot-priority order (weapon → helmet → chest → gloves → boots) at each rarity tier rather than jumping tiers on a single slot while others lag behind.
- Use free/event-sourced blueprints for testing Special Talent procs on lower-cost tiers before committing scarce Legendary materials to forging attempts.
- Track your alliance's blueprint-sharing or gifting norms — many alliances coordinate blueprint donations for members actively upgrading.

## Strategy Tips
- Avoid "one giant unfinished Legendary weapon while everything else is still Green" — balanced tier progression across all slots outperforms rushing a single flashy piece.
