# Archer Equipment Optimization

## Overview
Archer equipment optimization centers almost entirely on maximizing active-skill damage, since top Archer commanders (Yi Seong-Gye, Zhuge Liang, Hermann Prime, Edward of Woodstock) derive most of their value from skill nukes rather than sustained normal-attack damage.

## Recommended Usage
- **Early game:** a partial Purple Archer set with the weapon slot prioritized is sufficient; don't over-invest before your commander roster (Legendary Archer primaries) is in place.
- **Endgame:** the full 4-piece Legendary Archer set plus a Special Talent weapon (Archer talent tree match, +30% attribute bonus) is the top-tier target, prioritized over the Cavalry set specifically when Archer is your primary march type.
- **Rally-leading Archers:** pair skill-damage gear with rage-generation accessories (Horn of Fury) to maximize how often the AoE/single-target nuke fires during a rally.

## Strategy Tips
- Because Archer marches are fragile against Cavalry counter-rushes, consider a small defense/health allocation on non-weapon slots rather than pure glass-cannon stacking, unless you have strong garrison/escort support.
- Match Special Talent to Archer specifically — a Cavalry or Infantry Special Talent proc provides no bonus to an Archer-specialty commander.
