# Richard I + Charles Martel

## Purpose
Maximize garrison durability against enemy rallies by stacking two of the strongest Infantry defensive kits together.

## Explanation
Both commanders bring damage-reduction, health, and counter-damage passives; running them together on a garrison Infantry march produces very high effective health and can grind down attacking rallies before your city falls, especially valuable in KvK garrison defense.

## Strengths
- Extremely high effective health and sustain.
- Strong against repeated rally attempts by the same enemy alliance.

## Weaknesses
- Low proactive damage output — this comp wins by attrition, not burst.
- Redundant investment if you don't yet have both Legendary commanders; consider Guan Yu or Bjorn Ironside (Epic) as a substitute for either slot.

## Alternatives
- Guan Yu can replace either commander for a similar defensive profile.
- Bjorn Ironside (Epic) as a budget substitute for either slot.
