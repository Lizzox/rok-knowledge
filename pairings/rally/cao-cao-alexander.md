# Cao Cao + Alexander the Great

## Purpose
Large alliance rally combo balancing sustained rage generation/healing (Cao Cao) with strong repeatable skill damage (Alexander).

## Explanation
Cao Cao as primary keeps the rallying force topped up and generating rage faster, letting Alexander's damage-oriented passives and active skill fire more consistently across a long rally fight. This "sustain primary + damage secondary" structure is the standard template for mega-rally comps.

## Strengths
- Excellent sustain lets the rally outlast defenders in prolonged fights.
- Scales well with rally size — the bigger the rally, the more valuable the healing/rage utility becomes.

## Weaknesses
- Less effective in short, decisive skirmishes where sustain matters less than raw burst.
- Requires good troop-type synergy management since Cao Cao's Leadership specialty doesn't lock you into one troop type.

## Alternatives
- Sun Tzu (Epic) can substitute for Cao Cao in budget rally comps.
- Attila can substitute for Alexander when facing grouped/AoE-vulnerable defenders.
