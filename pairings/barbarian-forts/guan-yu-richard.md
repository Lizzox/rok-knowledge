# Guan Yu + Richard I

## Purpose
Durable Infantry march composition for clearing Barbarian Forts, which feature sustained waves of enemies requiring high effective health rather than burst damage.

## Explanation
Barbarian Forts reward marches that can survive prolonged combat against successive enemy waves; pairing two tanky Infantry Legendary commanders maximizes survivability so the march can complete the fort without needing to retreat and heal repeatedly.

## Strengths
- High survivability across long PvE engagements.
- Reduces medical tent usage/healing costs versus lower-durability comps.

## Weaknesses
- Slower clear speed than a burst-damage comp; not optimal if racing for fastest clear time in a timed event.

## Alternatives
- Charles Martel can substitute for either commander.
- Bjorn Ironside (Epic) as a budget substitute.
