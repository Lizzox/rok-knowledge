# Alexander the Great + Yi Seong-Gye

## Purpose
Maximize single-target-plus-splash skill damage for Cavalry open-field roaming and duels.

## Explanation
Alexander's active skill provides the primary damage source; Yi Seong-Gye's passives amplify that skill damage and reduce incoming damage, making the pairing greater than the sum of its parts. This has been a stable top pairing for years because both commanders' kits are largely passive/amplification-based rather than relying on complex conditional triggers.

## Strengths
- Very high, reliable skill damage output.
- Simple to pilot — no complex conditional skill sequencing required.
- Long track record of meta relevance across many rebalances.

## Weaknesses
- Limited crowd-control or defensive utility compared to newer, more complex Legendary pairings.
- Both commanders are Cavalry/Skill specialists — no Infantry/Archer flexibility from this pairing alone.

## Alternatives
- Substitute Attila or Guan Yu as primary if targeting grouped enemies (AoE) rather than single targets.
- Substitute Baibars (Epic) + Sun Tzu (Epic) as a budget version of the same "damage + amplification" formula.
