# Gathering March Commander Selection

## Purpose
Maximize resource-gathering efficiency (load capacity and/or gathering speed) rather than combat power.

## Explanation
Dedicated gathering marches should prioritize commanders/talents/equipment that increase gathering speed and load capacity over combat stats, since gathering marches are not expected to fight. Many kingdoms/events introduce specific gathering-boost commanders on a rotating basis (e.g., seasonal or event-exclusive commanders); this file is intentionally generic because the "best" gathering commander pairing shifts frequently with new releases.

## Strengths
- Even modest gathering-speed/load bonuses compound significantly over long farming sessions.

## Weaknesses
- Gathering marches are vulnerable to being attacked/plundered while out — pair with alliance scouting/defense rather than combat commanders.

## Alternatives
- Check current in-game commander pool for any temporary or newly-released gathering specialist before committing resources to a specific gathering build.
