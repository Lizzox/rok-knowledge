# Aethelflaed + Zhuge Liang

## Purpose
High single-target/splash Archer skill damage for Canyon (Lost Kingdom-style) combat modes where burst damage and range matter more than raw tankiness.

## Explanation
Both commanders are Archer/Skill specialists with strong skill-damage output; combining them front-loads damage output for quick, decisive engagements typical of canyon combat, where fights are often shorter and more skirmish-like than full city rallies.

## Strengths
- High burst damage output well-suited to shorter skirmish-style fights.
- Effective against Infantry-heavy opposing comps common in canyon defense.

## Weaknesses
- Archer fragility remains a liability if caught by a well-timed Cavalry counter-rally.

## Alternatives
- Attila can substitute for AoE-heavy canyon fights against grouped enemies.
