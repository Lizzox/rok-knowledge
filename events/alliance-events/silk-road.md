# Silk Road Speculators

## Overview
Silk Road is a monthly alliance event requiring coordinated defense of an alliance caravan as it transports goods across the map, with strong rewards including reportedly up to 9 Legendary commander sculptures ("Golden Heads") for successful completion.

## How It Works
An alliance caravan spawns near your nearest alliance fortress and begins transporting goods to a destination. Alliance members must defend the caravan from enemy interference throughout its journey.

## Requirements
Active alliance coordination — members need to communicate and position defensive marches along the caravan's route rather than relying on a single defender.

## Rewards
Legendary commander sculptures (community sources cite up to 9 across a full successful run) plus resources.

## Best Strategy
- Assign specific defensive shifts/zones to alliance members along the caravan's route so coverage doesn't lapse during off-peak hours.
- Prioritize defending during known high-risk windows (e.g., when the caravan passes near enemy territory).

## F2P Strategy
Since this is a coordination-based alliance event rather than a spending-based one, F2P players can access full rewards purely through active participation and communication — a strong value event for non-spenders.

## Common Mistakes
- Assuming "someone else" will defend the caravan — coordination gaps are the most common cause of failed runs.
- Not communicating caravan location/status changes to the full alliance in real time.

## Optimization Tips
Use alliance chat/voice coordination tools to broadcast caravan status updates and rally defenders quickly when the caravan comes under attack.
