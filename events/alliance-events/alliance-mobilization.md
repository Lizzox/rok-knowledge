# Alliance Mobilization

## Overview
Alliance Mobilization is a recurring event where alliance members complete individual tasks (gathering, building, training, research, combat) that contribute to a shared alliance-wide reward pool, distributed with rewards scaled by individual contribution rank.

## How It Works
Complete listed tasks (varying by day/rotation) to earn contribution points; the alliance collectively unlocks reward tiers as total contribution accumulates, with top individual contributors receiving enhanced rewards.

## Requirements
Consistent daily task completion — even small, regular contributions compound significantly over the event's full duration.

## Rewards
Alliance-wide chest rewards (resources, speedups, sculptures) scaled by both total alliance contribution and individual contribution rank.

## Best Strategy
- Complete mobilization tasks daily rather than trying to catch up late — most task types are time-gated per day.
- Focus on task categories matching your account's strengths (economy players gather/build, combat players fight/train) rather than forcing tasks outside your natural playstyle.

## F2P Strategy
Since this is a participation-based (not spending-based) event, consistent daily task completion gives F2P players full access to top contribution tiers.

## Common Mistakes
Letting daily tasks lapse and trying to catch up near the event's end — many task types can't be "double completed" to make up for missed days.

## Optimization Tips
Check alliance leadership announcements for prioritized task focuses before self-directing your participation, since coordinated alliance-wide task focus often unlocks reward tiers faster than uncoordinated individual effort.
