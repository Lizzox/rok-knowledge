# Ceroli Crisis

## Overview
Ceroli Crisis is a small-team PvE event occurring 3-4 times per month, where you team up with 3 other players (via invite link, random matchmaking, or a mix of both) to defeat a barbarian horde together.

## How It Works
Form or join a 4-player team (yourself plus 3 others), then jointly fight through a barbarian horde encounter within the event window.

## Requirements
A formed team of 4 (self plus 3 others) — invite alliance mates directly for coordinated play, or use random matchmaking if you don't have enough available teammates.

## Rewards
Equipment blueprints (a notable source for general Blacksmith crafting materials/blueprints), resources, and sculptures.

## Best Strategy
Coordinate with a consistent group of alliance mates rather than relying on random matchmaking each time — familiar teammates communicate and synchronize attacks more efficiently.

## F2P Strategy
Since this is a team-participation event rather than a spending event, F2P players get full access to rewards through consistent participation — worth prioritizing every time it's available given its frequency (3-4 times monthly).

## Common Mistakes
Skipping the event due to not having a pre-formed team — random matchmaking is a fully viable fallback and shouldn't be a reason to miss out on blueprint rewards.

## Optimization Tips
Since it recurs 3-4 times a month, treat Ceroli Crisis as a reliable recurring blueprint source to plan equipment-crafting timelines around.
