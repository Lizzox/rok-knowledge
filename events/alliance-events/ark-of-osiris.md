# Ark of Osiris

## Overview
Ark of Osiris (AOO) is a scheduled alliance-versus-alliance battleground, recurring roughly every two weeks, where two randomly matched alliances each field 30 players in a 1-hour match. It is one of the most competitive, coordination-dependent modes in the game — even lower-power alliances can win through superior planning and execution.

## Rules
- Each alliance selects 30 participating players for the match.
- Players attack enemies and capture/hold map structures to accumulate points over the 1-hour match.
- Most maps use a three-lane layout: a middle lane containing the Ark itself, and top/bottom lanes containing additional capturable buildings.

## Roles
- **Ark carriers/escorts** — focus on picking up the Ark (spawns center-map) and delivering it to your alliance's base for a large point boost; requires an escort to protect the carrying march.
- **Obelisk controllers** — the Obelisk is the most important structure, enabling an alliance to teleport up to 8 cities quickly into the contested area; controlling it early is often decisive.
- **Lane fighters** — hold top/bottom lane buildings and intercept enemy pushes toward the Ark or Obelisk.

## Team Strategy
- Control of the middle lane (containing the Ark) frequently determines the match outcome — prioritize securing it early rather than spreading effort evenly across all three lanes.
- Assign clear roles before the match starts (who carries, who escorts, who holds which lane) — improvising roles mid-match wastes the event's tight 1-hour window.

## Building Strategy
Use pre-match buff items — Enhanced Attack, Enhanced Defense, Enhanced Gathering, Troop Expansion, and Alliance Titles — before the battle begins rather than mid-fight, since some buffs may not apply retroactively.

## Combat Strategy
- Popular commander skills for this mode include To The Death, Hurl Stone, Mystical Formation, War Drums, Swift Stride, and Running Charge — prioritize commanders/skills that support your assigned role (mobility for carriers, AoE/control for lane fighters).
- Coordinate rally timing with alliance leadership so pushes on enemy-held structures arrive together rather than trickling in piecemeal.

## Common Mistakes
- Fumbling march/skill selection after the event starts instead of preparing in advance.
- Neglecting the Obelisk in favor of fighting over the Ark alone — losing map-wide mobility often costs more than a single Ark delivery is worth.
- Uncoordinated pushes that arrive in small groups and get picked off individually.

## Optimization Tips
- Review your alliance's performance after each match (what structures were lost, where coordination broke down) and adjust role assignments for the next AOO run.
