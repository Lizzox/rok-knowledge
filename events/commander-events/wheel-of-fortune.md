# Wheel of Fortune

## Overview
Wheel of Fortune is a recurring gem-spending gacha event, running roughly every 2 weeks for 3 days, and is the **only** place in the game to obtain Wheel-exclusive Legendary commander sculptures and their specific (non-universal) sculpture variants. The featured commander rotates on a schedule — top-tier Legendaries like Zhuge Liang, Nebuchadnezzar II, and Alexander Nevsky cycle through periodically alongside less-priority commanders.

## How It Works
Governors spend gems per spin. Spins accumulate toward guaranteed-reward thresholds (chests) at specific spin counts (commonly cited thresholds: 8, 12, and 30 spins, though exact thresholds should be checked in-game as they can vary by event run). Rewards include the featured commander's exclusive sculptures, universal commander sculptures, resources, and speedups.

## Best Commanders
Prioritize spinning specifically when the featured commander is one you need — check the commander cycle schedule (community-tracked, e.g., via techgamesnews.com) before committing gems, since skipping a low-priority rotation to save for a high-priority one is usually correct.

## Gem Strategy
- **Only spin to a guaranteed threshold.** Stopping mid-way between thresholds means paying full price for sculptures without the bonus chest's compounding value.
- If you cannot reach the next threshold with your current gem stockpile, **hold your gems** and carry them to the next event featuring the same (or an equally desired) commander, rather than spending under-threshold.

## Spin Recommendations
- Threshold spins (per community consensus): 8 spins, 12 spins, 30 spins — each unlocks a guaranteed bonus chest. Verify exact current thresholds in-game, as they are subject to change between event runs.

## F2P Strategy
- Save gems from daily quests, VIP points, and map exploration specifically for Wheel of Fortune runs featuring a commander you need, rather than spending reactively on lower-priority rotations.
- Treat Wheel of Fortune, "More Than Gems" bundles, and VIP progress as the strongest long-term uses of free gems — general community consensus ranks these above random impulse spending.

## Whale Strategy
- High spenders can push to the top guaranteed threshold (e.g., 30 spins) on every high-priority commander rotation to guarantee acquisition of exclusive sculptures unavailable elsewhere.

## When to Stop
Stop spinning once you've hit your target threshold for the current commander, or once the featured commander is no longer a priority for your account's build — chasing "just a few more spins" past your planned threshold is the most common gem-wasting mistake.

## Common Mistakes
- Spinning past a threshold without a plan.
- Spending on a low-priority commander rotation instead of saving for a high-priority one.
- Spending under a threshold and losing the compounding bonus-chest value.
