# Lucerne Scrolls

## Overview
Lucerne Scrolls is Rise of Kingdoms' "Battle Pass"-style system, located in the Trading Post building, spanning 80 levels of rewards unlocked by earning Daily Activity Points over roughly a 60-day season. It is the primary in-game source of Legendary accessory blueprints.

## Value Analysis
Because it's the primary source of Legendary accessory blueprints, accessory-blueprint acquisition should be treated as the priority reward track whenever Lucerne Scrolls is active — accessories are otherwise comparatively hard to obtain (event blueprints, VIP chests, Lost Kingdom rewards).

## Best Rewards
- Legendary accessory blueprints (the standout, hardest-to-replace reward).
- Resources, speedups, and commander sculptures fill out the remaining levels.

## Progression Strategy
- Complete daily quests consistently rather than trying to catch up late — Activity Points accumulate from routine daily play (gathering, training, research, barbarian kills), so consistent login/play habits naturally advance your Lucerne Scrolls level.
- Prioritize any quest categories that are under-completed relative to others, since Activity Points are typically capped per category per day — spreading activity across categories (rather than over-focusing one) usually yields more total points.

## F2P Strategy
Lucerne Scrolls is one of the more F2P-friendly systems since it rewards consistent daily activity rather than direct spending — treat daily quest completion as a low-effort, high-value habit.

## Common Mistakes
- Letting daily quests lapse and trying to "catch up" near the season's end — most Activity Point sources are daily-capped, so missed days are largely unrecoverable.
