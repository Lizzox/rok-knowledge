# Sunset Canyon

## Overview
Sunset Canyon is an always-available campaign mode where players duel other governors for ranking and rewards, distinct from the scheduled Lucerne Scroll event track but related to it (Lucerne Scrolls references Sunset Canyon as one of its blueprint sources).

## How It Works
Use daily duel attempts against matched opponents; both wins and losses grant season-related rewards, though wins presumably grant more.

## Requirements
Simply using your daily attempts — no special preparation is strictly required, though a stronger commander/troop build improves win rate and reward quality.

## Rewards
Speedups and season-track progress; rewards have reportedly been increased to match Tavern chest value as of the 2025-2026 period, making consistent daily participation meaningfully valuable.

## Best Strategy
Use all daily attempts every day without fail — since even losses grant season-related rewards, there's no reason to skip attempts, and skipped attempts are pure lost value.

## F2P Strategy
Sunset Canyon is one of the most F2P-friendly recurring systems since daily participation alone (regardless of win/loss) generates meaningful reward value.

## Common Mistakes
Forgetting to use daily attempts — since they don't roll over and losses still reward, unused attempts are simply wasted value with no offsetting benefit.
