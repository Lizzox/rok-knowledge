# The Karuak Ceremony

## Overview
The Karuak Ceremony is a recurring PvE event that should never be skipped, since it's a key source of Legendary commander sculptures ("Golden Heads"). Players choose a difficulty (Easy, Normal, Hard, Nightmare, Hell) at the start, then fight through 50 waves of barbarian enemies, each wave granting its own reward.

## How It Works
- Choose a difficulty tier at event start — higher difficulties grant better rewards per wave but field tougher barbarians.
- Every 10th wave features a special "Elite" barbarian with improved rewards.
- Every 5th wave grants substantially more XP than standard waves.

## Requirements
Marches capable of defeating the barbarian tier matching your chosen difficulty; XP-boosting runes and commanders with bonus barbarian-damage/EXP passives (e.g., Lohar, Minamoto no Yoshitsune) significantly improve efficiency.

## Rewards
Legendary commander sculptures, XP, and equipment materials — notably, the Legendary Infantry piece **Karuak's Humility** is obtained specifically through this event.

## Best Strategy
- Use all available marches to specifically target every 5th wave for the XP bonus, rather than clearing waves purely in sequence.
- Bring XP-boosting runes and EXP-focused commanders (Lohar, Diaochan) to accelerate leveling secondary commanders alongside farming sculptures.

## F2P Strategy
Choose the highest difficulty your current march strength can reliably clear — going too high wastes time on failed attacks, while going too low leaves reward value on the table. Re-assess difficulty each time the event recurs as your account grows.

## Common Mistakes
- Choosing a difficulty tier too far above your current combat strength, resulting in failed waves and wasted march time.
- Ignoring the every-5th-wave XP bonus pattern and clearing waves inefficiently.

## Optimization Tips
- Because Karuak's Humility (a Legendary Infantry boot-slot piece) is obtained here, Infantry-focused players should treat this event as a recurring equipment-farming opportunity, not just a sculpture source.
