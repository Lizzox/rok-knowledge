# The Mightiest Governor

## Overview
The Mightiest Governor (MGE) is a recurring multi-stage individual competitive event spanning 6-7 days plus a Final Sprint, scoring different activity categories on different days. Top finishers receive Legendary commander sculptures.

## Event Phases
1. **Training Troops** — points for training new troops; historically one of the highest-scoring phases.
2. **Developing Cities** — points from completing building upgrades. Top scorers pre-stage builds (queue them before the phase opens) and release speedups only once the scoring window begins.
3. **Technology** — points from completing research. Queue expensive research before the phase opens, then release research speedups inside the window.
4. **Gathering Resources / Defeating Barbarians** — points from clearing barbarians. Targeting Barbarians level 24-25 (commonly found in zone 2-3 of the original kingdom map) yields the highest per-kill score.
5. **Killing Enemies** — a PvP phase scoring points for defeating other governors' troops.
6. **Final Sprint** — any of the above activities count again, but at a reduced point rate compared to their dedicated phase.

## Scoring Methods
Approximate ranking-reward breakpoints (per community-tracked data, subject to change): 1st place 180 points, 2nd 90, 3rd 60, 4th 50, 5th 40, 6th 30, 7th-10th 20, 11th-15th 10, 16th-25th 5, 26th-35th 3, 36th-45th 2, 46th-50th 1. Verify exact current point values in-game, as they are periodically rebalanced.

## Kill Event Strategy
Coordinate with alliance mates during the Killing Enemies phase to avoid splitting kill credit on the same targets; prioritize attacking weaker/isolated marches for reliable, low-risk points over contesting strong defended positions.

## Training Strategy
Pre-queue troop training before the Training Troops phase opens using saved speedups, then release them inside the scoring window for maximum point efficiency.

## Research Strategy
Queue your most expensive available research before the Technology phase opens; release research speedups only once the phase begins scoring.

## Building Strategy
Similarly, queue building upgrades before the Developing Cities phase opens and release construction speedups inside the window — this "pre-staging" pattern is the core mechanic behind competitive MGE scores across all phases.

## F2P Approach
- F2P players should focus effort on phases where a modest speedup stockpile goes furthest — typically the Barbarian-killing phase, since barbarian clears require time/AP more than premium speedups.
- Don't try to compete across all 5 phases evenly if resources are limited; concentrate stockpiled speedups into 1-2 phases where you can realistically place well, rather than spreading thin and placing poorly everywhere.

## Common Mistakes
- Releasing speedups before the scoring window opens (wasting the pre-staging advantage).
- Contesting the same PvP targets as alliance mates during the Killing Enemies phase.
- Spreading limited speedup stockpiles evenly across all phases instead of concentrating on 1-2 realistic targets.

## Optimization Tips
- Track the specific phase order and timing for your kingdom's current MGE run in advance, since pre-staging requires knowing exactly when each phase opens.
