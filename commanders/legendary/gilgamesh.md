# Gilgamesh

## Overview
Gilgamesh is a Legendary Archer/Conquering commander whose kit is built around Archer troops almost exclusively, forming the current top city-rally pairing alongside Nebuchadnezzar II by combining conquering power with anti-healing mechanics.

## Statistics
> Verify exact numbers in-game.

- Specialties: Archer, Conquering
- Troop type: Archer

## Skills
- Kit strongly favors Archer units across most skills — recommended to run as many Archer troops as possible.

## Recommended Usage
Rally-leading Archer primary for city sieges, ideally paired with a commander sharing anti-heal/conquering synergy.

## Best Pairings
- **Nebuchadnezzar II** — perfect rally pairing; their combined conquering and anti-healing mechanics remove garrison defenders' main survival tools.
- **Ramesses II** — viable alternative, though with some counter-synergy issues that make it less than perfect.

## Equipment
- Skill-damage and archer-attack equipment sets.

## Strategy Tips
- Open-field talent build: start with Rejuvenate for extra rage, then pursue Venomous Sting in the Archery Tree for ~18% extra march speed.
- For open-field specifically, consider removing Conquering-tree talents in favor of Archer/Skill tree points for more rage and better base stats.

## Strengths
- Extremely strong city-rally anti-heal synergy with Nebuchadnezzar II.

## Weaknesses
- Kit is heavily Archer-dependent; mixed-troop use undermines his effectiveness.
