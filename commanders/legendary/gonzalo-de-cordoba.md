# Gonzalo de Córdoba

## Overview
Gonzalo de Córdoba is a Legendary Engineering commander (part of a newer Siege/Engineering commander archetype) offering unusually high damage for a siege-type commander, built around a "Vigilant" stack mechanic that punishes enemies who attack his troops.

## Statistics
> Verify exact numbers in-game — figures below are community-reported.

- Specialties: Versatility, Attack, Engineering
- Troop type: Siege

## Skills
- **Active:** ~2400 damage AoE — very high for a siege-type commander.
- **Passive:** Active skill use has a 50% chance to grant 1 stack of "Vigilant" for 8 seconds; when hit by a basic attack, consumes a Vigilant stack to deal ranged smite damage back and disarm the attacker for 1 second.
- **Passive:** Being hit by a basic attack has a 100% chance to grant +40% ranged normal damage for 3 seconds (5-second cooldown).

## Recommended Usage
Engineering/Siege primary for ranged damage output; rated the strongest siege ranged commander in the current meta per community sources.

## Best Pairings
- **Gajah Mada** — best pairing; both bring smite and ranged damage for strong synergy.
- **John Hunyadi** — Cordoba's high active-skill ranged damage compensates for a gap in Hunyadi's kit.

## Equipment
- Engineering/siege equipment sets.

## Strategy Tips
- Engineering talent priorities: Engineering Mastery, Ballistics, Quick Construction, Reinforced Frame, Fortification, Smoldering Payload, Ranged Advantage, Strategic Positioning.

## Strengths
- Highest ranged active-skill damage among siege/engineering commanders per community reports.

## Weaknesses
- Siege-type commanders generally lack the mobility/versatility of Cavalry/Archer/Infantry commanders; niche role.
