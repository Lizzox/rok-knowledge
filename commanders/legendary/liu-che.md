# Liu Che

## Overview
Liu Che is a Legendary Infantry/Versatility/Attack commander widely regarded as the best Infantry commander currently in the game, anchoring numerous top-tier Infantry pairings across open-field content.

## Statistics
> Verify exact numbers in-game.

- Specialties: Infantry, Versatility, Attack
- Troop type: Infantry

## Skills
- **Active — Glory of Shizong:** AoE damage to multiple target troops; reduces their march speed, providing crowd control alongside damage.

## Recommended Usage
Top-tier Infantry primary or secondary for open-field combat.

## Best Pairings
- **Gorgo** — best pairing per community consensus; insane synergy, with Gorgo's attack increase directly benefiting Liu Che's first skill.
- **William Wallace** — currently one of the best combinations in the game for open field.
- **Sargon the Great** — great synergy with high combined normal-attack damage.
- **Guan Yu** — works well in most Infantry pairings, providing high damage.
- **Scipio Africanus Prime** — great skill synergy plus survivability Liu Che needs.
- **Bai Qi**, **Ragnar Lodbrok Prime**, **Philip II** — also cited as strong Infantry partners.

## Equipment
- Skill-damage and infantry-attack equipment sets.

## Strategy Tips
- Talent builds: Infantry tree (recommended for open-field) or Skill tree, depending on desired damage-vs-survivability balance.
- Always max the active skill first — it's his most powerful and most commonly cited priority across all builds.
- Note: only the primary commander's talents apply — level your primary to 60 as the top overall priority.

## Strengths
- Best-in-class Infantry AoE damage plus crowd control; extremely flexible across many top pairings.

## Weaknesses
- As with most Infantry AoE commanders, most effective in mixed/grouped engagements rather than pure 1v1 duels.
