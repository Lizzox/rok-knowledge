# Afonso de Albuquerque

## Overview
Afonso de Albuquerque is a Legendary ranged/support commander strong in both ranged combat and team support, featuring a stacking self-damage-reduction passive tied to dealing ranged skill damage.

## Statistics
> Verify exact percentages in-game.

- Specialties: Versatility, Support
- Troop type: Ranged (Siege-adjacent)

## Skills
- **Passive:** Whenever the troop deals ranged skill damage in ranged mode, takes 5% less damage for the next 10 seconds, stacking up to 4 times.

## Recommended Usage
Ranged support commander benefiting from partners who boost ranged skill damage and rage generation.

## Best Pairings
- **John Hunyadi** — boosts ranged skill damage and rage generation, letting Afonso use AoE skills more often and with greater strength.

## Equipment
- Ranged/support equipment sets.

## Strategy Tips
- Talent build (Versatility-focused): Strategic Prowess, Loose Formation, march-speed increase, healing-effect boosts, counterattack-damage reduction.

## Strengths
- Solid stacking self-mitigation while dealing ranged damage; strong support utility for ranged marches.

## Weaknesses
- Best suited to specific ranged-focused pairings rather than general-purpose use.
