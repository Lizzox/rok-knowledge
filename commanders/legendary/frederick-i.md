# Frederick I

## Overview
Frederick I is a Legendary Leadership/Conquering/Skill commander from Germany, purpose-built for attacking and conquering enemy garrisons/cities in PvP.

## Statistics
> Verify exact percentages/damage in-game.

- Specialties: Leadership, Conquering, Skill
- Troop type: Leadership (mixed-troop capable)

## Skills
- **Active — Barbarossa:** 100% chance to deal damage (community-reported ~800) to the target over 3 seconds.
- **Passive:** +10% damage when attacking other governors' cities specifically.
- **Passive — King of Deutschland:** +15% troop capacity in battle, letting his march punch above its nominal size.

## Recommended Usage
Primary rally/attack leader for city sieges and PvP offense against enemy cities/garrisons.

## Best Pairings
- **Mehmed II** — a powerful nuker with troop-capacity synergy; recommended as primary with Frederick as secondary in mixed-unit armies.
- **Yi Seong-Gye** — adds area damage and extra rage, effective in early kingdom stages.
- **Sun Tzu** — solid early-game pairing, giving Infantry stats and rage generation.

## Alternative Pairings
- Any strong Conquering-tree secondary for sustained city-attacking pushes.

## Equipment
- Skill-damage and troop-capacity equipment sets for offensive sieges.

## Strategy Tips
- Prioritize the Conquering talent tree first — rush Entrenched, then Tear of Blessing to reduce troop losses during prolonged siege combat.

## Strengths
- Strong, focused city-attacking kit; troop-capacity bonus effectively increases march power.

## Weaknesses
- Kit is specialized for attacking cities/garrisons — less impactful in open-field duels against roaming marches.
