# Alexander the Great

## Overview
Alexander the Great is a founding-era Legendary Cavalry/Skill commander who has remained a top pick for open-field and rally cavalry play throughout the game's history, largely thanks to synergy with skill-damage amplifiers like Yi Seong-Gye.

## Statistics
> Verify exact damage factors/rage costs in-game; values shift with rebalances.

- Specialties: Cavalry, Skill
- Troop type: Cavalry

## Skills
- **Active — Companion Cavalry Charge (unlock 1★):** Deals skill damage to the primary target and splash damage to nearby enemies.
- **Passive (unlock 2★):** Increases march attack.
- **Passive (unlock 3★):** Increases skill damage further and/or reduces incoming damage.
- **Passive (unlock 4★):** Additional rage generation or damage bonus.
- **Mastery skill (5★):** Further amplifies skill/attack output.

## Recommended Usage
Primary commander for Cavalry-focused open-field solo play and rallies, leaning on his own strong active-skill damage.

## Best Pairings
- Alexander the Great (primary) + Yi Seong-Gye (secondary) — the classic evergreen cavalry skill-damage pairing.

## Alternative Pairings
- Paired with other rage/utility secondaries depending on comp needs (e.g., Boudica for extra rage generation).

## Equipment
- Skill-damage and cavalry-attack equipment sets.

## Strategy Tips
- Strong pick for new-to-mid Legendary rosters because his kit rewards straightforward skill-damage stacking without complex conditional triggers.

## Strengths
- High, reliable single-target-plus-splash skill damage.
- Long-standing meta relevance, cheap to build a strong support cast around.

## Weaknesses
- Outclassed at the very top end by newer, more mechanically complex Legendary commanders in high-KvK-tier play.
