# Vercingetorix

## Overview
Vercingetorix is a Legendary Leadership/Versatility/Support commander designed to buff other troops and commanders rather than deal damage himself — a "support amplifier" archetype best used to extend an already-strong march.

## Statistics
> Verify exact percentages in-game.

- Specialties: Leadership, Versatility, Support
- Troop type: Leadership (mixed-troop capable)

## Skills
- **Active:** Buffs nearby allies' attack (+30%) on a frequent cycle.
- **Passive:** ~10% chance per basic attack to shield the lowest-health nearby friendly/allied troop (including himself) for 3 seconds.

## Recommended Usage
Universal support secondary/primary for players who already have a strong core march and want to extend its power further, rather than a first investment priority.

## Best Pairings
- **Arthur Pendragon** — Arthur's active skill gains a ~40% damage boost from Vercingetorix's pre-active buff window; with proper Combo armaments, Arthur's combo attacks also trigger Vercingetorix's basic-attack passives more often.
- **Philip II** (Vercingetorix primary, Philip secondary) — currently one of the most stable mainstream universal dual-commander setups.

## Equipment
- Support/rage equipment sets.

## Strategy Tips
- Best value comes from pairing him with commanders who already have strong kits — he's an amplifier, not a standalone carry.

## Strengths
- Frequent, reliable buff/shield cycling that meaningfully extends a strong primary's power.

## Weaknesses
- Low standalone value — best reserved for players who already have a top-tier core march to extend.
