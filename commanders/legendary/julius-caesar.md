# Julius Caesar

## Overview
Julius Caesar is a Legendary Leadership/Siege/Attack commander able to lead any troop type, offering strong all-around damage amplification, defensive mitigation, and city-attacking utility.

## Statistics
> Verify exact numbers in-game — figures below are community-reported.

- Specialties: Leadership, Siege, Attack
- Troop type: Any (unrestricted)

## Skills
- **Active/Expertise — Ahead of His Time:** Damage (factor ~400) to target; for 5 seconds increases troop attack/defense by 20% and all damage by 30%.
- **Passive — Divine Julius:** When his army is below 60% of total units, troops have a chance for -30% damage taken; overall max damage reduction reported at ~40%.
- **Passive — City Killer:** At max level, reduces enemy defense by ~30% — strong for attacking other governors' cities.

## Recommended Usage
Versatile primary/secondary for nuking marches, city attacks, and mixed-troop compositions.

## Best Pairings
- **Yi Seong-Gye** — his active skill's attack/damage buff strongly improves YSG's already-elite nuking damage.
- **Richard I** — defensive pairing; Richard adds further damage reduction and healing.
- **Aethelflaed** — benefits from mixed-troop synergy; her active skill reduces enemy defense/attack/health and counterattack damage.
- **Scipio Africanus** (Epic) — solid budget pairing if no other Legendaries are available.

## Equipment
- Skill-damage and troop-capacity equipment for offensive builds; defense gear if leaning into Divine Julius's damage-reduction niche.

## Strategy Tips
- His damage-reduction passive is conditional (army below 60% strength) — expect it to matter more in prolonged fights than short bursts.

## Strengths
- Flexible, troop-type-agnostic buffs make him a strong universal secondary; solid city-attacking utility via City Killer.

## Weaknesses
- Divine Julius's damage reduction only activates under specific army-strength conditions, reducing reliability early in a fight.
