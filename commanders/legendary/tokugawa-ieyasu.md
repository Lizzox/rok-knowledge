# Tokugawa Ieyasu

## Overview
Tokugawa Ieyasu is a Legendary garrison Cavalry commander built for defending castles and structures, featuring shield generation, health increases, and enemy-strength reduction — one half of the current strongest all-around garrison pairing (with Gorgo).

## Statistics
> Verify exact numbers in-game.

- Specialties: Cavalry, Garrison
- Troop type: Cavalry (garrison-focused)

## Skills
- Strong single-target damage; increased garrison damage; additional punitive damage to enemies; shield generation and HP increase.

## Recommended Usage
Premier garrison defender, especially effective against skill-damage-heavy rallying attackers.

## Best Pairings
- **Gorgo** (Gorgo primary) — widely considered the strongest all-around garrison pairing in the current meta, particularly effective against skill-damage-heavy rallies; Tokugawa specializes in reducing enemy skill damage and applying rally-weakening debuffs.

## Equipment
- Defense and garrison equipment sets. Infantry-adjacent talent recommendations: Iron Spear, Hold the Line, Snare of Thorns, Shield of the King (3/3 each).

## Strategy Tips
- His kit lacks multi-target damage and recovery — pair with a commander (like Gorgo) that covers those gaps.

## Strengths
- Strong single-target garrison damage plus shield/HP utility; excellent anti-rally skill-damage reduction.

## Weaknesses
- Lacks multi-target damage and self-recovery — a narrow garrison specialist.
