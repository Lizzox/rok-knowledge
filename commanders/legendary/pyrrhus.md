# Pyrrhus

## Overview
Pyrrhus is a Legendary Infantry/Versatility/Defense commander built around normal-attack damage amplification and shield mechanics, historically a strong early-KvK Infantry pick.

## Statistics
> Verify exact numbers in-game — figures below are community-reported.

- Specialties: Infantry, Versatility, Defense
- Troop type: Infantry

## Skills
- **Active:** Direct damage (damage factor ~1400) to a target; increases the commander's troop normal-attack damage for 3 seconds.
- **Passive:** +10% health, +15% march speed for Infantry units.
- **Passive:** -20% skill damage taken; if the troop has an active shield, deals +10% more damage.

## Recommended Usage
Infantry primary for open-field combat, especially strong during early KvK phases.

## Best Pairings
- **Charles Martel** — best pairing option specifically for KvK 1; both focus on normal-attack damage and shield mechanics.
- **Alexander the Great** — excellent shield synergy, with Alexander supplying significant damage.
- **Richard I** — powerful KvK 1/KvK 2 Infantry pairing; less direct synergy than Charles Martel but still a tanky combination.

## Equipment
- Defense and normal-attack-damage equipment sets.

## Strategy Tips
- Talent build: prioritize Elite Soldiers (base attack/defense/health) and Fleet of Foot (march speed) in the Infantry tree.

## Strengths
- Strong shield-synergy kit and reduced skill-damage taken make him resilient versus nuker-heavy compositions.

## Weaknesses
- Best known as an early-KvK pick — may be outpaced by newer Infantry Legendaries in late-game content.
