# Belisarius Prime

## Overview
Belisarius Prime is the Legendary "Prime" rework of the Epic commander [[belisarius]], reworked into a Cavalry hit-and-run specialist with rage denial and burst march speed — but community sources note his own talent tree is weak, so he's typically run as secondary.

## Statistics
> Verify exact numbers in-game — figures below are community-reported.

- Specialties: Cavalry, Skill
- Troop type: Cavalry

## Skills
- **Active:** ~2400 single-target damage; for 2 seconds, the target loses 50 rage per second (100 total); if the target is surrounded, applies Deception for 5 seconds.
- **Passive:** +30% Cavalry attack, +20% march speed; on exiting combat, +60% march speed for 5 seconds.
- **Skill upgrade priority:** 5,5,1,5 recommended; Expertise is strong and worth prioritizing, though the active and second skill can be upgraded first for hit-and-run play if Expertise isn't immediately available.

## Recommended Usage
Secondary commander for open-field hit-and-run play, swarm-target picking, and fast objective-carrying tasks (e.g., Ark carrying, rune grabbing) thanks to the burst march-speed passive.

## Best Pairings
- **Alexander Nevsky** (Nevsky primary) — Nevsky's surrounded-target bonus combines directly with Belisarius Prime's own surrounded-target Deception effect.
- **Joan of Arc Prime** (Joan primary, Support tree) — very strong, versatile pairing for nearly every situation.
- **Huo Qubing** or **Justinian I** — also solid partners.

## Equipment
- Skill-damage equipment; Wedge Formation recommended since Belisarius Prime and his top pairings deal heavy skill damage (+5% skill damage from the formation).

## Strategy Tips
- Because his talent tree is weak, invest primarily in the primary commander's tree and use Belisarius Prime as a stat-and-skill contributor.
- Use a mobility talent tree specifically when tasked with speed-critical objectives (Ark carrying, rune grabbing).

## Strengths
- Strong rage-denial and hit-and-run mobility; flexible across many top Cavalry pairings.

## Weaknesses
- Weak personal talent tree — almost always relegated to secondary.
