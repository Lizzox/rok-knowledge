# Aethelflaed

## Overview
Aethelflaed is a Legendary Archer/Skill commander, generally regarded as one of the strongest Archer-line commanders for rally and open-field play thanks to high single-target skill damage.

## Statistics
> Verify exact damage factors/rage costs in-game.

- Specialties: Archer, Skill
- Troop type: Archer

## Skills
- **Active (unlock 1★):** High single-target skill damage with possible splash to nearby enemies.
- **Passive (unlock 2★):** Increases skill/archer attack.
- **Passive (unlock 3★):** Increases crit rate or additional damage.
- **Passive (unlock 4★):** Further damage scaling.
- **Mastery skill (5★):** Damage amplification.

## Recommended Usage
Archer rally leader/primary for high burst damage against Infantry-heavy defensive comps.

## Best Pairings
- Paired with rage-generation or skill-damage-amplifying secondaries.

## Alternative Pairings
- Paired with other Archer-specialty commanders for a full Archer-focused march.

## Equipment
- Skill-damage and archer-attack equipment sets.

## Strategy Tips
- Archer marches punish Infantry-heavy garrisons; use Aethelflaed when scouting shows the enemy favors Infantry.

## Strengths
- High burst skill damage, strong against Infantry compositions.

## Weaknesses
- Archers are inherently fragile; vulnerable if caught by Cavalry rallies.
