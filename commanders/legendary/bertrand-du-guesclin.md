# Bertrand du Guesclin

## Overview
Bertrand du Guesclin is a Legendary Cavalry/Conquering/Defense commander built for tanky rallying, particularly effective in Ark of Osiris and KvK where withstanding being swarmed matters more than raw solo damage.

## Statistics
> Verify exact numbers in-game.

- Specialties: Cavalry, Conquering, Defense
- Troop type: Cavalry

## Skills
- **Active — Battle Hungry:** For 3 seconds, attacks have a 100% chance to deal direct damage (damage factor ~700) and reduce target rage by 20.
- **Passive:** +10% Cavalry attack, +10% health; outside alliance territory, +10% march speed and -5% damage from all sources.
- **Passive:** When attacking cities/strongholds, +10% damage; Cavalry gains a stacking +1% damage bonus per 10 seconds in battle (up to 15s duration per stack).
- **Passive:** +20% Cavalry defense; when attacked, ~10% chance to heal (healing factor ~250) every 5 seconds.

## Recommended Usage
Rally tank for Ark of Osiris and KvK, particularly valuable when paired with a high-damage but fragile Cavalry primary.

## Best Pairings
- **Alexander Nevsky** (Nevsky primary) — the definitive best pairing; Nevsky supplies damage while Bertrand's survivability keeps the march from being a glass cannon.

## Equipment
- Defense and health equipment sets for tanky rally roles.

## Strategy Tips
- His value is closely tied to Nevsky's strength — his tier-list position benefits significantly from that specific synergy.

## Strengths
- Strong self-healing and defense; excellent at surviving swarm attempts in Ark of Osiris/KvK.

## Weaknesses
- Relatively low personal damage output — largely a support/tank role for a stronger primary.
