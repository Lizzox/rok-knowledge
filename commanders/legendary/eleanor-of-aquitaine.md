# Eleanor of Aquitaine

## Overview
Eleanor of Aquitaine is a Legendary Cavalry/Garrison/Defense commander designed specifically for defending structures — castles, passes, fortresses, and alliance flags during KvK — rather than open-field combat.

## Recommended Usage
Dedicated defender for passes, forts, and alliance-flag structures during KvK; not intended for open-field use.

## Best Pairings
- **Charles Martel** — amplifies garrison defense and counter-damage effectively.
- **Richard I** — adds extra damage output and rally strength for offensive maneuvers within a defensive structure context.
- **Jan Žižka** — strong overall pairing; benefits from Cavalry without strictly requiring it, plus good anti-swarm survivability.
