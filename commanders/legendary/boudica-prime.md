# Boudica Prime

## Overview
Boudica Prime is the Legendary "Prime" rework of the Epic commander [[boudica]] (see also `commanders/epic/`), distinguished by an Expertise skill with an 80% chance to dispel control effects (silence, disarm, heal-immune) — a kit that requires her Expertise to be unlocked to realize full value.

## Statistics
> Verify exact percentages in-game.

- Specialties: Archer, Expertise-focused
- Troop type: Archer

## Skills
- **Expertise:** ~80% chance to dispel control effects such as silence, disarm, and heal-immune from herself/troops.
- All 4 base skills are described as broadly useful; max the first active skill before upgrading others, then proceed in any order.

## Recommended Usage
Tanky Archer secondary that becomes noticeably stronger the fewer troops remain, forming durable pairs with commanders sharing similar mechanics.

## Best Pairings
- **Honda Tadakatsu** — extremely durable combo; Honda's skill damage increases as troop count decreases while Boudica gains healing/tankiness under the same condition.
- **Amanitore** — Boudica's dispel removes Amanitore's self-silence downside (from her 80% damage-boost mechanic), resulting in unrestricted nuking plus Boudica's tankiness.
- **Henry V** — similar synergy to the Amanitore pairing but tankier with more single-target focus and less AoE.

## Equipment
- Archer-attack and rage-generation equipment; Rejuvenate-focused talents for extra rage.

## Strategy Tips
- Talent priority: Rejuvenate first for rage generation, then Archer-tree buffs for attack/defense/health/march-speed.
- Her Expertise is central to her strongest pairings — prioritize unlocking it before assuming she performs at full strength.

## Strengths
- Uniquely strong control-dispel utility; excellent tankiness synergy with several top Archer commanders.

## Weaknesses
- Full value gated behind her Expertise unlock — underwhelming without it.
