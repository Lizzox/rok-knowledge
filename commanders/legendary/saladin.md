# Saladin

## Overview
Saladin is a Legendary Cavalry commander rated Tier S for both open-field combat and rallying objectives, unusual among Cavalry Legendaries for strong anti-debuff/cleanse utility alongside solid damage and mobility-crippling effects.

## Statistics
> Verify exact numbers in-game — figures below are community-reported.

- Specialties: Cavalry
- Troop type: Cavalry

## Skills
- **Active — Hold Fast:** At max level, deals ~1,400 damage; cripples enemy march speed by ~30% and healing effect by ~40%.
- **Passive — Fate and Bond:** Reduces incoming skill damage by ~30% with a chance to recover/cleanse.

## Recommended Usage
Cavalry primary for both open-field roaming and rally leadership; particularly strong when reinforcing/attacking structures thanks to healing-reduction and counterattack mitigation against tanky defenders.

## Best Pairings
- **Genghis Khan** — one of the best nuking Cavalry pairs in the game.
- **Alexander Nevsky** — rated one of the best Cavalry pairs overall.
- **Mehmed II** — Saladin's healing-reduction/counterattack-mitigation debuffs complement Mehmed's high city-conquering attack.
- **Cao Cao** — high-mobility combo for quickly repositioning across the map, with Saladin as primary.

## Equipment
- Cavalry-attack and rage-generation equipment sets.

## Strategy Tips
- Talent priority: rush Rejuvenate in the Support Tree first (150 rage per skill use for either commander), then build into Cavalry attack/defense/march-speed/health nodes.

## Strengths
- Rare combination of high damage, mobility denial, and personal damage mitigation in one Cavalry kit.

## Weaknesses
- Less pure burst than dedicated nuker Cavalry commanders like Genghis Khan when used solo rather than paired.
