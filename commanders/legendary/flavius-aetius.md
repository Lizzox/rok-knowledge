# Flavius Aetius

## Overview
Flavius Aetius is a Legendary Infantry/Garrison/Skill commander widely used for KvK objective defense and Ark of Osiris, offering high single-target damage that ramps up further against low-health targets.

## Statistics
> Verify exact numbers in-game — figures below are community-reported.

- Specialties: Infantry, Garrison, Skill
- Troop type: Infantry

## Skills
- **Active — Spear of Empire:** Damage factor ~2300 to target; if the target has ≤50% units remaining, deals continuous additional damage (damage factor ~150) for 3 seconds.
- **Passive — Shield of Eagles:** +15% Infantry attack; while garrisoned, +15% Infantry defense and +15% health.
- **Passive — Defense of Gaul:** +20% counterattack damage; while garrisoned, normal attacks apply a stacking debuff (+1% damage taken from Infantry per stack, up to 10 stacks, 15s duration).

## Recommended Usage
Garrison objective defender for KvK and Ark of Osiris; also viable for open-field Infantry damage dealing.

## Best Pairings
- **Scipio Africanus** (Legendary) — massive field damage output, though the pairing lacks defensive skills and can be quickly destroyed if focused.
- **Guan Yu** (Guan Yu primary) — excellent field-fighting pair; Flavius serves as secondary given Guan Yu's stronger talent tree/skills.
- **Zenobia** — one of the best KvK/AOO structure-defense pairings, with strong counterattack and bonus stats; use Zenobia's talent tree for max damage/defense.

## Equipment
- Garrison-defense and skill-damage equipment sets.

## Strategy Tips
- Talent trees: Infantry, Garrison, Skill — choose based on whether the priority is open-field damage or garrison defense.

## Strengths
- Strong single-target damage that scales up against weakened enemies; excellent stacking counterattack debuff while garrisoned.

## Weaknesses
- Lacks strong defensive skills of his own when used in aggressive field pairings — vulnerable if focused without a tankier partner.
