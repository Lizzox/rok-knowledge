# Bai Qi

## Overview
Bai Qi is a Legendary Infantry/Versatility/Smite commander whose AoE Smite active skill is described by community sources as the highest AoE skill damage in the game, surpassing prior benchmarks like King Arthur's.

## Statistics
> Verify exact numbers in-game — figures below are community-reported.

- Specialties: Infantry, Versatility, Smite
- Troop type: Infantry

## Skills
- **Active:** Smite damage to up to 5 enemy troops in a fan; each target has a ~30% chance to take +15% extra damage — reportedly the highest AoE skill damage in the game (surpassing ~2,320 on 3 targets from a prior benchmark commander), with a built-in debuff ideal for large-scale field battles.

## Recommended Usage
Infantry primary for large-scale open-field and rally combat, particularly strong against grouped/massed enemy formations.

## Best Pairings
- **Liu Che** — one of the strongest Infantry pairings in the game, combining high Smite AoE damage, AoE slow, multiple debuffs, and rage generation.
- **Philip II** — solid alternative if Liu Che is unavailable, with strong buffs and synergy.

## Equipment
- Smite-damage equipment sets.

## Strategy Tips
- Talent build: rush Thunderous Smite at the end of the Smite tree first (+10% Smite damage), then Undying Fury, Hold the Line, and Strong of Body in the Infantry tree.

## Strengths
- Best-in-class AoE Smite damage for large-scale battles; strong debuff utility.

## Weaknesses
- Full damage ceiling requires the specific Smite-damage itemization/talent approach rather than standard skill-damage builds.
