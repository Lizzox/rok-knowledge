# John Hunyadi

## Overview
John Hunyadi is a Legendary ranged Engineering (Siege) commander, punishing enemies who close to melee range with significantly higher damage output at close range than at standard range.

## Statistics
> Verify exact numbers in-game — figures below are community-reported.

- Specialties: Engineering, Ranged
- Troop type: Siege

## Skills
- **Active:** Ranged direct damage (damage factor ~900, or ~1600 if the target is within melee range); +20% ranged skill damage for 3 seconds after use.
- **Passive:** +30% Siege attack, +15% rage gain speed; active-skill use has a ~20% chance (40% if not in melee range) to deal extra ranged direct damage (damage factor ~300).
- **Specialization:** +40% damage against marches with less than 50% units remaining.

## Recommended Usage
Ranged Siege primary for punishing overextended melee marches and finishing weakened targets.

## Best Pairings
- **Stephen III** — best synergy, purpose-designed to pair with John Hunyadi; combines strong skill damage, sustained attacks, and durability for Siege units.
- **Babur** — universal alternative pairing.
- **Margaret** — viable, though with minor synergy flaws compared to Babur.

## Equipment
- Siege/Engineering equipment sets.

## Strategy Tips
- His melee-range damage bonus rewards positioning that baits enemies into close combat rather than always kiting at max range.

## Strengths
- Strong finishing damage against weakened targets; punishing melee-range damage spike.

## Weaknesses
- Siege-based kit inherits the general mobility/versatility limitations of ranged commanders.
