# Takeda Shingen

## Overview
Takeda Shingen is a Legendary Cavalry commander notable for being the first Cavalry commander whose damage comes from normal attacks rather than skill damage — an unusual, tank-oriented kit built around sustain and counter-attack resilience.

## Statistics
> Verify exact percentages in-game.

- Specialties: Cavalry, Defense
- Troop type: Cavalry

## Skills
- **Passive:** Heals slightly-wounded troops every second whenever his first active skill is used (lasts ~4s); only activates when the army contains Cavalry units.
- **Passive:** +40% Cavalry defense.
- **Passive:** Increases survival of Takeda and his troops on the battlefield.
- **Passive:** ~10% chance to take 25% less damage from counterattacks and normal attacks — strong versus heavy nukers.

## Recommended Usage
Tanky Cavalry secondary/primary for open-field brawling, especially effective against skill-damage-heavy "nuker" opponents due to his counter-damage mitigation.

## Best Pairings
- **Attila** (primary, best pairing) — Attila's +30% normal attack and +40% Cavalry attack synergize directly with Takeda's normal-attack-based damage; together bonus damage can reach ~380% when both use active skills.
- **Genghis Khan** — Khan's active skill damage is amplified further by Takeda's buffs, with Takeda as secondary.
- **Joan of Arc Prime** — benefits from Takeda's buffs, healing, and damage-taken reduction as secondary.

## Equipment
- Defense and health equipment sets; his kit already provides strong survivability so offense-leaning gear on the pairing's primary is often preferred.

## Strategy Tips
- Because his damage comes from normal attacks (not skills), pair him with commanders whose kits amplify normal-attack damage rather than pure skill-damage amplifiers.

## Strengths
- Excellent survivability and counter-nuker resilience; strong buffs for a normal-attack-focused primary like Attila.

## Weaknesses
- Low personal skill-damage output; relies on pairing for real offensive threat.
