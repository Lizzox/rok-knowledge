# Qin Shi Huang

## Overview
Qin Shi Huang (QSH) is a Legendary Archer/Versatile/Skill commander uniquely built around consuming rage to deal AoE damage, forming what community sources call the best Archer open-field pairing in the game alongside Zhuge Liang.

## Statistics
> Verify exact numbers in-game — figures below are community-reported.

- Specialties: Archer, Versatile, Skill
- Troop type: Archer

## Skills
- **Active — One Conquers Six:** AoE damage; grants 60% of max damage percentage at level 5 — should be prioritized first for leveling.
- Kit includes a recurring silence effect (fueled by rage consumption) central to his open-field dominance.

## Recommended Usage
Archer open-field primary; among the strongest current picks for open-field combat when paired correctly.

## Best Pairings
- **Zhuge Liang** (QSH primary) — rated the single best Archer open-field pairing in the game, with no other march comparing per community consensus; Zhuge Liang's Chu Shi Biao skill aids rage regeneration for QSH's silence-cycling kit.
- **Yi Seong-Gye** — strong budget alternative if Zhuge Liang is unavailable; also improves rage via Art of Archery and adds 5-target AoE via Expertise.

## Equipment
- Skill-damage and rage-generation equipment sets.

## Strategy Tips
- Talent build: mix of Archer and Skill trees, working toward Whistling Arrows (Archer) then Clarity and Rejuvenate (Skill); alternatively, a Skill+Leadership-focused build prioritizing rage generation, skill damage, and survivability to cycle the silence effect frequently.
- Always level his first active skill first regardless of build.

## Strengths
- Best-in-class open-field Archer performance when paired with Zhuge Liang; recurring silence severely disrupts enemy skill usage.

## Weaknesses
- Full potential requires the Zhuge Liang pairing specifically; budget pairings are strong but a clear step down.
