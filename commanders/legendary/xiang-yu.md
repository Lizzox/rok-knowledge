# Xiang Yu

## Overview
Xiang Yu is a Legendary Cavalry/Conquering/Skill commander built as a rage-engine nuker, notable for reduced active-skill rage cost while on the map, and long-standing top pairing status with William I in open field.

## Statistics
> Verify exact numbers in-game — figures below are community-reported.

- Specialties: Cavalry, Conquering, Skill
- Troop type: Cavalry

## Skills
- **Active:** Direct damage (damage factor ~900) to up to 3 targets in a forward fan; hit targets suffer -10% defense for 3 seconds.
- **Passive:** +20% Cavalry attack, +3% march speed; when attacked, ~20% chance to gain +5% march speed for 3 seconds.
- **Passive:** -50 rage cost for active skills while on the map.

## Recommended Usage
Cavalry rage-engine primary for both open-field roaming and rallying.

## Best Pairings
- **William I** (open field) — the number-one open-field pairing per community consensus, even after Alexander Nevsky's release, combining double AoE skill damage, march speed, attack buffs, and debuffs.
- **Chandragupta Maurya** (rally) — current rally meta; works against essentially every garrison type including Zenobia's Infantry garrison.
- **Alexander Nevsky** — very high skill damage when paired with Xiang Yu's fast skill cycling, though the pairing lacks defense.
- **Saladin** — supplies the tankiness Xiang Yu's kit otherwise lacks.
- **Baibars** (Epic) — solid F2P/budget option providing AoE, march speed, and attack.

## Equipment
- Skill-damage and rage-generation equipment sets.

## Strategy Tips
- Talent build ("Rage-Engine"): start Skill Tree with Rejuvenate, then Feral Nature, then Cavalry Tree's Undying Fury — all rage-focused nodes.

## Strengths
- Extremely fast skill cycling due to reduced rage cost; longstanding top-tier open-field and rally pairings.

## Weaknesses
- Lacks personal defensive tools — needs a tankier partner (e.g., Saladin) to cover that gap.
