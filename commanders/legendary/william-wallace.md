# William Wallace

## Overview
William Wallace is a Legendary Infantry commander built around "Smite" damage — a damage type affected by normal-damage bonuses rather than skill-damage bonuses — making him a top-tier rally/open-field Infantry pick with a distinct itemization/talent path.

## Statistics
> Verify exact numbers in-game — figures below are community-reported. Active skill rage requirement: ~1000.

- Specialties: Infantry, Smite
- Troop type: Infantry

## Skills
- **Active (1000 rage):** Smite damage (damage factor ~2400) to a target troop; extra smite damage (damage factor ~300) to up to 3 enemy troops currently attacking his troop.

## Recommended Usage
Infantry rally leader/open-field primary; particularly effective for rallying given the multi-target smite component.

## Best Pairings
- **Liu Che** (Liu Che secondary) — Liu Che is the best Infantry commander in the game per community consensus; used as secondary specifically to benefit from William Wallace's Smite talent tree.
- **Scipio Aemilianus** — excellent, dominant rally pairing (best suited to rallying over open field).
- **Scipio Prime** — solid alternative if Liu Che is unavailable, offering damage, shielding, and march-speed buffs.

## Equipment
- Smite-damage equipment sets (distinct from typical skill-damage gear, since Smite scales with normal-damage bonuses).

## Strategy Tips
- Talent build: rush Thunderous Smite at the end of the Smite tree first (core damage talent, +10% Smite damage), then shift to the Infantry branch for Strong of Body and Hold the Line.

## Strengths
- Very high multi-target Smite damage; anchors several dominant rally pairings.

## Weaknesses
- Requires understanding the distinct Smite-damage itemization/talent path rather than standard skill-damage builds.
