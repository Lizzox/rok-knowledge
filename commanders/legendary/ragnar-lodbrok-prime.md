# Ragnar Lodbrok Prime

## Overview
Ragnar Lodbrok Prime is the Legendary "Prime" rework of the Epic Ragnar Lodbrok, dealing most of his damage through skill damage with a rage-threshold mechanic that grants bonus damage once rage reaches 70%.

## Statistics
> Verify exact numbers in-game — figures below are community-reported.

- Specialties: Infantry, Versatility, Skill
- Troop type: Infantry

## Skills
- **Active — Raven's Fury:** ~2000 direct damage to 3 enemy troops.
- **Passive:** Whenever rage reaches 70%, troops gain +15% skill damage for 4 seconds.

## Recommended Usage
Infantry primary/secondary for open-field combat, particularly strong when paired with rage-generation partners that reliably trigger the 70%-rage bonus.

## Best Pairings
- **Liu Che** (Liu Che secondary) — the number-one open-field pairing; both deal heavy AoE damage with strong defense, march speed, and attack — a well-balanced Infantry march.
- **Scipio Prime** — Scipio Prime gains rage ~30% faster, synergizing directly with Ragnar Prime's 70%-rage damage bonus for high combined damage while staying tanky.
- **Scipio Africanus (Prime)** — described as probably the best pairing, with both commanders buffing each other for a strong secondary Infantry march.

## Equipment
- Skill-damage and rage-generation equipment sets.

## Strategy Tips
- Talent build: primarily Skill tree (e.g., Feral Nature) with remaining points in the Infantry tree, since most damage comes from skill damage rather than normal attacks.

## Strengths
- Strong AoE skill damage with a valuable rage-threshold damage spike; excellent synergy with fast rage-generation partners.

## Weaknesses
- Full damage potential depends on reliably reaching the 70% rage threshold — poor rage generation blunts his kit.
