# Hector

## Overview
Hector is a Legendary Leadership/Versatility/Support commander able to lead Infantry, Cavalry, or mixed troops, dealing strong single-target damage and buffing normal-attack or skill damage depending on the primary commander's specialty.

## Statistics
> Verify exact percentages in-game.

- Specialties: Leadership, Versatility, Support
- Troop type: Leadership (mixed-troop capable)

## Skills
- **Passive:** If the primary commander has Infantry or Cavalry talents, ~50% chance to grant +20% normal damage for 5 seconds (7s cooldown); otherwise grants +30% skill damage for 5 seconds (7s cooldown).
- **Passive:** On the map, Infantry troops have a ~50% chance to increase Smite damage by 15% (up to 3 times per second, 10s cooldown); Cavalry troops have a ~20% chance to launch extra combo attacks (damage factor ~400, up to 3 times per second, 10s cooldown).

## Recommended Usage
Flexible secondary commander for both Infantry-Smite and Cavalry-Combo compositions; also viable in a garrison role (e.g., paired with David IV).

## Best Pairings
- **Achilles** — strong synergy, though overlapping cooldowns limit how much their skills can snowball together.
- **Arthur Pendragon**, **Attila**, **William Wallace**, **Bai Qi** — all viable partners depending on troop-type focus (Combo vs. Smite).
- **David IV** — specialized garrison-role pairing.

## Equipment
- Skill-damage or Smite-damage equipment sets depending on which pairing archetype is used.

## Strategy Tips
- Pair Hector specifically with commanders whose kits benefit from his Combo (Cavalry) or Smite (Infantry) triggers — his value is entirely conditional on the partner's specialty.

## Strengths
- Rare flexibility across Infantry and Cavalry team comps; strong single-target damage contribution.

## Weaknesses
- Value is highly pairing-dependent; picking an incompatible partner wastes much of his kit.
