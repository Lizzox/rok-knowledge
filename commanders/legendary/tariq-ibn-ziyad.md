# Tariq ibn Ziyad

## Overview
Tariq ibn Ziyad is a Legendary Infantry/Conquering commander excelling as a rally leader in KvK and Ark of Osiris, offering very high single-target damage and strong Infantry attack buffs.

## Statistics
> Verify exact numbers in-game — figures below are community-reported.

- Specialties: Infantry, Conquering
- Troop type: Infantry

## Skills
- **Active:** Direct damage (damage factor ~2500) to a target.
- **Passive:** +40% Infantry attack, +10% damage against Cavalry; +10% march speed outside alliance territory.
- **Passive:** While attacking cities/strongholds, +30% Infantry defense; normal attacks have a ~30% chance to reduce target attack by 30% for 3 seconds.

## Recommended Usage
Rally-leading Infantry primary for KvK and Ark of Osiris objective sieges.

## Best Pairings
- **Scipio Africanus (Legendary)** — best pairing per community consensus; insane combined damage output.
- **Sargon the Great** — excellent open-field synergy; both boost Infantry attack significantly.
- Other viable Infantry alternatives: **Alexander the Great**, **Leonidas**.

## Equipment
- Infantry-attack and city-siege equipment sets.

## Strategy Tips
- Talent build: Defense and Infantry trees.

## Strengths
- Very high single-target damage combined with strong city/stronghold-attacking Infantry buffs.

## Weaknesses
- Kit is heavily rally/siege-oriented; less notable outside those specific contexts.
