# Pakal II

## Overview
Pakal II is a Legendary Infantry/Conquering/Defense commander noted for significant damage reduction and survivability while still dealing solid damage — a strong all-around Infantry pick, especially for rallies.

## Statistics
> Verify exact numbers in-game.

- Specialties: Infantry, Conquering, Defense
- Troop type: Infantry

## Skills
- **Passive:** +40% health (a very strong Infantry health bonus).
- **Passive:** +30% attack.
- **Active-linked passive:** Each use of his first skill grants ~20% damage reduction, triggerable every 5 seconds.

## Recommended Usage
Rally primary or secondary requiring high survivability plus meaningful damage; run with Infantry troops.

## Best Pairings
- **Harald Sigurdsson** — offers strong combined sustain and damage; kits mesh very well.
- **Guan Yu** (Guan Yu primary) — Guan Yu's AoE nuking damage plus Pakal II's survivability and anti-swarm resilience form a hard-to-break combo.
- **Alexander the Great** — viable pairing usable with either commander as primary.

## Equipment
- Defense and health equipment sets for rally survivability.

## Strategy Tips
- Rally talent build: prioritize survivability and rage generation via the Defense tree, which is especially valuable in long rally fights.

## Strengths
- Excellent survivability-to-damage ratio; strong anti-swarm resilience when paired with an AoE partner.

## Weaknesses
- Individually less flashy than pure nuker Infantry Legendaries — his strength is in the combined package with a partner.
