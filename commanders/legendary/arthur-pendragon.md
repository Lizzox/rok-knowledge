# Arthur Pendragon

## Overview
Arthur Pendragon (King Arthur) is a Legendary Cavalry/Combo commander built around low-rage-cost, rapid-firing combo attacks, forming several premier Cavalry pairings in the current meta.

## Statistics
> Verify exact numbers in-game.

- Specialties: Cavalry, Combo
- Troop type: Cavalry

## Skills
- Active skill has notably low rage requirements, enabling very frequent skill activation as part of the "combo" mechanic.

## Recommended Usage
Cavalry primary/secondary for open-field, rallies, and "murder ball" (concentrated single-target-focus) group compositions.

## Best Pairings
- **Huo Qubing** — potentially the best pairing; strong synergy, low rage requirements, and boosted skill damage for insane combined output.
- **Subutai** — Subutai is specifically designed to pair with Arthur as secondary; considered the best pairing for that combo.
- **Alexander Nevsky** (Nevsky primary) — high skill damage boost and AoE burst damage.
- **Philip II** — boosts stats/damage across almost every troop type; particularly strong in murder-ball situations.
- **Vercingetorix** — Arthur's active skill gains a ~40% damage boost from Vercingetorix's pre-active buff window.

## Equipment
- Combo-damage and rage-generation equipment sets.

## Strategy Tips
- Talent build: max the Combo branch first, up to Iron and Steel (primary damage source), then invest remaining points into the Cavalry branch (Undying Fury, Equestrian Excellence) for rage regeneration and march speed.
- Key talents: Combo Mastery, Quick Strike, Relentless Assault (Combo tree); Charge, Cavalry Tactics, Iron Spear (Cavalry tree).
- Always max the first active skill first.

## Strengths
- Extremely frequent skill activation due to low rage cost; anchors several top-tier Cavalry pairings.

## Weaknesses
- Full value depends on specific partner synergy (Huo Qubing, Subutai) — less remarkable in a vacuum.
