# Ishida Mitsunari

## Overview
Ishida Mitsunari is a Legendary commander from Japan considered one of the best dedicated Gathering commanders, while also functioning as a strong secondary commander in PvP by boosting skill damage, health, and rage regeneration for a primary partner.

## Statistics
> Verify exact percentages in-game.

- Specialties: Gathering, Support
- Troop type: Mixed / gathering-focused

## Skills
- **Passive:** Troops become enraged faster and assault more over the following 3 seconds.
- **Passive:** Faster food/stone/wood gathering.
- **Passive:** Increased troop load capacity.
- **Passive:** Improved troop health; deals more active skill damage when serving as secondary commander.

## Recommended Usage
Dedicated gathering-march commander; alternatively, a secondary support commander in PvP.

## Best Pairings
- **Baibars** (Epic) — benefits significantly from the ~15% active skill damage bonus; has useful crowd control and is easy to fully max as an Epic.
- **Sun Tzu** (Epic) — fast rage regeneration together lets both commanders' active skills fire frequently.

## Equipment
- Gathering-speed/load equipment for farming; rage/health gear for the PvP secondary role.

## Strategy Tips
- Two talent builds: a Gathering build (maximize gathering speed) for farming, and a PvP build maximizing rage regeneration, healing, defense, and health for combat use.

## Strengths
- Versatile: excellent gathering efficiency plus meaningful PvP secondary utility.

## Weaknesses
- Not a primary combat damage dealer.
