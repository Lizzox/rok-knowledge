# Sargon the Great

## Overview
Sargon the Great is a Legendary Infantry/Skill/Versatility commander whose active skill provides a sustained direct-damage buff, plus a stacking "Awed" debuff that increases the target's skill damage taken — making him an excellent amplifier for a high skill-damage partner.

## Statistics
> Verify exact percentages in-game.

- Specialties: Infantry, Skill, Versatility
- Troop type: Infantry

## Skills
- **Active — Sar Kissatim:** Boosts direct damage for 5 seconds; can be used repeatedly to maintain a continuous damage buff.
- **Passive:** Attacks have a 100% chance to apply a stack of "Awed" to the target, increasing skill damage taken by 3% for 10 seconds (up to 10 stacks).
- **Passive:** +15% march speed and +10% damage against troops for Infantry units.

## Recommended Usage
Infantry primary/amplifier for open-field combat, ideally paired with a strong skill-damage secondary that benefits from the Awed debuff.

## Best Pairings
- **Guan Yu** — supplies AoE damage that Sargon otherwise lacks.
- **Scipio Prime** — the Awed debuff pairs particularly well with Scipio's Fiery Rage expertise skill (+10% skill damage).
- **Alexander the Great** — huge survivability and bonus stats for field battles.
- **Liu Che** — smite/normal-damage synergy forms a powerful combo.

## Equipment
- Skill-damage and rage-generation equipment sets.

## Strategy Tips
- Talent build: Skill tree focused on rage regeneration to maximize active-skill uptime.

## Strengths
- Strong stacking skill-damage-taken debuff amplifies any high-damage partner significantly.

## Weaknesses
- Lacks AoE damage of his own — needs a partner to capitalize on the Awed debuff.
