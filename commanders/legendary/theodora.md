# Theodora

## Overview
Theodora is a Legendary Garrison/Support commander purpose-built for defending cities and structures, considered one of the premier city-defense commanders in the current meta.

## Statistics
> Verify exact percentages in-game.

- Specialties: Garrison, Support
- Troop type: Leadership/Garrison-focused

## Skills
- **Active — Byzantine Empress:** Hits up to 5 enemies around her; damage reduced 15% per target after the first.
- **Passive — Keeper of Secrets:** As garrison commander, troops gain bonus defense and take reduced damage from rallying armies.
- **Passive — Co-Ruler:** Attack bonus for all troops; troops above 50% strength deal increased damage.
- **Passive:** ~10% chance, when her garrison troops take damage, to gain extra damage for 3 seconds.

## Recommended Usage
Dedicated garrison/city defender against enemy rallies.

## Best Pairings
- **Wu Zetian** — best-rated garrison partner; counters rally troops while empowering Theodora as primary garrison commander.
- **Yi Sun-Sin** (Yi Sun-Sin primary, Theodora secondary) — rated the top city-defense pairing in the current meta for mixed-troop garrisons; Yi Sun-Sin covers defense/damage reduction while Theodora adds rally-damage reduction.

## Equipment
- Defense and garrison-focused equipment sets.

## Strategy Tips
- Two recommended talent builds: one for defending your own city, one for defending structures (forts/passes) — pick based on the specific defensive target.
- Prioritize garrison stats, incoming-damage/skill-damage reduction, and rage restoration in talent choices.

## Strengths
- Elite-tier city/structure defense utility; strong synergy with other top defensive Legendaries.

## Weaknesses
- Narrow role — offers little value outside of garrison/defense contexts.
