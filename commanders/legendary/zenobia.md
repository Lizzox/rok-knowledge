# Zenobia

## Overview
Zenobia is a Legendary Garrison/Support commander built around sustained healing for garrison defenders, forming (alongside Yi Sun-Sin) what community sources call the current best garrison meta pairing.

## Statistics
> Verify exact percentages in-game.

- Specialties: Garrison, Support
- Troop type: Garrison-focused

## Skills
- **Active — Queen of Palmyra:** Heals the nearby allied troop with the lowest remaining unit percentage (healing factor ~100), then heals her own troops the following turn (healing factor ~500); healed troops gain +20% health for 2 seconds.
- **Passive — Unmatched Beauty:** While leading garrison (own city or stronghold), troops take 3% reduced damage from normal attacks and deal 3% increased normal attack damage.
- **Passive — Conqueror's Ambition:** Infantry units gain +10% health and +10% attack; all troops deal 2% more damage to rallied troops.

## Recommended Usage
Garrison healer/defender, ideally paired with a mixed-troop-capable partner to reinforce with any troop type.

## Best Pairings
- **Yi Sun-Sin (YSS)** — rated the current best garrison meta pairing, enabling reinforcement with any troop type.
- **Flavius** — best secondary if reinforcing the garrison with Infantry-only troops.
- **Wu Zetian** — supplies damage, debuff, and skill-damage reduction Zenobia otherwise lacks, together forming a very hard-to-break garrison.

## Equipment
- Defense and healing-focused equipment sets.

## Strategy Tips
- Prioritize rage-generating talents in the Garrison Tree, especially "Nowhere to Turn," which grants substantial rage when attacked — letting her healing skill fire more often.

## Strengths
- Exceptional sustained garrison healing; strong synergy in the current defensive meta.

## Weaknesses
- Kit provides little value outside dedicated garrison/defense roles.
