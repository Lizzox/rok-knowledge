# Lu Bu

## Overview
Lu Bu is a Legendary Leadership/Conquering commander focused on attacking cities, offering a strong AoE active skill that both damages and shreds enemy defense, making follow-up damage from a paired commander far more effective.

## Statistics
> Verify exact numbers in-game — figures below are community-reported.

- Specialties: Leadership, Conquering
- Troop type: Leadership (mixed-troop capable)

## Skills
- **Active — Sky Piercer:** Hits up to 3 targets in a fan-shaped forward area (damage factor ~1000, reduced 15% per extra target); reduces target defense by 50% for 3 seconds.
- **Passive — Horse of Horses:** Extra damage when attacking cities.
- **Passive — Man of Men:** Bonus attack; normal attacks have a ~10% chance (50% when paired with Diaochan specifically) to deal +30% damage for 3 seconds.

## Recommended Usage
City-attacking primary; his defense-shred active skill is best leveraged by pairing with a high-damage secondary that benefits from the debuff.

## Best Pairings
- **Attila** — insane combined buffs plus Lu Bu's defense reduction make a strong rallying-objective pair.
- **Chandragupta Maurya** — Lu Bu provides the defense debuff while Chandragupta supplies skill damage.
- **Diaochan** (Epic) — a classic legendary/epic thematic pairing; also directly boosts Man of Men's proc chance.

## Equipment
- Skill-damage and city-siege equipment sets.

## Strategy Tips
- Two talent build options: one maximizing active-skill damage/rage restoration for uptime, another boosting mixed-troop stats for city attacks — choose based on whether you're leading or supporting.
- Since Lu Bu has no defensive ability of his own, pair with a commander offering healing/defense.

## Strengths
- Strong AoE damage combined with a significant defense-shred debuff, amplifying any paired damage dealer.

## Weaknesses
- No self-sustain/healing; vulnerable in prolonged fights without a supportive pairing.
