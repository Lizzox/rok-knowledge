# Ashurbanipal

## Overview
Ashurbanipal is a Legendary Archer/Conquering/Skill commander rated as an extremely strong pick for rallying objectives in KvK and Ark of Osiris, with AoE damage that resists being easily swarmed.

## Statistics
> Verify exact numbers in-game — figures below are community-reported.

- Specialties: Archer, Conquering, Skill
- Troop type: Archer

## Skills
- **Active:** Direct damage to up to 5 enemy troops in a fan (damage factor ~1500, each additional target reduces damage 15%); extra direct damage (damage factor ~1000) to the current primary target.
- **Passive:** +20% Archer attack; outside alliance territory, Archer units also gain +20% defense and +15% march speed.
- **Passive:** +20% skill damage dealt, -15% normal damage taken.
- **Passive (rally-specific):** When leading a rally, direct damage hits have a 50% chance to grant +3% skill damage for 10 seconds, stacking up to 10 times.

## Recommended Usage
Rally-leader Archer primary for objective-based combat (KvK, Ark of Osiris) where the stacking rally buff has time to build up.

## Best Pairings
- **Zhuge Liang** — both deal AoE damage to 5 targets with strong Archer bonus stats, excellent synergy.
- **Boudica Prime** — strong single-target damage plus sustain, especially for rallying buildings.
- **Hermann Prime** — among the highest Archer damage output combos, both with AoE, effective against garrison reinforcements.

## Equipment
- Skill-damage and archer-attack equipment sets, plus rally-capacity gear.

## Strategy Tips
- Value compounds the longer a rally lasts due to the stacking skill-damage buff — best used in prolonged objective sieges rather than quick skirmishes.

## Strengths
- Excellent AoE damage resistant to swarming; strong stacking rally buff for sustained sieges.

## Weaknesses
- Full value requires rally-leading and sustained engagement; less impactful in short solo skirmishes.
