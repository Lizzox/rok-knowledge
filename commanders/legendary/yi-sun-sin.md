# Yi Sun-sin

## Overview
Yi Sun-sin (YSS) is a Legendary Garrison/Versatility commander widely rated one of the best city defenders in the game, notable for being able to lead a mixed army (no single troop-type restriction) while defending.

## Statistics
> Verify exact percentages in-game.

- Specialties: Garrison, Versatility
- Troop type: Mixed (any)

## Skills
- **Active — Duke of Loyalty and Warfare:** Deals heavy damage; when serving as garrison commander, can hit up to 3 targets with 15% reduced damage per additional target.
- **Passive — Battle of Myeongnyang:** ~10% chance for +20% defense and +15% damage for a short duration; only applies within alliance territory or while serving as garrison commander.
- **Passive — Geobukseon:** When army strength falls below 50%, troops gain +30% defense and +20% damage — a comeback mechanic.
- **Passive — Broken Moon:** +20% attack and +20% defense when commanding two or more troop types — rewards mixed-army garrisons.

## Recommended Usage
Premier garrison/city defense commander, especially for alliances defending with mixed-troop-type garrisons.

## Best Pairings
- **Wu Zetian** — one of the strongest garrison defenders; specifically covers YSS's relative weakness against large rally armies.
- **Theodora** — enhances rage generation and resilience across multiple simultaneous rallies.
- **Zenobia** — "YSS + Zenobia" is described as the current best garrison meta, letting alliance members reinforce with any troop type.

## Equipment
- Defense and garrison-focused equipment; his talent build should focus on incoming-damage reduction.

## Strategy Tips
- Recommended talents: Adamantine Walls (+15% watchtower defense as garrison commander), Strategic Prowess (+20% troop defense for 2s after skill use).

## Strengths
- Best-in-class flexible garrison defense; comeback mechanic below 50% strength adds resilience.

## Weaknesses
- Relatively weaker against sustained large rally armies without a complementary partner like Wu Zetian.
