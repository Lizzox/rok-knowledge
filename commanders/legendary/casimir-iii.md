# Casimir III

## Overview
Casimir III is a Legendary Integration/Gathering/Defense commander, one of the best dedicated gathering commanders in the game, but community sources explicitly advise F2P players against investing universal sculptures into him.

## Statistics
> Verify exact numbers in-game.

- Specialties: Integration, Gathering, Defense
- Troop type: Siege (gathering-focused)

## Skills
- **Active:** Direct damage (damage factor ~1400) to a target; target deals 10% less normal damage for 3 seconds.
- **Passive:** Gathering all resources from a point grants additional resource items.
- **Passive — Last Piast:** +40% attack for Siege units, +25% extra damage while gathering from a resource point.

## Recommended Usage
Dedicated resource-gathering commander for maximizing farming efficiency.

## Best Pairings
- Any other gathering commander (e.g., **Joan of Arc**).

## Equipment
- Gathering-speed and load-capacity equipment sets.

## Strategy Tips
- Talent build: focus entirely on the Gathering tree to maximize his gathering specialization.
- F2P players should NOT invest universal commander sculptures into Casimir III — prioritize meta open-field/combat commanders instead. Best suited to whale accounts or dedicated farm accounts.

## Strengths
- Best-in-class gathering efficiency with bonus resource generation.

## Weaknesses
- Zero meaningful combat value; poor investment priority for F2P resource allocation.
