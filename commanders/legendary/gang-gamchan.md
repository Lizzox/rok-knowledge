# Gang Gamchan

## Overview
Gang Gamchan is a Legendary Cavalry/Versatility/Combo commander described as the strongest single-target damage dealer among Cavalry combo commanders, with built-in self-healing tied to combo attacks.

## Statistics
> Verify exact numbers in-game — figures below are community-reported.

- Specialties: Cavalry, Versatility, Combo
- Troop type: Cavalry

## Skills
- **Active:** Launches 2 combo attacks against a target (damage factor ~1400); for 5 seconds after, Cavalry gain +5% health and +5% defense.
- **Passive:** -10% damage taken on the map; +20% Cavalry march speed, +30% Cavalry defense.
- **Passive:** Combo-attack damage recovers a portion of slightly-wounded units (~10% of the combo damage dealt as healing).

## Recommended Usage
Cavalry primary for open-field single-target duels, particularly effective in extended fights due to built-in sustain.

## Best Pairings
- **Achilles** — best pairing; Achilles' AoE combo triggers Gang Gamchan's healing mechanic, adding both AoE damage and survivability.
- **Arthur Pendragon** — viable alternative if Achilles is unavailable.

## Equipment
- Cavalry-attack and sustain-focused equipment sets.

## Strengths
- Strong single-target damage, useful debuff, and self-healing sustain via combo attacks.

## Weaknesses
- Lacks AoE damage of his own — needs a partner (like Achilles) to cover that gap.
