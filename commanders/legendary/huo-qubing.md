# Huo Qubing

## Overview
Huo Qubing is a Legendary Cavalry/Versatility/Skill commander known for exceptional single-target damage, effective in open-field battles, rallies, and Ark of Osiris/Osiris League content.

## Statistics
> Verify exact numbers in-game.

- Specialties: Cavalry, Versatility, Skill
- Troop type: Cavalry

## Skills
- Kit centers on very high single-target active-skill damage, amplified further by specific rage-generation/damage-boosting pairings.

## Recommended Usage
Cavalry primary for open-field duels, rallies, and Ark of Osiris — the current-meta top single-target Cavalry nuker per community sources.

## Best Pairings
- **Alexander Nevsky** — rated the strongest meta combination as of recent community guides, dominating both open field and rallies; Nevsky's skill significantly boosts primary-target damage.
- **Joan of Arc** — strong field cavalry synergy; boosts damage and rage regeneration for more frequent skill use.
- **William I** — AoE damage, added defense/survivability, and rage regeneration.
- **Xiang Yu** — enhances skill damage and march speed for both open field and rally.
- Budget alternatives: **Honda Tadakatsu**, **Mehmed II** — any AoE/defense Cavalry commander works reasonably.

## Equipment
- Skill-damage and cavalry-attack equipment sets; rage-generation gear to maximize skill uptime.

## Strategy Tips
- Talent build options: maximize rage for constant heavy active-skill usage, or shift points from Cavalry to Skill tree for faster hit-and-run skill cycling.

## Strengths
- Among the strongest single-target Cavalry damage in the current meta, especially paired with Alexander Nevsky.

## Weaknesses
- Primarily single-target focus — less effective versus spread-out/grouped enemy formations than AoE specialists.
