# Thutmose III

## Overview
Thutmose III is a Legendary Archer/Versatility/Support commander from Egypt, offering solid Archer stat buffs, a skill-damage-vulnerability debuff, and counterattack mitigation.

## Statistics
> Verify exact percentages in-game.

- Specialties: Archer, Versatility, Support
- Troop type: Archer (benefits from, but doesn't strictly require, full Archer)

## Skills
- **Passive:** +15% Archer attack, +10% march speed; +15% damage outside alliance territory.
- **Passive:** +10% Archer defense; attacks have a ~10% chance to apply a debuff increasing the target's skill damage taken by 15% for 3 seconds.
- **Passive:** -10% counterattack damage taken; when attacked, ~10% chance to deal skill damage back to the attacker.

## Recommended Usage
Archer primary for open-field combat, benefiting most from a majority-Archer march though not strictly requiring 100% Archer troops.

## Best Pairings
- **Boudica Prime** — noted as a pairing that works well together.

## Equipment
- Skill-damage and archer-attack equipment sets.

## Strategy Tips
- Talent priority: Rejuvenate first for rage generation, then march-speed talents to maximize open-field versatility.

## Strengths
- Solid all-around Archer buffs plus a useful skill-damage-vulnerability debuff for follow-up damage.

## Weaknesses
- Less specialized/impactful than top-tier dedicated Archer nukers like Edward of Woodstock or Aethelflaed.
