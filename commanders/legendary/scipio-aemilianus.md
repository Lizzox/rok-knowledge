# Scipio Aemilianus

## Overview
Scipio Aemilianus is a Legendary Infantry/Conquering/Smite commander purpose-built for leading Infantry rallies, especially strong when the rally is being swarmed en route to its target.

## Statistics
> Verify exact numbers in-game — figures below are community-reported.

- Specialties: Infantry, Conquering, Smite
- Troop type: Infantry

## Skills
- **Active — Africanus:** Heavy Smite damage to target (up to damage factor ~2700 at max level) plus increases target's damage taken afterward (up to ~20%).
- **Passive — Huntmaster:** Up to +20% Infantry march speed and +30% Infantry health at max level.
- **Passive — Dawn of Rome:** Increases normal damage; chance to remove/prevent negative effects; can reduce incoming normal damage.

## Recommended Usage
Rally leader for Infantry rallies, particularly effective versus swarm-heavy defenses.

## Best Pairings
- **William Wallace** — incredibly strong rally pairing per community consensus.
- **Tariq ibn Ziyad** — solid alternative if William Wallace is unavailable, with strong combined damage synergy.

## Equipment
- Smite-damage and Infantry-durability equipment sets.

## Strategy Tips
- Talent build (rallying): heavily prioritize the Smite tree, which is described as newer but powerful.

## Strengths
- Excellent rally-leading kit designed specifically to counter swarm tactics against the rally.

## Weaknesses
- Kit is rally-specific; less notable for solo open-field use.
