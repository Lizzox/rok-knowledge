# Cao Cao

## Overview
Cao Cao is a Leadership/Support Legendary commander known for strong march-wide healing and rage-generation utility, making him one of the most enduring rally-leader picks in RoK history alongside Sun Tzu.

## Statistics
> Verify exact percentages/rage costs in-game.

- Specialties: Leadership, Skill
- Troop type: Leadership (boosts whichever troop type is led)

## Skills
- **Active — Roiling Waves (unlock 1★):** Deals skill damage and applies a debuff to enemies.
- **Passive (unlock 2★):** Increases rage generation for the march.
- **Passive (unlock 3★):** Heals the march / reduces troop losses.
- **Passive (unlock 4★):** Additional damage or defensive bonus.
- **Mastery skill (5★):** Further amplifies healing or rage generation.

## Recommended Usage
Rally leader for large alliance rallies where sustained rage generation and healing keep the rally's troops alive and skills firing more often.

## Best Pairings
- Paired as primary with strong Infantry/Cavalry secondary commanders in mega rallies.

## Alternative Pairings
- Sun Tzu (similar rage/healing archetype) can substitute depending on availability.

## Equipment
- Rage-generation and healing-boost equipment sets.

## Strategy Tips
- His healing and rage utility scale with rally size, making him disproportionately strong in large alliance rallies versus solo play.

## Strengths
- Exceptional sustained utility for prolonged rally combat.
- Long-term meta relevance due to healing/rage being evergreen needs.

## Weaknesses
- Weak damage output compared to pure skill-damage Legendary commanders; not a strong open-field solo pick.
