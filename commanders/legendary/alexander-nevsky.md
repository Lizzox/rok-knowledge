# Alexander Nevsky

## Overview
Alexander Nevsky is a Legendary Cavalry/Skill commander widely regarded as one of the strongest single-target Cavalry nukers in the current meta, headlining numerous top-tier pairings across open field and rally content.

## Statistics
> Verify exact numbers in-game — figures below are community-reported.

- Specialties: Cavalry, Skill
- Troop type: Cavalry

## Skills
- **Active — Hero of Peipus:** Massive single-target damage (damage factor ~2300); inflicts a defense-reduction debuff up to ~45% when the enemy is surrounded.
- **Passive — Expeditionary:** Increases Cavalry attack and march speed; increases health outside alliance territory.
- **Passive — Guard of the Republic:** +20% Cavalry defense; reduces damage Nevsky receives from enemies.

## Recommended Usage
Elite Cavalry primary for open field and rallies; per community sources, frequently used as a **secondary** commander since his own talent tree doesn't apply in that slot but his skills/stats still contribute.

## Best Pairings
- **Joan of Arc Prime** (Nevsky primary, Joan secondary) — top cavalry open-field pairing as of early 2026; Nevsky's charge damage plus Joan's heal-over-time sustain the march through extended fights.
- **Xiang Yu** — one of the most powerful rallying pairings; Xiang Yu increases Nevsky's skill damage and debuffs targets.
- **Chandragupta Maurya** — rallying pair; Chandragupta adds survivability/defense-reduction while Nevsky nukes.
- **Justinian I**, **Bertrand du Guesclin**, **Huo Qubing**, **Jan Žižka** — all cited elsewhere as premier Nevsky pairings across different roles.

## Equipment
- Skill-damage and cavalry-attack equipment sets.

## Strategy Tips
- Since his talent tree doesn't apply when used as secondary, decide his role (primary vs. secondary) based on which partner's talent tree is stronger overall for the specific comp.

## Strengths
- Extremely high single-target burst with a strong defense-shred debuff versus surrounded targets; anchors numerous top-tier pairings.

## Weaknesses
- Talent tree only matters when used as primary — plan pairing roles accordingly.
