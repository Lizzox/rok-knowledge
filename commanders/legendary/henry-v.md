# Henry V

## Overview
Henry V is a Legendary Archer/Conquering/Support commander offering strong single-target damage plus anti-swarm sustain mechanics, built around reducing incoming skill/normal damage while amplifying what the target takes.

## Statistics
> Verify exact percentages in-game.

- Specialties: Archer, Conquering, Support
- Troop type: Archer

## Skills
- **Passive:** -30% skill damage taken.
- **Passive:** +attack, +defense, +march speed for his troops; enemy skill damage taken +30%.
- **Expertise:** Reduces damage taken when swarmed by multiple enemies.

## Recommended Usage
Archer primary for rallies and prolonged single-target engagements requiring durability against swarms.

## Best Pairings
- **Gilgamesh** — natural pairing; both reduce normal-attack damage taken, resulting in high combined sustain.
- **Nebuchadnezzar** (Henry as primary) — similar mechanics that make the pairing very hard to swarm down.
- **Boudica Prime** — comparable tanky single-target archetype pairing.

## Equipment
- Skill-damage and rage/defense equipment sets.

## Strategy Tips
- Talent build: Rejuvenate and Loose Formation for rage generation and reduced skill damage taken, then fill the Archer tree for buffs.
- Run a full-Archer march to access his complete kit and talent synergy.

## Strengths
- Strong single-target damage combined with unusually high swarm resistance for an Archer commander.

## Weaknesses
- Requires a full-Archer composition for full value; less flexible than mixed-troop Legendaries.
