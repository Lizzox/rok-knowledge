# Gorgo

## Overview
Gorgo is a Legendary Infantry/Garrison/Defense commander rated among the strongest Infantry commanders currently, offering high skill damage combined with excellent durability that scales over long fights.

## Statistics
> Verify exact numbers in-game.

- Specialties: Infantry, Garrison, Defense
- Troop type: Infantry

## Skills
- Kit includes strong damage reduction, increased counterattack damage, and durability that improves the longer a fight lasts — well suited to prolonged defensive engagements.

## Recommended Usage
Open-field Infantry brawler and long-duration garrison defender; community sources caution against deploying her as a garrison commander before she reaches max level, as doing so risks heavy losses.

## Best Pairings
- **Liu Che** — best open-field pairing; Gorgo's attack-damage increase directly benefits Liu Che's first skill.
- **Flavius** — strong garrison pairing for defending objectives.
- **Tokugawa** — reduces enemy skill damage and applies rally-weakening debuffs, complementing Gorgo's damage reduction.
- **Hector** — enhances counterattack damage and survivability; Gorgo's damage reduction extends his uptime further.

## Equipment
- Defense and garrison equipment sets; damage-reduction gear compounds her natural durability.

## Strategy Tips
- Prioritize Impenetrable Fortifications and Impregnable talents in the Infantry/Garrison tree.
- Do not use her as a garrison commander until fully leveled — an underleveled Gorgo in garrison risks heavy troop losses per community guidance.

## Strengths
- Excellent scaling durability in long fights; strong open-field and garrison dual-usefulness.

## Weaknesses
- Underperforms significantly if deployed underleveled, especially in garrison.
