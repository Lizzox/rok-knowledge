# Mulan

## Overview
Mulan ("Warrior Woman") is a Legendary support commander from China with no troop-type restriction, making her one of the most flexible secondary commanders in the game — she can be slotted into virtually any march composition.

## Statistics
> Verify exact percentages in-game.

- Specialties: Support, Versatility
- Troop type: Any (unrestricted)

## Skills
- **Active — Soul of the Crusaders:** Grants troops/allies in the area increased attack, defense, and health (~5–15%) plus increased march speed (~5–25%) for 2 seconds.
- **Passive:** +10% attack; when Mulan is the secondary commander, her active skill also causes the target to take increased damage from all sources for 3 seconds.
- **Passive — One-of-a-Kind General:** As secondary, increases attack and applies the vulnerability debuff described above.

## Recommended Usage
Best used as a **secondary commander** — her kit is explicitly stronger in that slot (per her own skill text), buffing the primary commander's damage output and the whole march's stats.

## Best Pairings
- **Joan of Arc** — buffs stack for a game-changing combo in Ark of Osiris, KvK, and Ruins content.
- **Alexander the Great** — improves his already-strong open-field resistance against nukers.
- **Richard I** — combines strong debuffs/healing/defense for a hard-to-kill pairing.
- **William I** (full Cavalry march) — stackable buffs make this a strong Cavalry secondary slot.

## Alternative Pairings
- Any primary commander benefits from her unrestricted, stat-stacking support kit.

## Equipment
- Support/rage equipment sets; since she's not the main damage dealer, gearing can prioritize whatever the march's primary commander needs.

## Strategy Tips
- Skill priority: max her first skill (Soul of the Crusaders) first as the strongest, then the second skill for survivability.
- Do not force her into a primary role — her passives are explicitly secondary-slot oriented.

## Strengths
- Total troop-type flexibility; stacks well with almost any primary commander.
- Strong in high-value modes (Ark of Osiris, KvK, Ruins).

## Weaknesses
- Underwhelming if used as a primary commander instead of secondary.
