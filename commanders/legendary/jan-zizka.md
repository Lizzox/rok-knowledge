# Jan Žižka

## Overview
Jan Žižka is a Legendary Cavalry/Garrison commander whose kit is almost entirely garrison-oriented — community sources explicitly state there's little point using him for open-field fighting.

## Statistics
> Verify exact percentages in-game.

- Specialties: Cavalry, Garrison
- Troop type: Cavalry (garrison-focused)

## Skills
- **Active — One Eyed Knight:** Skill damage to target; deals additional skill damage when his troop has fewer than 50% units remaining — a comeback mechanic valuable for defending objectives.
- **Passive:** +3% Cavalry attack; while garrisoned, +3% Cavalry defense and +2% skill damage dealt.
- **Passive:** -2% normal damage taken; while garrisoned and taking skill damage, ~10% chance to dispel all normal buffs from the attacker (triggerable every 8s).

## Recommended Usage
Top-tier garrison Cavalry defender for objective defense; not recommended for open-field use.

## Best Pairings
- **Jadwiga** — best Cavalry pairing per community sources; excellent stat synergy boosting normal attack damage, nuke damage, and rage generation.
- **Alexander Nevsky** — exceptional pairing; Nevsky's 4th skill combined with Jan Žižka can increase skill damage taken by the target by up to 60%.

## Equipment
- Garrison-defense and Cavalry equipment sets.

## Strategy Tips
- Run pure-Cavalry troops where possible, especially when paired with Nevsky, to maximize buff overlap.

## Strengths
- Excellent garrison comeback mechanic; strong Cavalry-garrison stat synergy.

## Weaknesses
- Explicitly poor in open-field combat — a narrow, garrison-only investment.
