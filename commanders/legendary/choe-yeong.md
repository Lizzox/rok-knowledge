# Choe Yeong

## Overview
Choe Yeong is a Legendary Archer/Garrison/Defense commander from Korea, one of only two commanders in the game (per community sources) able to deal true damage — damage that bypasses normal defense calculations — via a heal-to-damage conversion mechanic.

## Statistics
> Verify exact numbers in-game — figures below are community-reported.

- Specialties: Archer, Garrison, Defense
- Troop type: Archer

## Skills
- **Active:** For 10 seconds, every heal performed also deals 80% of the heal strength as true damage to up to 5 nearby enemy troops (15% reduced damage per additional target beyond the first); also heals a large portion of his own slightly-wounded troops (healing factor ~1400).
- **Passive:** Active-skill use inflicts a stacking 10% health-reduction debuff on the target for 7 seconds (stacks up to 5 times).
- **Passive:** +25% counterattack damage.

## Recommended Usage
Premier Archer-garrison defender; also viable in open field with the right pairing.

## Best Pairings
- **Dido** (Dido primary, Choe Yeong secondary) — the standard garrison configuration.
- **Heraclius** (as secondary instead of Dido) — an alternative garrison build.
- **Shajar al-Durr** — recommended open-field pairing (with a 5/5/1/1 skill upgrade split).

## Equipment
- Defense and garrison-focused Archer equipment sets.

## Strategy Tips
- His true-damage heal-conversion mechanic is uniquely powerful against high-defense targets since true damage ignores defense stats.

## Strengths
- Rare true-damage mechanic; strong self-healing and counterattack damage for garrison defense.

## Weaknesses
- Kit is primarily garrison-oriented; open-field usage requires a specific supporting pairing to shine.
