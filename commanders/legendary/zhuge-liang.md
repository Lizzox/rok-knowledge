# Zhuge Liang

## Overview
Zhuge Liang is a Legendary Archer/Skill commander long recognized as a strong open-field skill-damage dealer, particularly effective versus grouped or Infantry-heavy enemy compositions.

## Statistics
> Verify exact damage factors/rage costs in-game.

- Specialties: Archer, Skill
- Troop type: Archer

## Skills
- **Active (unlock 1★):** Skill damage to primary target with splash to nearby enemies.
- **Passive (unlock 2★):** Increases skill damage.
- **Passive (unlock 3★):** Increases attack or crit rate.
- **Passive (unlock 4★):** Further damage scaling.
- **Mastery skill (5★):** Damage amplification.

## Recommended Usage
Archer primary for open-field roaming and rallies targeting Infantry-heavy defenders.

## Best Pairings
- Paired with rage-generation or skill-amplifying secondaries (e.g., Yi Seong-Gye-type support).

## Alternative Pairings
- Paired with other Archer commanders for a full-Archer march.

## Equipment
- Skill-damage and archer-attack equipment sets.

## Strategy Tips
- Effective "roaming skirmisher" pick given strong splash damage output relative to march size.

## Strengths
- High reliable skill damage, good versus Infantry.

## Weaknesses
- Archer fragility versus Cavalry rushes remains a structural weakness.
