# Yi Seong-Gye

## Overview
Yi Seong-Gye (commonly abbreviated "YSG") is one of the longest-standing top-tier Legendary Cavalry/Skill commanders, prized for pairing exceptionally well as a secondary commander with almost any Cavalry or skill-focused primary due to a passive that boosts skill damage and active-skill damage output. He has remained meta relevant for years, which is unusual in RoK's commander power-creep cycle.

## Statistics
> Exact numeric damage factors and rage costs shift slightly with rebalances — verify current values in-game before theorycrafting a build.

- Specialties: Cavalry, Skill
- Troop type: Cavalry

## Skills
- **Active — Northern Expedition (unlock 1★):** Deals skill damage to a target and nearby enemies; damage factor scales with star level.
- **Passive — Growth of Power (unlock 2★):** Increases the primary commander's skill damage.
- **Passive — Great Rule (unlock 3★):** Reduces damage taken by the march.
- **Passive — King's Decree (unlock 4★):** Further increases skill damage/attack for the march.
- **Mastery skill (5★, Legendary):** Additional skill-damage or rage-related bonus (exact mastery effect should be checked in-game; skill sets are periodically reworked).

## Recommended Usage
YSG is used almost exclusively as a **secondary commander** to amplify a primary commander's skill damage — a "damage amplifier" archetype. He is rarely the primary himself in current meta.

## Best Pairings
- Alexander the Great (primary) + Yi Seong-Gye (secondary) — classic long-standing rally/open-field cavalry combo.
- Any strong Cavalry skill-damage primary benefits from YSG's amplification passives.

## Alternative Pairings
- Guan Yu, Richard I, or other Cavalry/Skill legendary primaries.

## Equipment
- Skill-damage and rage-generation focused equipment sets recommended for the primary; YSG himself benefits from survivability gear when paired as secondary.

## Strategy Tips
- Because his kit is almost entirely passive skill-damage amplification, YSG's value is realized through the *primary* commander's active skill — pick a primary with a strong, frequently-triggered active skill.

## Strengths
- Exceptional, evergreen secondary-commander value across many team comps.
- Strong in both rally and open-field due to skill-damage amplification being broadly useful.

## Weaknesses
- Weak or unremarkable as a standalone primary commander.
- Entirely dependent on the primary commander's active skill to convert his bonuses into results.
