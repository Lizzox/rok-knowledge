# Richard I

## Overview
Richard I is a Legendary Infantry/Skill commander who has remained one of the premier Infantry tanks in RoK since near-launch, valued for garrison defense and durable open-field infantry play.

## Statistics

- Troop type: Infantry

## Skills
- **Active (unlock 1★):** Deals skill damage and taunts/holds enemy attention.
- **Passive (unlock 2★):** Increases defense.
- **Passive (unlock 3★):** Reduces incoming damage further / increases health.
- **Passive (unlock 4★):** Additional damage-reduction or counterattack effect.
- **Mastery skill (5★):** Further defensive amplification.

## Recommended Usage
Primary tank for garrison defense and Infantry-led open-field marches designed to absorb damage while secondary/allied damage dealers finish fights.

## Best Pairings
- Paired with Infantry-focused secondaries for maximum tankiness in garrisons.

## Alternative Pairings
- Paired with Guan Yu for a dual-Infantry defensive garrison combo.

## Equipment
- Defense/health-focused equipment sets (tanking gear).

## Strategy Tips
- Excellent low-risk garrison pick for players who don't have top-end offensive Legendary commanders yet.

## Strengths
- Outstanding durability, long-standing garrison meta relevance.

## Weaknesses
- Low damage output; not a first pick for offensive rallies or rankings-focused open-field kills.
