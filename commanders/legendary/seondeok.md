# Seondeok

## Overview
Seondeok is a Legendary Integration/Gathering/Attack commander built purely around resource farming — not intended for open-field fighting, rallies, garrison defense, or Canyon combat. Her value is entirely in making farming marches more efficient, with particular strength in gold gathering.

## Statistics
> Verify exact percentages in-game.

- Specialties: Integration, Gathering, Attack
- Troop type: Mixed / Siege recommended for gathering marches

## Skills
- **Passive:** +30% gold gathering speed.
- **Passive:** +20% gathering speed for other resources.

## Recommended Usage
Dedicated gathering-march commander, especially valuable for gold farming since her bonus to gold is higher than to other resources.

## Best Pairings
- **Cleopatra VII** — both commanders are purpose-built for resource farming; pair whichever has the stronger gathering talent build as primary.
- Any gathering-specialty commander works acceptably as a partner.

## Equipment
- Gathering-speed and load-capacity equipment sets.

## Strategy Tips
- Focus talent points entirely in the Gathering tree: prioritize "Gathering Mastery" per resource (gold especially) and "The More The Better" for bonus resources on gather completion.
- Send Siege troops on gathering marches led by Seondeok, per community recommendation.

## Strengths
- Best-in-class gold-gathering efficiency.

## Weaknesses
- Zero combat utility — do not use her for anything but dedicated gathering marches.
