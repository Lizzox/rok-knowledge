# Ivar the Boneless

## Overview
Ivar the Boneless is a Legendary Infantry/Conquering/Defense commander rated as an extremely strong rally commander, combining high AoE Smite damage with self-sustaining rage generation that lets his active skill fire unusually often.

## Statistics
> Verify exact numbers in-game — figures below are community-reported.

- Specialties: Infantry, Conquering, Defense
- Troop type: Infantry

## Skills
- **Active:** Smite damage to up to 3 enemy troops in a fan (damage factor ~2500, reduced 15% per additional target); for 3 seconds after, +15 rage per second.
- **Passive:** +20% Smite damage; +10% damage against Cavalry units.
- **Passive:** ~50% chance to gain 30 extra rage when dealing Smite damage.

## Recommended Usage
Rally-leading Infantry primary with exceptionally high skill uptime due to strong self-rage-generation.

## Best Pairings
- **Scipio Aemilianus** (Scipio primary) — Scipio's 20% damage debuff sets up Ivar's ~2,500 damage-factor active to hit an already-weakened target.
- **Sun Tzu Prime** — very strong rallying pairing; Sun Tzu Prime's kit synergizes with Ivar's rage engine for a highly functional Infantry march.
- **Bai Qi**, **William Wallace**, **Liu Che** — also viable, high-AoE-damage alternatives.

## Equipment
- Smite-damage and rage-generation equipment sets.

## Strategy Tips
- His rage-generation passives mean he benefits less from external rage-generation partners than most nukers — prioritize raw damage synergy in pairing choice instead.

## Strengths
- Extremely high, self-sustaining AoE Smite damage output for rallies.

## Weaknesses
- Requires the specific Smite-damage itemization/talent approach shared with William Wallace, Bai Qi, etc.
