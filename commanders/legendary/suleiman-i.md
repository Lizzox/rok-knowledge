# Suleiman I

## Overview
Suleiman I is a Legendary Leadership/Conquering commander built around buff/debuff support rather than direct skill damage, well-suited to leading mixed-troop marches and multi-rally scenarios.

## Statistics

- Specialties: Leadership, Conquering
- Troop type: Leadership (mixed-troop capable)

## Skills
- **Active — Magnificent Sultan:** Deals damage while reducing enemy defense and health for 2 seconds.
- **Passive — Annexation:** Increases troop attack/defense; significant damage reduction when fighting outside alliance territory.
- **Passive — The Siege of Vienna:** When attacking cities/garrisons, chance to reduce damage taken from normal attacks by ~20% — important for rally-oriented builds.

## Recommended Usage
Mixed-troop rally leader/support commander for multi-rally scenarios, valued for buff/debuff utility rather than raw personal damage.
