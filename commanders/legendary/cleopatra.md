# Cleopatra VII

## Overview
Cleopatra VII is a Legendary commander widely regarded as one of the best dedicated Gathering commanders in the game, thanks to fast gathering rate and high load capacity, plus an Integration trait letting her lead mixed-unit armies and a Support trait for battlefield healing.

## Statistics
> Verify exact percentages in-game.

- Specialties: Integration, Gathering, Support
- Troop type: Mixed (Integration)

## Skills
- **Active:** Heals lightly-wounded units in her own army and nearby friendly armies, and grants a short (~2s) defense increase.
- **Passive:** Increases stone gathering speed.
- **Passive:** Increases food, wood, and gold gathering speed.
- **Passive:** Increases siege unit attack and defense while gathering at resource points.

## Recommended Usage
Dedicated gathering-march commander; also usable as a support/healing secondary in PvE (barbarians/forts) thanks to her active heal.

## Best Pairings
- **Lohar** — his EXP-boost and healing skill complement Cleopatra's own healing kit, easing barbarian/fort farming.
- **Sun Tzu** — both bring AoE heal/damage utility, strong together defending a garrison.

## Alternative Pairings
- Any commander needing fast leveling benefits from Lohar+Cleopatra's EXP bonus synergy (per Lohar's kit).

## Equipment
- Gathering-speed and load-capacity equipment sets for her primary gathering role.

## Strategy Tips
- Prioritize the Gathering talent branch — per community guides, rush Gathering Mastery for Gold and Stone first since Stone is her best-gathered resource.
- Civilizations with gathering bonuses (e.g., France, China per some guides) further amplify her efficiency.

## Strengths
- Best-in-class gathering efficiency; flexible enough to also serve support/healing roles.

## Weaknesses
- Not a combat-damage commander; limited value in a pure PvP rally/open-field damage role.
