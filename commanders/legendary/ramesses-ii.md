# Ramesses II

## Overview
Ramesses II is a Legendary Archer/Versatile/Attack commander, community-described as the most powerful debuffing Archer commander available to F2P players, notable for very high single-target damage combined with an anti-heal effect.

## Statistics
> Verify exact numbers in-game — figures below are community-reported.

- Specialties: Archer, Versatile, Attack
- Troop type: Archer

## Skills
- **Active — King of Kings:** Damage ranging from ~1,600 to ~2,000 depending on level; also increases defense-reduction debuff by 5%; prevents the target from healing for 2 seconds.

## Recommended Usage
Archer primary/secondary for open-field and rally, particularly strong versus targets relying on healing sustain.

## Best Pairings
- **Yi Seong-Gye** — boosts King of Kings damage by ~50% (up to ~3,000 damage in 2 seconds); both being Archer-specialty adds extra attack/defense/march-speed synergy.
- **Artemisia I** — natural pairing; in exchange for the silence risk, both gain +50% extra damage.
- **Edward of Woodstock** — versatile partner strong in both Rally and Open Field.
- **Hermann** (Epic) — Hermann's damage skills and Silence effect pair well.

## Equipment
- Skill-damage and archer-attack equipment sets.

## Strategy Tips
- Strong F2P-accessible pick — community sources specifically flag him as the best debuffing Archer available without heavy spending.
- The anti-heal component of King of Kings is especially valuable against sustain-heavy defensive comps.

## Strengths
- Very high single-target damage plus a meaningful anti-heal debuff; excellent F2P value.

## Weaknesses
- Primarily single-target — less effective than AoE Archer commanders against grouped enemies.
