# Hannibal Barca

## Overview
Hannibal Barca is a Legendary Leadership/Conquering/Skill commander built for mixed-army compositions, rewarding armies that field all three troop types at once, and specializing in attacking enemy garrisons.

## Statistics
> Verify exact numbers in-game.

- Specialties: Leadership, Conquering, Skill
- Troop type: Leadership (mixed-troop capable)

## Skills
- **Passive:** +10% damage when the army fields 3 different troop types simultaneously — directly rewards mixed compositions.
- **Active — Siege:** Very strong against enemy garrisons; includes a healing factor (community-reported ~1000) that offsets some damage taken while attacking.

## Recommended Usage
Primary for mixed-troop rallies attacking enemy garrisons/cities.

## Best Pairings
- **Julius Caesar** (Caesar primary) — Caesar's offense/defense buffs create excellent synergy.
- **Mehmed II** — strong secondary for rallying reinforced structures; boosts march attack, skill damage, and rallied army capacity.
- **Aethelflaed** — higher defense reduction, good for mixed-troop rally/PvP.
- **Scipio Africanus** — recommended budget pairing for players without additional Legendary commanders.

## Equipment
- Skill-damage and troop-capacity equipment for sustained siege pushes.

## Strategy Tips
- Talent build: Attack Tree (Lord of War → Burning Blood → Effortless), then Leadership Tree (Strategic Prowess → Fresh Recruits → Armored to the Teeth).
- Field all three troop types to trigger his mixed-army damage passive — running a single troop type wastes part of his kit.

## Strengths
- Strong garrison-attacking healing/damage kit; rewards flexible mixed-troop rosters.

## Weaknesses
- Less effective if forced into a single-troop-type comp, since his signature passive requires mixed troops.
