# Gajah Mada

## Overview
Gajah Mada, community-described as Indonesia's first RoK commander, is a Legendary Engineering commander using Siege units for ranged attacks, distinguished by an unusually large health buff that makes his march far tankier than typical siege comps.

## Statistics
> Verify exact numbers in-game.

- Specialties: Versatility, Defense, Engineering
- Troop type: Siege

## Skills
- **Passive (2nd skill):** +40% health — an exceptionally large buff, making his march unusually tanky for a siege-based commander.
- **Passive (4th skill):** Applies a 15% march-speed debuff to enemies for 3 seconds; for the next 5 seconds, his troops deal +10% ranged normal damage, and each ranged basic attack deals extra smite damage to the target.

## Recommended Usage
Ranged siege commander with strong survivability, suited for both ranged poking and unexpected melee engagement.

## Best Pairings
- **Gonzalo de Córdoba** — best pairing; combines long-range damage with Gajah Mada's staying power in melee if ambushed.
- **Margaret** — decent fallback if Gonzalo de Córdoba is unavailable, though not standout.

## Equipment
- Engineering/siege equipment sets.

## Strategy Tips
- Engineering talent priorities: Superior Engineering, Battering Ram, Siege Mastery, Ruthless Assault, Structural Weakness, Destruction Specialist, Fiery Engineering, Winds of Fury, Expert Construction.

## Strengths
- Unusually tanky for a siege/ranged commander thanks to the large health passive.

## Weaknesses
- Siege units generally deal less effective damage in normal open-field combat versus dedicated Cavalry/Archer/Infantry commanders.
