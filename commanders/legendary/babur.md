# Babur

## Overview
Babur is a Legendary Siege/Engineering commander built for ranged combat, with a kit almost entirely reliant on running pure-Siege troop compositions.

## Statistics
> Verify exact numbers in-game.

- Specialties: Engineering, Attack, Versatility
- Troop type: Siege

## Skills
- **Passive — Baburnama:** Siege unit attack/defense increased; ranged normal attack damage increased.
- **Passive — Tiger's Roar:** In ranged mode, reduced damage from normal attacks; ranged normal attacks have a chance to deal additional skill damage (triggerable every 5s).
- **Passive — Stoneshaker:** Increased Siege unit health; taking damage has a chance to grant increased defense/damage for 3 seconds (triggerable every 5s).
- **Expertise — Amir of Fergana:** Increases Siege attack; using an active skill grants rage per second for 3 seconds.

## Recommended Usage
Dedicated ranged Siege-unit commander; run only with Siege troops.

## Best Pairings
- **Margaret** — extremely strong synergy; either commander can serve as primary.

## Equipment
- Siege/Engineering equipment sets.

## Strategy Tips
- Like Margaret, his buffs only apply to Siege units — do not run mixed troop types.

## Strengths
- Solid ranged damage/defense/rage kit specifically for Siege-unit marches.

## Weaknesses
- Entirely dependent on pure-Siege composition; low flexibility outside that niche.
