# Subutai

## Overview
Subutai is a Legendary Cavalry/Conquering/Combo commander with reportedly the highest single-target DPS in the game, built around a "combo attack" mechanic and strong rally-specific buffs. Community sources note there is currently no strong use case for him as primary — he's used almost exclusively as secondary.

## Statistics
> Verify exact numbers in-game.

- Specialties: Cavalry, Conquering, Combo
- Troop type: Cavalry

## Skills
- **Passive:** +15% Cavalry march speed; while leading a rally, rallied Cavalry gain +20% attack and +30% defense.
- **Passive:** Combo attacks have a ~60% chance to gain +15% normal damage for 8 seconds.
- **Active:** When attacking a march on the map, inflicts Burn on the target for 2 seconds.

## Recommended Usage
Secondary commander for Cavalry rallies, valued for extremely high single-target damage output when paired with a rage-control primary.

## Best Pairings
- **Bertrand du Guesclin** — strong pairing maximizing rage regeneration and uptime.
- **King Arthur** — Arthur's rage control ensures Subutai's skills trigger more often, critical given how much damage those skills deal.

## Equipment
- Rage-generation and Cavalry-attack equipment sets.

## Strategy Tips
- Since there's currently no strong primary use case, talent tree investment is less critical — focus resources on his partner's talent tree instead.

## Strengths
- Reportedly the highest single-target DPS in the game when paired correctly.

## Weaknesses
- No established strong primary role; entirely dependent on a rage-control partner to shine.
