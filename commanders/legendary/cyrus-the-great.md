# Cyrus the Great

## Overview
Cyrus the Great is a Legendary Archer/Versatility/Attack commander, per community sources gated behind kingdoms open 310+ days, offering strong single-target damage plus a damage-over-time effect that also suppresses the target's skill damage output.

## Statistics
> Verify exact numbers in-game — figures below are community-reported.

- Specialties: Archer, Versatility, Attack
- Troop type: Archer

## Skills
- **Active — The Immortals:** Damage factor ~1400 to current target; target takes +20% additional damage for 3 seconds.
- **Passive — Battle of Thymbra:** +15% march speed, +30% attack for Archer units — useful for both offense and disengaging.
- **Passive — Persian Bow:** With a pure-Archer army, normal attacks have a ~10% chance to deal damage-over-time (damage factor ~250) for 3 seconds; while active, the target deals 40% reduced skill damage.

## Recommended Usage
Archer primary for open-field and rallies where suppressing enemy skill damage output matters.

## Best Pairings
- **Nebuchadnezzar II** — natural pairing combining defensive stats with powerful nuking damage.
- **Yi Seong-Gye**, **Boudica Prime**, **Gilgamesh** — viable for various roles.
- **Ramesses II** — good synergy; both boost each other's active skills for high combined damage.

## Equipment
- Skill-damage and archer-attack equipment sets.

## Strategy Tips
- Talent build: Skill Build (maximizes rage/damage) generally recommended over the Archery Build (more survivability) since the extra damage from the Skill Build is substantial.
- Run pure-Archer troops to unlock Persian Bow's full effect.

## Strengths
- Strong damage plus a valuable skill-damage-suppression debuff on top.

## Weaknesses
- Late-game gating (per community reports) limits availability in newer kingdoms.
