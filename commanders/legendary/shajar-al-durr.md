# Shajar al-Durr

## Overview
Shajar al-Durr is a Legendary Archer/Versatility/Skill commander whose active skill behaves differently depending on whether she is primary or secondary — high direct damage as primary, or a heal-to-true-damage conversion (similar to Choe Yeong) as secondary.

## Statistics
> Verify exact numbers in-game — figures below are community-reported.

- Specialties: Archer, Versatility, Skill
- Troop type: Archer

## Skills
- **Active (as primary):** Direct damage (damage factor ~2800) to a target troop.
- **Active (as secondary):** For 10 seconds, whenever she heals a target, also deals 80% of the heal strength as true damage to a target troop.
- **Passive:** +35% Archer health, +20% march speed.

## Recommended Usage
Best used as a **secondary** commander for open-field Archer marches, per community consensus, to access her true-damage heal conversion.

## Best Pairings
- **Zhuge Liang** — the top Archer commander overall; works well with virtually any Archer partner including Shajar.
- **Choe Yeong** — active skills sync perfectly, though Choe Yeong's garrison focus makes the pairing less ideal for open field.
- **Ashurbanipal** — a reasonable alternative pairing.

## Equipment
- Skill-damage and healing-focused Archer equipment sets.

## Strategy Tips
- Her kit specifically supports healing and enemy march-speed debuffing — well suited to sustained open-field Archer play.

## Strengths
- Flexible primary/secondary active skill; true-damage conversion bypasses enemy defense when secondary.

## Weaknesses
- Full value requires being run as secondary — less optimal as a standalone primary commander.
