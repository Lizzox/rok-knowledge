# Shapur

## Overview
Shapur is a Legendary Archer/Conquering commander built for long rallies, dealing damage over time plus a debuff that reduces enemy strength and, under certain conditions, prevents shields — encouraging prolonged fights that favor his team.

## Statistics
> Verify exact percentages in-game.

- Specialties: Archer, Conquering
- Troop type: Archer

## Skills
- **Passive:** +20% Archer march speed, +20% Archer health; when taking damage, ~25% chance to gain +30% attack for 3 seconds; while leading a rally, an extra +20% Archer march speed.
- **Active/passive:** Deals additional damage to a target troop each second for 4 seconds; if the target has less than 500 rage, it also cannot receive shield effects for 2 seconds.

## Recommended Usage
Rally-leading Archer commander for long, sustained sieges where his damage-over-time and anti-shield mechanics compound over time.

## Best Pairings
- **Ashurbanipal** — the meta pairing; their skills interact especially well with each other.

## Equipment
- Skill-damage and rally-durability equipment sets.

## Strategy Tips
- Conquering talent priorities: Moment of Triumph, Marionette, Entrenched, Signal Flare, Meteor Shower.

## Strengths
- Strong sustained damage-over-time output plus a valuable anti-shield mechanic against low-rage targets.

## Weaknesses
- Value is realized over long rallies; less impactful in short skirmishes.
