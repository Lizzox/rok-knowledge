# Minamoto no Yoshitsune

## Overview
Minamoto no Yoshitsune is a Legendary Cavalry/Peacekeeping/Skill commander, frequently recommended as one of the best Cavalry commanders for new players thanks to high single-target damage and strong barbarian-farming utility.

## Statistics
> Verify exact numbers in-game — figures below are community-reported.

- Specialties: Cavalry, Peacekeeping, Skill
- Troop type: Cavalry

## Skills
- **Active — Kyohachiryu:** Direct damage (damage factor ~1400) plus a 50% chance to deal additional damage (damage factor ~600) each second for 2 more seconds — one of the most powerful damage skills in the game.
- **Passive:** +10% march speed and +20% attack for Cavalry units.
- **Passive — Master of Kendo:** +50% damage against barbarians.

## Recommended Usage
Strong early-to-mid game Cavalry primary for both PvP nuking and barbarian/fort farming.

## Best Pairings
- **Cao Cao** — one of the best nuke pairs in the game.
- **Boudica** (Minamoto primary, Boudica secondary) — top duo specifically for barbarian fort clearing.
- **Yi Seong-Gye** — strong early-game damage-dealing march.
- **Genghis Khan** — considered a "golden pairing" combining the game's top Cavalry nuke damage.

## Equipment
- Skill-damage and cavalry-attack gear for PvP; barbarian-damage-boosting gear for PvE farming.

## Strategy Tips
- Barbarian/fort farming talent build: max Peacekeeping branch first, then Rejuvenate in the Skill branch.
- PvP talent build: prioritize Feral Nature in the Skill branch first, then Emblazoned Shield and Undying Fury in the Cavalry branch.

## Strengths
- Extremely strong single-target damage; excellent versus barbarians specifically due to Master of Kendo.

## Weaknesses
- Outpaced by top-tier PvP-specialized Legendaries at the very highest competitive level.
