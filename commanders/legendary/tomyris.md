# Tomyris

## Overview
Tomyris is a Legendary Archer/Attack/Conquering commander rated among the best Archer nukers, unique for a poison-stacking damage mechanic where her active skill deals modest direct damage but escalating poison damage per stack.

## Statistics
> Verify exact numbers in-game — figures below are community-reported.

- Specialties: Archer, Attack, Conquering
- Troop type: Archer

## Skills
- **Active — Arrow of Vengeance:** Low direct damage, but applies a stacking poison effect; poison damage multiplies (community-reported "x20") and grows with additional stacks.

## Recommended Usage
Archer primary for prolonged fights and rallying objectives where poison stacks have time to accumulate.

## Best Pairings
- **Edward of Woodstock** — undisputed best pairing per community consensus; Edward's kit amplifies and complements the poison mechanic.
- **El Cid** — viable Archer synergy pairing.
- **Frederick** — adds durability without conflicting mechanics.
- **Yi Seong-Gye** — with YSG's bonus damage, Tomyris can reportedly deal up to ~3,300 damage per active skill use to a single target.

## Equipment
- Skill-damage and archer-attack equipment; anything extending fight duration benefits her poison-stacking mechanic.

## Strategy Tips
- Talent priorities: Archer Tree for unit damage/attack speed, Attack Tree for overall damage/rage generation, Conquering Tree for city-attack damage and reduced counterattack damage.
- Her poison mechanic rewards sustained engagements over instant burst trades.

## Strengths
- Unique, escalating poison damage that punishes prolonged fights; very strong with the right amplifying pairing.

## Weaknesses
- Weaker in short, decisive skirmishes where poison stacks don't have time to build up.
