# Sun Tzu Prime

## Overview
Sun Tzu Prime is the Legendary-tier version of the long-running Epic support commander Sun Tzu, retaining the archetype's signature area damage, rage generation, and defensive utility while adding stronger scaling appropriate to Legendary rarity. "Prime" commanders in RoK are generally significantly stronger reworks of an existing Epic commander, obtained separately from the base version.

## Statistics
> Verify exact percentages/rage costs in-game — Prime commander kits are periodically rebalanced.

- Specialties: Leadership, Skill
- Troop type: Leadership

## Skills
- **Active (unlock 1★):** AoE skill damage to enemies.
- **Passive (unlock 2★):** Reduces damage taken by the march and boosts health.
- **Passive (unlock 3★):** Increases rage generation for the march.
- **Passive (unlock 4★):** Increases skill damage further.
- **Mastery skill (5★):** Additional support/damage scaling.

## Recommended Usage
Elite rally-leader pick combining damage reduction, rage generation, and AoE damage — one of the strongest all-round rally commanders when available.

## Best Pairings
- Paired with high-burst Cavalry or Archer secondaries to capitalize on the extra rage generation.

## Alternative Pairings
- Base Sun Tzu (Epic) can substitute in the same archetype role for players without Sun Tzu Prime.

## Equipment
- Rage-generation and defensive equipment sets.

## Strategy Tips
- If you already run base Sun Tzu, prioritize other Legendary slots first unless Sun Tzu Prime offers a clear power jump for your specific rally role.

## Strengths
- Extremely well-rounded: damage, defense, and rage generation in one kit.

## Weaknesses
- Resource-intensive to fully build given it's a newer/Prime-tier Legendary.
