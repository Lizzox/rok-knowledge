# Hermann Prime

## Overview
Hermann Prime is the Legendary "Prime" rework of the Epic commander [[hermann]], built around AoE poison-stacking damage. Community sources note he is extremely strong in open engagements against multiple targets but explicitly weak in 1v1 duels.

## Statistics
> Verify exact numbers in-game.

- Specialties: Archer, Versatility, Support
- Troop type: Archer

## Skills
- **Active:** Direct damage to up to 3 enemy troops in a fan area.
- **Passive:** Basic attacks have a ~10% chance to deal extra direct damage and inflict 2 stacks of Poison for 10 seconds.

## Recommended Usage
Open-field Archer primary/secondary for multi-target engagements (group fights, rallies); avoid using him as a dedicated 1v1 duelist.

## Best Pairings
- **Zhuge Liang** — top-rated pairing; both AoE kits complement each other exceptionally well.
- **Yi Seong-Gye** — one of the best open-field pairings; YSG's rage generation lets Hermann Prime use skills more often for huge combined AoE damage.
- **Ashurbanipal** — strong pairing either direction, trading damage mitigation for damage output depending on which is primary.
- **Boudica Prime** — adds survivability; even though her kit doesn't directly synergize with his AoE, her Archer buffs are valuable.

## Equipment
- Skill-damage and archer-attack equipment; rage-generation gear to maximize skill uptime.

## Strategy Tips
- Talent build: Archer Tree combined with Support Tree, prioritizing Rejuvenate (Support) and Razor Sharp (Archer) for rage regeneration.

## Strengths
- Excellent multi-target AoE poison damage; very strong in genuine open-field group combat.

## Weaknesses
- Explicitly weak in 1v1 duels — situational depending on fight type.
