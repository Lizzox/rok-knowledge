# Justinian I

## Overview
Justinian I is a Legendary Cavalry/Conquering/Support commander whose kit specifically enhances rally-leading and combat effects tied to "surrounded" mechanics, alongside solid Cavalry stat buffs.

## Statistics
> Verify exact percentages in-game.

- Specialties: Cavalry, Conquering, Support
- Troop type: Cavalry

## Skills
- **Active/passive (rally-specific):** As rally leader, reduces target defense by 30%; adjusts "surrounded" troop-count calculations in his favor for related effects.
- **Passive:** +30% Cavalry defense; outside alliance territory, Cavalry units deal +5% damage and gain +15% march speed.
- **Passive:** +20% Cavalry health, +10% Cavalry attack (map-wide).

## Recommended Usage
Cavalry rally leader/open-field primary, particularly effective when leading rallies due to the built-in defense-shred.

## Best Pairings
- **Alexander Nevsky** (Nevsky primary, Justinian secondary) — rated one of the most consistent Cavalry rally pairings; Nevsky supplies skill damage/rage generation while Justinian adds survivability and sustained damage output.

## Equipment
- Cavalry-attack and rage-generation equipment sets.

## Strategy Tips
- Full Cavalry marches are effectively mandatory to benefit from his stacked buffs — mixed comps waste much of his kit.
- Talent build should focus on rage generation and Cavalry buffs regardless of rally vs. open-field usage.

## Strengths
- Strong rally-specific defense-shred; solid all-around Cavalry stat buffs.

## Weaknesses
- Full value requires pure-Cavalry composition and, ideally, a rally-leading role.
