# Margaret

## Overview
Margaret is a Legendary Siege/Engineering commander whose kit is built entirely around ranged Siege-unit combat — her buffs apply only to Siege units, making a pure-Siege composition mandatory.

## Statistics
> Verify exact percentages in-game.

- Specialties: Engineering, Support, Versatility
- Troop type: Siege

## Skills
- **Passive:** For 3 seconds, ranged normal attacks deal +20% extra damage plus additional skill damage.
- **Passive:** +5% ranged skill damage while in ranged mode.
- **Passive:** Skill-damage hits have a ~10% chance to reduce target march speed by 10% for 3 seconds (triggerable once every 5 seconds).
- **Passive:** +10% Siege unit defense; when taking damage, ~10% chance to self-heal.

## Recommended Usage
Dedicated ranged Siege-unit commander; must be run with only Siege units to benefit from her kit.

## Best Pairings
- **Babur** — the recommended secondary; synergy is described as excellent.

## Equipment
- Siege/Engineering equipment sets exclusively.

## Strategy Tips
- Do not mix troop types — Margaret's buffs specifically require Siege units to activate.

## Strengths
- Strong ranged skill-damage kit with a useful march-speed debuff and self-sustain.

## Weaknesses
- Entirely dependent on a pure-Siege composition; inflexible for mixed-troop strategies.
