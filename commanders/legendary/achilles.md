# Achilles

## Overview
Achilles is a Legendary Cavalry/Versatility/Combo commander built around AoE combo attacks with a self-stacking debuff on enemies that increases their damage taken from his signature skill.

## Statistics
> Verify exact numbers in-game.

- Specialties: Cavalry, Versatility, Combo
- Troop type: Cavalry

## Skills
- **Active:** Launches 2 combo attacks against up to 5 nearby enemy troops (damage factor ~1000); targets lose 20% march speed for 3 seconds. Applies "Sundered," a stacking debuff (+5% damage taken from Pelean Spear per stack, up to 3 stacks).
- **Passive:** +40% Cavalry attack, +20% march speed; outside alliance territory, +10% health.

## Recommended Usage
Cavalry primary for open-field and KvK, especially strong versus grouped/multiple enemies due to AoE combo damage.

## Best Pairings
- **Arthur Pendragon** — best pairing per community consensus.
- **Philip II** — versatile partner boosting stats/damage for almost every troop type.
- **Gang Gamchan** — Achilles' AoE combo triggers Gang Gamchan's healing mechanic for a durable, high-damage duo.

## Equipment
- Cavalry-attack and Combo-focused equipment sets.

## Strategy Tips
- Talent trees: Combo and Cavalry.
- Skill leveling priority: max the first active skill first; aim to fully Expertise Achilles (5-5-5-5 setup) for best results.

## Strengths
- Strong AoE combo damage with a valuable stacking damage-taken debuff on enemies.

## Weaknesses
- Full potential requires Expertise investment; less impactful without it.
