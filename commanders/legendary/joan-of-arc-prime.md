# Joan of Arc Prime

## Overview
Joan of Arc Prime is the Legendary "Prime" rework of the Epic commander [[joan-of-arc]], offering a march-wide stat/rage buff kit that makes her an excellent secondary for other high skill-damage Cavalry commanders.

## Statistics
> Verify exact numbers in-game.

- Specialties: Cavalry, Support, Versatility
- Troop type: Cavalry

## Skills
- **Active — Divine Revelation:** Deals skill damage to up to 3 targets in a forward fan area; her troop and 2 surrounding friendly troops gain +1% damage for 3 seconds and +3 rage per second.

## Recommended Usage
Cavalry secondary for pure-Cavalry marches, particularly those leaning on high skill-damage output.

## Best Pairings
- **Alexander Nevsky** and **Xiang Yu** — rated the best overall pairings; with Xiang Yu, Joan of Arc Prime typically runs as secondary since his talent tree is stronger.
- **Huo Qubing** — newer strong pairing; his kit reduces active-skill rage requirements by up to ~150, synergizing with her rage-generation buff.
- **Charles Martel** — adds defense/survivability.
- **Saladin** — adds cavalry power and versatility.
- **Alexander the Great** — boosts attack and skill damage.
- **Aethelflaed** — potent support march with enemy stat reduction.

## Equipment
- Cavalry-attack and rage-generation equipment sets.

## Strategy Tips
- Talent build: Cavalry and Support trees, starting with 2 points in Rejuvenate (Support), then Emergency Protection to reduce incoming skill damage.
- Run pure-Cavalry marches to maximize her buff synergy with other Cavalry skill-damage dealers.

## Strengths
- Strong rage-generation and stat-buff support kit; flexible across many top Cavalry pairings.

## Weaknesses
- Best used as secondary rather than primary in most top pairings, given generally stronger primary talent trees among her partners.
