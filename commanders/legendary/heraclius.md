# Heraclius

## Overview
Heraclius is a Legendary Garrison/Leadership commander specializing in garrison defense, damage reduction, shields, and counterattacks, forming (with Zenobia) the current top defensive meta pairing for protecting cities and alliance structures.

## Statistics
> Verify exact numbers in-game.

- Specialties: Garrison, Leadership
- Troop type: Leadership (garrison-focused)

## Skills
- **Active:** Direct damage to up to 5 nearby target troops (15% reduced damage per additional target); grants his troop a Mighty Shield for 5 seconds.
- **Passive:** +10% health; as garrison commander, normal attacks have a ~30% chance to increase counterattack damage by 10% for 3 seconds.
- **Passive:** +10% skill damage; using an active skill grants a Mighty Shield for 3 seconds (triggerable once every 8 seconds).

## Recommended Usage
Premier garrison defender for city and alliance-structure protection, especially during KvK.

## Best Pairings
- **Zenobia** — the strongest defensive meta pairing per community consensus; top choice for city/structure protection in KvK.
- **Flavius Aetius** and **Charles Martel** — also cited as strong synergy partners for meta defensive setups.
- **Dido** — Heraclius as secondary forms a top anti-swarm archer-garrison configuration.

## Equipment
- Defense and garrison-focused equipment sets.

## Strategy Tips
- Talent priority: rush Impregnable, then Divine Favor, to reduce damage taken while boosting Heraclius's own damage output.

## Strengths
- Elite garrison/structure defense with strong shield and counterattack synergy.

## Weaknesses
- Narrow role — limited value outside garrison/structure defense contexts.
