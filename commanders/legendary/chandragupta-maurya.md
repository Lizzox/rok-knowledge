# Chandragupta Maurya

## Overview
Chandragupta Maurya is a Legendary Cavalry/Skill commander whose kit combines a strong march-wide damage buff with a stacking debuff ("Exhaust") that erodes enemy health and defense over time.

## Statistics
> Verify exact percentages in-game.

- Specialties: Cavalry, Skill
- Troop type: Cavalry

## Skills
- **Active — Hidden Moon:** Increases the damage of all troops under his command by 40% for 3 seconds, and grants 1 stack of "Blessed" (+15% Cavalry attack per stack for 10s, stacking up to 3 times).
- **Passive — Arthashastra:** When all troops are Cavalry, +20% health; each attack has a 50% chance to apply "Exhaust" to the target, reducing their health and defense by 5% (stacking debuff).

## Recommended Usage
Cavalry rally/open-field primary, particularly strong in prolonged fights where the Exhaust debuff can stack multiple times.

## Best Pairings
- **Takeda Shingen** (Chandragupta primary) — Takeda's debuffs and defensive stats (cavalry defense, healing) round out Chandragupta's offense.
- **Genghis Khan** (Khan primary) — Khan supplies damage, rage regeneration, and cavalry attack that complement Chandragupta.
- **Attila** (Attila primary) — maximizes cavalry attack and conquering output via stacking passives.
- **Saladin** — recommended when the priority is fast stronghold destruction via Saladin's high single-target damage.

## Equipment
- Skill-damage and cavalry-attack equipment; rage-generation gear to cycle Hidden Moon more often.

## Strategy Tips
- Start talent investment in the Skill Tree for Rejuvenate (extra rage) before committing fully to the Cavalry Tree, to cycle his active skill faster.

## Strengths
- Strong self-buff plus a stacking shred debuff that compounds in longer engagements.

## Weaknesses
- Requires a pure-Cavalry composition to unlock his full passive value.
