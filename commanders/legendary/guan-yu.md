# Guan Yu

## Overview
Guan Yu is a Legendary Infantry/Skill commander and one of the classic tank picks in RoK, complementing Richard I and Charles Martel in the long-standing "Infantry tank trio" of garrison/open-field defense.

## Statistics
> Verify exact percentages/rage costs in-game.

- Specialties: Infantry, Skill
- Troop type: Infantry

## Skills
- **Active (unlock 1★):** Deals skill damage and applies a control effect (e.g., stun/slow) to enemies.
- **Passive (unlock 2★):** Increases defense.
- **Passive (unlock 3★):** Increases health / reduces damage taken.
- **Passive (unlock 4★):** Additional defensive or counter effect.
- **Mastery skill (5★):** Further tankiness amplification.

## Recommended Usage
Garrison defense and Infantry open-field tanking; strong pick for players building an early Infantry-focused Legendary roster.

## Best Pairings
- Paired with Richard I or Charles Martel for a maximally durable Infantry garrison march.

## Alternative Pairings
- Paired with a Skill-damage secondary to add offensive presence to an otherwise defensive march.

## Equipment
- Defense/health equipment sets.

## Strategy Tips
- Because Infantry tanks share overlapping roles, most players only need one or two of Guan Yu/Richard I/Charles Martel rather than all three — pick based on which sculptures/shards you can obtain first.

## Strengths
- High durability, control effects useful in both PvE (barbarian forts) and PvP garrison defense.

## Weaknesses
- Low damage ceiling; redundant if you already run another top Infantry tank.
