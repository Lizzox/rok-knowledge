# Pepin III

## Overview
Pepin III is a Legendary Integration/Gathering/Support commander, a dedicated gathering specialist offering shield and skill-damage-reduction utility on top of resource bonuses.

## Statistics
> Verify exact numbers in-game.

- Specialties: Integration, Gathering, Support
- Troop type: Mixed (gathering-focused)

## Skills
- **Active/passive:** Grants a damage-absorbing shield (damage factor up to ~1200) for 3 seconds; for the following 3 seconds, troops take up to 25% less skill damage (scaling with skill level).

## Recommended Usage
Gathering march commander, functioning best as a secondary alongside another gathering specialist.

## Best Pairings
- Any other gathering commander (e.g., **Joan of Arc**).

## Equipment
- Gathering-speed and defensive equipment sets.

## Strategy Tips
- F2P players should not invest universal commander sculptures in Pepin III — reserve that investment for meta open-field/combat commanders. He's best suited to whale accounts or dedicated farm accounts.

## Strengths
- Useful bonus-resource gathering plus a defensive shield/skill-damage-reduction utility.

## Weaknesses
- Low priority for F2P sculpture investment given minimal combat value.
