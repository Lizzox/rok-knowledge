# Trajan

## Overview
Trajan is a Legendary Leadership/Support commander described by community sources as one of the best support commanders for open-field combat, offering rage generation, a defense/health passive, and mixed-troop capacity scaling.

## Statistics
> Verify exact numbers in-game.

- Specialties: Leadership, Support
- Troop type: Leadership (mixed-troop capable)

## Skills
- **Active — Trajan's Column:** Strong skill damage and rage bonus for Trajan and nearby friendly troops; applies a debuff making the target take +25% damage.
- **Passive:** +20% defense (max level); +20% health outside alliance territory — useful for open-field survivability.
- **Passive — Great Expeditions:** +10% troop capacity, benefiting mixed-troop compositions.

## Recommended Usage
Support secondary/primary for open-field combat, particularly strong alongside partners whose 4th skill counts as an "active skill" to trigger his rage-focused talents.

## Best Pairings
- **Harald Sigurdsson** — "dream team" pairing; Harald's 4th skill counts as an active skill, triggering most of Trajan's talents for very high synergy.
- **Alexander the Great** — described as an "undefeatable" combination together.
- **Constantine I** — solid alternative if Alexander is unavailable, slightly weaker synergy but still strong.

## Equipment
- Rage-generation and support equipment sets.

## Strategy Tips
- Talent priority: Rejuvenate in the Support Tree first, granting ~150 extra rage each time Trajan uses his primary skill/applies his buff, accelerating skill cycling further.

## Strengths
- Strong rage-generation support kit with a valuable vulnerability debuff and troop-capacity bonus.

## Weaknesses
- Best value depends on pairing with a partner whose skills specifically synergize with his talent triggers (e.g., Harald Sigurdsson).
