# Edward of Woodstock

## Overview
Edward of Woodstock is a Legendary Archer/Versatility/Skill commander from Britain, widely rated as one of the strongest archer primaries in the game, with 3 of his 4 skills directly benefiting Archer units.

## Statistics
> Verify exact percentages/damage in-game.

- Specialties: Archer, Versatility, Skill
- Troop type: Archer

## Skills
- **Active — Archer's Honor:** Very high single-target damage, among the most dominant single-target active skills in the game.
- **Passive:** +30% health for Archer units, giving him unusual tankiness for an Archer commander.
- **Passive:** +25% skill damage and +5% damage against Infantry units — a hard counter to Infantry-heavy comps.
- Additional passive further reinforcing Archer-only march composition (full Archer marches recommended; mixed comps waste his kit).

## Recommended Usage
Primary commander for full-Archer open-field and rally marches; used as primary in the vast majority of cases due to his talent tree strength.

## Best Pairings
- **Tomyris** — community-rated best overall pairing for Edward.
- **Yi Seong-Gye** — boosts Edward's active skill damage by a further ~50% and adds rage restoration.

## Alternative Pairings
- Other Archer-amplifying secondaries as Tomyris/YSG become available.

## Equipment
- Skill-damage and archer-attack equipment sets; his own passives already cover health, so pure offense gear is favored.

## Strategy Tips
- Run 100% Archer troops — his passives are wasted on mixed comps.
- Excellent versus Infantry-heavy garrisons/rallies; weaker against well-piloted Cavalry counter-rushes.

## Strengths
- Among the highest single-target damage output of any Legendary commander.
- Unusual Archer durability thanks to the health passive.

## Weaknesses
- Full commitment to Archer troops required; no flexibility for mixed marches.
