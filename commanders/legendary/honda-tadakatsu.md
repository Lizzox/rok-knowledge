# Honda Tadakatsu

## Overview
Honda Tadakatsu is a Legendary Leadership/Skill commander (nicknamed "The Deer of Tokugawa") whose damage output and value increase as his troops take losses — a comeback-style mechanic that also scales with mixed-troop-type compositions.

## Statistics
> Verify exact numbers in-game.

- Specialties: Leadership, Skill
- Troop type: Leadership (mixed-troop capable)

## Skills
- **Active:** Damage factor ~1500 to up to 3 targets in a forward fan (750 if used as secondary commander); damage reduced 15% per additional target.
- **Passive:** +2% march speed, +3% attack (+10% vs. troops).
- **Passive:** -1% damage taken.
- **Passive (4th skill):** +2% troop capacity; deals +1% (or +5% with 2+ troop types) increased skill damage per 8% of units lost — rewards fighting through losses, especially with mixed troops.

## Recommended Usage
Strong secondary commander, especially for Xiang Yu or Scipio-led marches; also viable as primary for prolonged attritional fights.

## Best Pairings
- **Xiang Yu** (Xiang Yu primary) — Honda adds DPS, movement speed, and troop capacity as secondary.
- **Tomyris** (Tomyris secondary) — her stacking poison further increases Honda's active skill damage.
- **Harald Sigurdsson** (Harald primary) — combined with Honda's Rejuvenate talent, grants extra rage per skill use, accelerating his high-damage active skill.
- **Scipio** (Honda secondary) — damage reduction, march-speed boost for Infantry, and extra march capacity.

## Equipment
- Skill-damage and rage-generation equipment sets.

## Strategy Tips
- Talent priority: Rejuvenate (60 rage restored per skill use), then Clarity (+6% skill damage for 6s after active skill), combined with Tactical Mastery for a further skill-damage bonus.

## Strengths
- Damage scales up as troops are lost, especially in mixed-troop marches; flexible as both primary and secondary.

## Weaknesses
- Best value is realized in prolonged fights or as a secondary — less impactful in short, decisive skirmishes.
