# Harald Sigurdsson

## Overview
Harald Sigurdsson is a Legendary Infantry/Conquering/Skill commander from the Vikings, distinguished by counterattack-focused passives — he punishes enemies who attack him, especially when surrounded.

## Statistics
> Verify exact percentages in-game.

- Specialties: Infantry, Conquering, Skill
- Troop type: Infantry

## Skills
- **Active — Berserker:** Strong direct damage, hitting up to 3 targets in AoE when surrounded; also increases all his troops' damage by 20% for 2 seconds.
- **Passive:** Infantry units gain +30% attack and +10% march speed.
- **Passive:** ~20% chance, on normal attack, to re-trigger his active skill and grant troops immunity to defense-reduction effects for 3 seconds (triggerable once every 5 seconds).
- **Passive:** Counterattack damage on the map is increased 20%, further increased when surrounded (+2% per surrounding enemy, up to 10 enemies).

## Recommended Usage
Open-field Infantry brawler that rewards being surrounded/engaged by multiple enemies rather than avoiding it.

## Best Pairings
- **Guan Yu** — one of the best Infantry nukers in the game; supplies extra damage output and a healing skill Harald's kit otherwise lacks.
- **Alexander the Great** — fills Harald's defensive gaps; especially strong against non-nuking opponents.

## Equipment
- Attack and counterattack-boosting equipment sets for open-field brawling.

## Strategy Tips
- Talent build: rush "Undying Fury" for rage generation, then "Clarity" for damage, then "Strong of Body" for a health buff.
- His kit is strongest when deliberately engaging multiple enemies at once rather than 1v1 dueling.

## Strengths
- Strong counterattack scaling when surrounded; solid Infantry attack/speed buffs.

## Weaknesses
- Lacks built-in healing — relies on a pairing (e.g., Guan Yu) to cover sustain.
