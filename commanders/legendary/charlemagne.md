# Charlemagne

## Overview
Charlemagne ("Pater Europae") is a Legendary Leadership/Conquering/Skill commander from France, built specifically around leading mixed-troop rallies against enemy cities and strongholds. He can lead any troop type, making him a flexible rally-leader pick for alliances that want to combine Infantry/Cavalry/Archer into one march.

- Specialties: Leadership, Conquering, Skill
- Troop type: Leadership (mixed-troop capable)

## Skills
- **Active — Eagle of Jupiter:** Single-target skill damage; damage scales up notably when leading a rally due to total march power scaling.
- **Passive — Happy Home:** ~10% chance to grant a damage-absorbing shield; useful both attacking cities and defending garrisons.
- **Passive — Plot Maneuvers:** Increases rally damage and defense by roughly 10%; only applies when Charlemagne initiates the rally (must be the rally leader).

## Recommended Usage
Primary rally leader for city/stronghold sieges using mixed-troop compositions.

## Best Pairings
- Strong Cavalry or Archer secondary commanders to add burst damage to his rally-buff foundation.

## Alternative Pairings
- Any high skill-damage Legendary secondary benefits from his rally-wide damage/defense buff.

## Equipment
- Rally leader equipment sets (rage generation, skill damage) recommended; his shield passive already covers some survivability.

## Strategy Tips
- Only Charlemagne's own Plot Maneuvers buff triggers when he personally leads the rally — do not run him as a secondary if you want this bonus.
- Effective in Open Field, mixed-troop rallying, and Canyon combat per community recommendations.

## Strengths
- Flexible mixed-troop leadership; strong city-siege focused kit.
- Rally-wide buff scales with mixed comps rather than locking you into one troop type.

## Weaknesses
- Individually not the highest raw damage Legendary; value is tied to leading rallies specifically.
