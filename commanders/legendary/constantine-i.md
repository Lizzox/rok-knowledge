# Constantine I

## Overview
Constantine I is a Legendary Infantry/Support commander offering strong defensive utility (attack reduction, damage reduction) plus one of the largest flat heals of any commander, making him a premier garrison and open-field tank.

## Statistics
> Verify exact percentages in-game.

- Specialties: Infantry, Support
- Troop type: Infantry

## Skills
- **Active:** Reduces enemy attack by ~40% and reduces damage taken by ~10% — a strong defensive debuff/buff combo.
- **Passive:** +10% garrison/watchtower damage — useful specifically when serving as a garrison commander.
- **Passive — Cross of the Divine:** Large healing factor (community-reported ~15,000).
- **Passive — Sole Augustus:** +15% attack, +5% defense for troops under his leadership.

## Recommended Usage
Garrison defense and open-field Infantry tanking; his healing skill makes him unusually self-sufficient in prolonged fights.

## Best Pairings
- **Alexander the Great** — widely rated as the best pairing; Alexander's defense boost and attack compensate for Constantine's lower offensive output.
- **Charles Martel** — another strong defensive pairing option.
- **Sun Tzu** (Epic) — rage restoration lets Constantine use his active skill more frequently.

## Equipment
- Defense and health equipment sets for garrison use.

## Strategy Tips
- Open-field talent build: start Infantry Tree (rush Elite Soldiers, then Fleet of Foot, Strong of Body), then Support Tree for Rejuvenate (extra rage).
- His garrison-damage passive makes him specifically strong when leading a garrison rather than roaming in the open field.

## Strengths
- Exceptional sustain via large flat heal; strong defensive debuffs on enemies.

## Weaknesses
- Lower raw offensive output; needs a damage-focused pairing to be threatening rather than just durable.
