# William I

## Overview
William I is a Legendary Cavalry/Skill commander excelling in open-field battles and rallies, known for strong AoE damage paired with mobility-reducing debuffs. Per community sources, he is gated behind kingdoms open 310+ days.

## Statistics
> Verify exact percentages/gating requirements in-game.

- Specialties: Cavalry, Skill
- Troop type: Cavalry

## Skills
- **Active — Hidden Bloodline:** AoE damage hitting up to 3 targets in a forward rectangle; damage reduced 15% per additional target beyond the first. Also reduces struck troops' march speed by 10% for 3 seconds and can prevent skill buffs on affected targets.

## Recommended Usage
Cavalry primary/secondary for open-field roaming and rallies, valued for both damage and crowd control.

## Best Pairings
- **Alexander Nevsky** (Nevsky primary, William secondary) — rated one of the strongest current pairings.
- **Genghis Khan** — both are heavy nukers, dealing high damage to multiple and single targets.
- **Joan of Arc Prime** — strong synergy per community guides.

## Alternative Pairings
- Budget options: **Pelagius**, **Baibars**, **Belisarius** for players without top Legendary partners.

## Equipment
- Cavalry-attack and skill-damage equipment; march-speed gear complements his own slow debuff for chase potential.

## Strategy Tips
- Talent build should maximize damage output, mobility, and rage generation for cavalry.

## Strengths
- Strong AoE damage with built-in crowd control (slow + skill-buff prevention).

## Weaknesses
- Late-game gating (per community reports) makes him unavailable in newer kingdoms.
