# Nebuchadnezzar II

## Overview
Nebuchadnezzar II is a Legendary Archer/Skill commander built for city rallies, combining strong AoE damage with counterattack amplification and rage-denial — forming, with Gilgamesh, what community sources call the current top city-rally pairing.

## Statistics
> Verify exact numbers in-game — figures below are community-reported.

- Specialties: Archer, Skill
- Troop type: Archer

## Skills
- **Active — Babylonian:** Damage factor ~1500 to up to 5 targets in a forward fan; damage reduced 15% per additional target.
- **Passive — Blessing of Marduk:** +30% counterattack damage when leading a rally attack; incoming attacks have a ~10% chance to deal direct damage back to the attacker (5s cooldown).
- **Passive — Chaldean Archers:** +30% Archer defense, +15% march speed.
- **Passive — Destroyer of Carchemish:** +15% troop damage; normal attacks have a ~10% chance to reduce target rage by 100.

## Recommended Usage
Rally-leading Archer primary for city sieges, particularly effective against garrison defenders relying on damage reduction or healing.

## Best Pairings
- **Gilgamesh** — current top city-rally pairing; combined conquering and anti-healing mechanics remove garrison defenders' two main survival tools (damage reduction and healing).
- **Cyrus the Great** — arguably the best pairing; creates one of the fastest Archer marches in the game with strong nuking damage and survivability.
- **Yi Seong-Gye** — immense nuking power and rage generation together.

## Equipment
- Skill-damage and archer-attack equipment sets.

## Strategy Tips
- Talent priority: Rejuvenate in the Skill Tree first, then Venomous Sting in the Archer Tree for march speed; also consider Clarity and Latent Power for additional skill damage.

## Strengths
- Excellent AoE rally damage with built-in rage denial and counterattack amplification — very strong against sustain-based garrison defenses.

## Weaknesses
- Full value is realized specifically in rally-leading city-siege scenarios rather than solo open-field play.
