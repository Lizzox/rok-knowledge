# Jadwiga

## Overview
Jadwiga is a Legendary Cavalry/Garrison commander whose talent tree is effectively limited to garrisoning — community sources note that non-garrison talent builds underperform due to mobility-talent limitations while she's garrisoned.

## Statistics
> Verify exact percentages in-game.

- Specialties: Cavalry, Garrison
- Troop type: Cavalry (garrison-focused)

## Skills
- **Active — Might of the Pious:** Damage factor ~900 to current target; troops deal +5% damage for 3 seconds.
- **Passive:** +2% Cavalry defense, +5% march speed on the map; while garrisoned, +4% Cavalry defense and +4% health.

## Recommended Usage
Garrison Cavalry defender; her Impregnable talent (reducing garrison skill damage taken by ~15%) makes her particularly strong against high-skill-damage attackers.

## Best Pairings
- **Jan Žižka** (Jan primary, Jadwiga secondary) — rated the best Cavalry pairing; excellent stat synergy for normal attack damage, nuke damage, and rage generation. Jan Žižka should be primary since Jadwiga's talent tree is comparatively weak.
- **Yi Sun-Sin (YSS)** — strong for defending objectives in the current meta, making the garrison very hard to swarm.

## Equipment
- Garrison-defense equipment sets.

## Strategy Tips
- Build exclusively for garrison use — non-garrison talent builds are explicitly weaker due to her mobility-talent limitations.

## Strengths
- Strong garrison skill-damage mitigation via Impregnable; excellent anti-swarm synergy with YSS.

## Weaknesses
- Narrow, garrison-only viability; weak talent tree relative to top Cavalry partners.
