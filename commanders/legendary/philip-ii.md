# Philip II

## Overview
Philip II is a Legendary Leadership/Versatility/Support commander rated as the top support-march commander in the current meta, offering a large mixed-troop-type stat buff and a self-amplified damage/defense skill.

## Statistics
> Verify exact numbers in-game.

- Specialties: Leadership, Versatility, Support
- Troop type: Leadership (mixed-troop capable)

## Skills
- **Active:** Grants up to 5 allied troops +10% damage reduction and +20% damage bonus for 2 seconds; effects are doubled for Philip's own troop (+20% damage reduction, +40% damage bonus).
- **Passive:** +35% attack for Cavalry, +35% defense for Infantry, +35% health for Archer units under his command; +20% march speed for his troop.

## Recommended Usage
Dedicated support march commander for massive open-field battles, buffing allied troops of all types simultaneously.

## Best Pairings
- Pair with a high active-skill-damage, fast-rage-cycling commander, or another support commander for buffing allies.
- **Ragnar Lodbrok Prime** and **Liu Che** — single-troop Infantry synergy works with Philip II, with Liu Che performing even better when paired with dedicated Infantry commanders like William Wallace or Scipio Africanus Prime.

## Equipment
- Support/rage equipment sets; avoid over-investing in rage-generation talents since his kit already approaches the rage cap easily.

## Strategy Tips
- Talent priority: some Rejuvenate for extra rage (but not excessive, since the build already nears the rage cap); Formation and Armaments talents synergize well with his kit.

## Strengths
- Best-in-class mixed-troop support buffs for massive open-field engagements; doubled self-buff on his own troop.

## Weaknesses
- Provides support rather than direct damage — needs a strong damage-dealing ally to convert buffs into results.
