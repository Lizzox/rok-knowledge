# Genghis Khan

## Overview
Genghis Khan is regarded as one of the best Legendary Cavalry commanders for new kingdoms, offering the highest single-target skill damage factor of any commander per community sources. His kit is almost entirely Cavalry-focused.

## Statistics
> Verify exact numbers in-game — damage factor figure below is community-reported.

- Specialties: Cavalry, Skill
- Troop type: Cavalry

## Skills
- **Active — Chosen One:** Extremely high single-target damage, community-reported damage factor up to ~1700 — among the highest in the game.
- **Passive — Cyclone of the Steppes:** Reduces the rage cost of active skills by up to 100.

## Recommended Usage
Primary Cavalry nuker for open-field and rallies; strong pick for newer kingdoms/accounts still building a full roster.

## Best Pairings
- **Minamoto no Yoshitsune** — excellent rage engine alongside Khan.
- **Cao Cao** — generates extra rage Khan can capitalize on, boosts mobility, improves defense by reducing enemy attack, heals wounded troops.
- **Yi Seong-Gye** — rage engine plus strong skill damage amplification.

## Equipment
- Skill-damage and cavalry-attack equipment sets; rage-generation gear to loop Chosen One as often as possible.

## Strategy Tips
- Talent priorities: Rejuvenate (rage restore on skill use), Feral Nature (chance for extra rage on normal attack), Undying Fury (rage restoration from normal attacks).

## Strengths
- Among the highest single-target skill damage in the game; excellent for new/mid-tier rosters.

## Weaknesses
- Kit relies heavily on rage cycling — pairing with a poor rage-generation secondary blunts his primary strength.
