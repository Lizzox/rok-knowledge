# El Cid

## Overview
El Cid ("Holy Overlord") is a Legendary Archer/Versatility/Skill commander from Spain, notable for a disabling active skill that shuts down enemy attacks and active skills briefly — a control-oriented Archer kit rather than pure burst.

## Statistics
> Verify exact percentages/durations in-game.

- Specialties: Archer, Versatility, Skill
- Troop type: Archer

## Skills
- **Active:** Disables the enemy's normal attack and active skill for ~1 second — a disruption/control effect rather than direct damage.
- **Passive:** Archer units gain bonus defense and march speed.
- **Passive:** When troops fall below 50% strength, gain additional damage and march speed (a "desperation" comeback passive).
- **Passive:** ~10% chance for bonus damage; +5% damage to and -5% damage taken from Infantry units.

## Recommended Usage
Archer primary/secondary for open-field combat where disrupting the enemy's skill timing matters as much as raw damage.

## Best Pairings
- **Ramesses II** — Ramesses' damage and defense-reduction combine with El Cid's extra defense/march speed for a tanky, high-damage duo.
- **Frederick** and **Julius Caesar** — recommended for open-field combat pairings.

## Alternative Pairings
- Any high-burst secondary benefits from El Cid's attack-disable buying extra time to land hits safely.

## Equipment
- Archer-attack and defense equipment sets; his kit already grants some survivability via the desperation passive.

## Strategy Tips
- His comeback passive (bonus damage below 50% strength) rewards fighting through losses rather than retreating early — useful in prolonged skirmishes.

## Strengths
- Unique disable/control utility uncommon among Archer commanders.
- Strong versus Infantry due to built-in damage modifiers.

## Weaknesses
- Lower raw burst than dedicated nuker Archers like Edward of Woodstock or Aethelflaed.
