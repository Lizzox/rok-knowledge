# Leonidas I

## Overview
Leonidas I is a Legendary Infantry/Versatility/Defense commander from Greece, built around a dramatic "last stand" comeback mechanic that makes his troops deal drastically more damage as they near defeat — a strong open-field skirmisher.

## Statistics
> Verify exact numbers in-game — figures below are community-reported approximations.

- Specialties: Infantry, Versatility, Defense
- Troop type: Infantry

## Skills
- **Active — King of Sparta:** At max level, grants troops +30% health and deals damage (community-reported ~600) to 3 targets in front of him.
- **Passive — Three Hundred Spartans:** +30% health for Infantry units, +15% rage generation speed.
- **Passive:** Generates a shield absorbing ~800 damage for 3 seconds, during which troop attack is increased ~15%.
- **Passive — Son of the Lion:** +10% overall damage, stacking up to 4 times (up to +40%).
- **Passive — Battle of Thermopylae:** When troop strength falls below 10%, damage dealt increases by as much as +400% — a dramatic comeback/last-stand mechanic.

## Recommended Usage
Open-field Infantry brawler; particularly strong in prolonged fights where his last-stand passive can swing outcomes.

## Best Pairings
- **Guan Yu** (primary) + Leonidas (secondary) — Guan Yu's silence sets up a +50% damage bonus for both commanders; rated the most effective pairing.
- **Alexander the Great** — strongest defensive commander in the game per community consensus; combined with Leonidas, the pairing is very hard to beat in open field.
- **Joan of Arc** — another strong pairing option.

## Alternative Pairings
- Any Infantry-focused secondary that stacks defensive buffs further extends his last-stand viability.

## Equipment
- Defense/health equipment sets to reach the low-health threshold safely and survive there.

## Strategy Tips
- His kit rewards deliberately fighting at low health rather than retreating — talent/equipment choices that maximize survivability at the "brink" amplify Battle of Thermopylae's payoff.

## Strengths
- Extremely strong comeback potential in prolonged open-field fights.
- Strong synergy with silence-based primaries (Guan Yu).

## Weaknesses
- Comeback mechanic is situational — less valuable in short, decisive skirmishes that end before troops drop below 10% strength.
