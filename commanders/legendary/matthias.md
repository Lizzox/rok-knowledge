# Matthias

## Overview
Matthias is a Legendary Leadership/Garrison/Attack commander offering large AoE garrison damage combined with rage-triggered damage mitigation and a shield, designed specifically for defending cities.

## Statistics
> Verify exact numbers in-game — figures below are community-reported.

- Specialties: Leadership, Garrison, Attack
- Troop type: Leadership (garrison-focused)

## Skills
- **Active:** AoE damage, damage factor ~2000, hitting up to 5 targets.
- **Passive:** +40% combined base stats (~15% attack, ~15% defense, ~10% health); passive chance to re-trigger the active skill when taking normal attacks.
- **Passive:** At 70% garrison rage, takes 25% less skill damage for 4 seconds; while garrisoning a city, also gains a damage-absorbing Mighty Shield (damage factor ~800) for 4 seconds.

## Recommended Usage
Dedicated city-garrison defender, especially strong against sustained skill-damage-heavy rally attacks.

## Best Pairings
- **Lapu-Lapu** (Lapu-Lapu primary) — best pairing; excellent AoE synergy, extra anti-rally damage, and benefits from constant skill activation.
- **Wu Zetian**, **Heraclius**, **Yi Sun-Sin (YSS)**, **Amanitore** — solid alternatives if Lapu-Lapu is unavailable.

## Equipment
- Garrison-defense equipment sets.

## Strategy Tips
- His rage-threshold damage-reduction and shield mechanics reward long defensive engagements where rage naturally builds up.

## Strengths
- Strong AoE garrison damage plus meaningful damage mitigation at high rage.

## Weaknesses
- Kit is garrison-specific; limited value outside city/structure defense.
