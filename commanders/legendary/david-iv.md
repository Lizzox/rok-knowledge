# David IV

## Overview
David IV is a Legendary Cavalry commander purpose-built for garrison defense, combining combo-attack damage with a self-cleanse mechanic that removes negative status effects.

## Statistics
> Verify exact numbers in-game — figures below are community-reported.

- Specialties: Cavalry, Garrison, Combo
- Troop type: Cavalry (garrison-focused)

## Skills
- **Active:** Launches 2 combo attacks against up to 3 enemy troops in a fan (damage factor ~1400); immediately cleanses 2 instances of Slow, Poison, Exhaustion, or other normal debuffs from his troop.
- **Passive:** +35% Cavalry defense; while serving as garrison commander, garrison takes -10% skill damage.
- **Passive:** Using an active skill while attacking a troop on the map causes the target to take +40% more combo attack damage for the next 3 seconds.

## Recommended Usage
Dedicated garrison Cavalry defender, especially effective against debuff-heavy or high-skill-damage attackers thanks to his cleanse and damage-reduction kit.

## Best Pairings
- **Hector** — combo synergy adds extra damage; rated best option for garrison pairing.
- **Attila** — strong survivability together, especially against big swarms or high-skill-damage archer/attacker comps.

## Equipment
- Garrison-defense and Combo-damage equipment sets.

## Strategy Tips
- Use Delta Formation, which grants +10% combo damage — a strong fit given his reliance on combo attacks.

## Strengths
- Strong garrison defense with valuable self-cleanse and skill-damage mitigation.

## Weaknesses
- Kit is garrison-specific; not intended for open-field roaming.
