# Charles Martel

## Overview
Charles Martel is a Legendary Infantry/Skill commander frequently ranked among the strongest garrison-defense and Infantry open-field commanders, thanks to strong damage-reduction and counter-damage passives.

## Statistics
> Verify exact percentages/rage costs in-game.

- Specialties: Infantry, Skill
- Troop type: Infantry

## Skills
- **Active (unlock 1★):** Deals skill damage to enemies in range.
- **Passive (unlock 2★):** Increases defense/health.
- **Passive (unlock 3★):** Reduces damage taken and reflects a portion back to attackers.
- **Passive (unlock 4★):** Further defensive/counterattack scaling.
- **Mastery skill (5★):** Additional damage-reduction or garrison-specific bonus.

## Recommended Usage
Top-tier garrison defender; also strong for Infantry-led open-field brawling where sustained tankiness matters.

## Best Pairings
- Paired with Richard I or Guan Yu for maximum garrison durability.

## Alternative Pairings
- Paired with a skill-damage secondary for a more balanced offense/defense Infantry march.

## Equipment
- Defense and counter-damage focused equipment sets.

## Strategy Tips
- One of the most consistently recommended F2P-accessible-ish garrison Legendary picks in long-term tier lists.

## Strengths
- Exceptional garrison survivability and counter-damage value.

## Weaknesses
- Not a rally-leader pick; limited offensive burst versus Cavalry-focused commanders.
