# Cheok Jun-Gyeong

## Overview
Cheok Jun-Gyeong (CJ) is a Legendary Infantry/Versatile/Attack commander usable for both field fighting and rallying, notable for a passive that converts incoming skill damage into a counter-nuke against the attacker.

## Statistics
> Verify exact numbers in-game.

- Specialties: Infantry, Versatile, Attack
- Troop type: Infantry

## Skills
- **Active — Courageous:** Damage factor ~1700 to target; if target has <30% units remaining, deals additional damage (damage factor ~300).
- **Passive — Line Breaker:** +40% Infantry attack.
- **Passive:** When troops take skill damage, ~20% chance to reduce that damage by 50% and deal counter-damage (damage factor = stacks × 50) to up to 3 targets in a fan.

## Recommended Usage
Infantry primary/secondary for both open-field nuking and rallies; best paired with other high-damage Infantry commanders rather than pure-survivability partners.

## Best Pairings
- **Guan Yu** — combined nuking damage reportedly reaches ~4000 damage factor, plus Guan Yu's silence.
- **Alexander the Great** (Alexander primary, expertise required) — massive damage increase for Cheok's primary skill; both share nearly identical talent trees for perfect synergy.

## Equipment
- Skill-damage and infantry-attack equipment sets.

## Strategy Tips
- Talent priority: Attack Tree first — Fight to the Death (damage boost, increases damage taken), then Effortless (damage scaling with battle length), then Armored Joints (neutralizes the extra damage taken from Fight to the Death).
- Avoid pairing with pure-survivability commanders — his kit rewards nuking synergy.

## Strengths
- Very high combined nuking damage with the right Infantry partner; a built-in skill-damage counter mechanic.

## Weaknesses
- Underperforms when paired with defensive-only commanders instead of fellow nukers.
