# Artemisia I

## Overview
Artemisia I is a Legendary Archer/Garrison/Defense commander known for high fan-shaped multi-target skill damage combined with garrison-defense utility — a hybrid offense/defense Archer kit.

## Statistics
> Verify exact numbers in-game — sourced values below (e.g. "1,800 damage") are approximate community-reported figures, not officially confirmed for the current patch.

- Specialties: Archer, Garrison, Defense
- Troop type: Archer

## Skills
- **Active — Battle of Salamis:** Deals large damage (community-reported up to ~1,800) to 3 targets in a fan-shaped area; notably also deals self-damage as a tradeoff.
- **Passive — Bow and Mount:** Archer units gain +20% defense and +20% health.
- **Passive — Ally of Xerxes:** While leading garrison (own city or a stronghold), incoming normal attacks are reduced ~10%; garrison troops' normal attacks have a ~10% chance to Disarm the target for 1 second.
- **Passive — Queen of Caria:** At 80% rage, ~50% chance to Silence her own troops for 3 seconds while granting +50% damage for 5 seconds — a high-risk/high-reward damage spike with a self-silence downside.

## Recommended Usage
Garrison defense primary, or an aggressive Archer nuker when the self-silence risk is managed/paired around.

## Best Pairings
- **Yi Seong-Gye** — amplifies her already-high skill damage further.
- **Ramesses II** — adds healing, defense, and skill-damage reduction for a tankier duo.
- **Amanitore** — can clear/resist the self-silence from Queen of Caria, effectively removing Artemisia's main kit downside.

## Alternative Pairings
- Any commander with silence-resistance or cleanse utility mitigates her self-silence risk.

## Equipment
- Defense and archer-attack equipment sets for garrison use; skill-damage sets if built as an aggressive nuker.

## Strategy Tips
- Pairing with Amanitore specifically resolves her biggest kit weakness (self-silence) — prioritize this pairing if targeting max damage output.

## Strengths
- High multi-target damage output plus real garrison defensive utility in one kit.

## Weaknesses
- Self-damage on her active and self-silence risk from Queen of Caria are real drawbacks without the right pairing.
