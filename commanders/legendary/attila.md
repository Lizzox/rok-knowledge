# Attila

## Overview
Attila is a Legendary Cavalry/Skill commander recognized for strong area-of-effect skill damage, making him effective against grouped enemy marches and barbarian/fort clears in addition to PvP.

## Statistics
> Verify exact damage factors/rage costs in-game.

- Specialties: Cavalry, Skill
- Troop type: Cavalry

## Skills
- **Active (unlock 1★):** AoE skill damage to multiple enemies in range.
- **Passive (unlock 2★):** Increases skill damage.
- **Passive (unlock 3★):** Increases attack or rage generation.
- **Passive (unlock 4★):** Further AoE or single-target damage scaling.
- **Mastery skill (5★):** Damage amplification.

## Recommended Usage
Cavalry open-field and rally primary, also strong for clearing groups of barbarians due to AoE skill.

## Best Pairings
- Paired with Yi Seong-Gye for skill-damage amplification.

## Alternative Pairings
- Paired with rage-generation secondaries for more frequent skill activation.

## Equipment
- Skill-damage and cavalry-attack equipment sets.

## Strategy Tips
- A strong choice when facing large stacked enemy groups (events, barbarian camps) thanks to AoE skill damage.

## Strengths
- Strong AoE damage profile, versatile across PvE and PvP cavalry roles.

## Weaknesses
- Less specialized single-target burst than some newer Legendary cavalry commanders.
