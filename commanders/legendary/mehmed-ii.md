# Mehmed II

## Overview
Mehmed II is a Legendary Leadership/Conquering/Skill commander built around city destruction — strong AoE skill damage plus a unique rallied-army-capacity boost that increases how many troops a rally can bring to the fight.

## Statistics
> Verify exact numbers in-game — figures below are community-reported.

- Specialties: Leadership, Conquering, Skill
- Troop type: Leadership (mixed-troop capable)

## Skills
- **Active — Ottoman Cannons:** Damage factor ~1150 to up to 5 targets; when attacking a city, gains an additional ~700 damage bonus specifically against the garrison/watchtower.
- **Passive — Sage King:** Increases rallied army capacity — a unique ability not shared by most other commanders.

## Recommended Usage
Rally leader/secondary for attacking cities and reinforced structures; the rallied-capacity bonus makes him valuable purely as army-size scaling.

## Best Pairings
- **Yi Seong-Gye** (YSG primary, Mehmed secondary) — insane nuking damage output plus effectively countering swarm/mixed defenses.
- **Hannibal Barca** — another strong city-conquering commander, excellent synergy for siege pushes.
- **Alexander the Great** — covers Mehmed's lack of defense while benefiting from the increased troop capacity.

## Equipment
- Skill-damage and rally-capacity equipment sets.

## Strategy Tips
- Talent priorities: build rage regeneration (Hidden Wrath, Burning Blood, Rejuvenate), defense (Strategic Prowess, Buckler Shield), then max the Skill branch for damage.

## Strengths
- Strong AoE damage with a bonus specifically against city defenses; unique army-capacity scaling.

## Weaknesses
- Lacks personal defensive tools — needs a defensively strong pairing partner.
