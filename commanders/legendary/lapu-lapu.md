# Lapu-Lapu

## Overview
Lapu-Lapu is a Legendary Leadership/Garrison/Skill commander built specifically and narrowly for city defense — community sources are explicit that he is extremely ineffective outside of garrison defense (open field, flag garrisoning, rallying).

## Statistics
> Verify exact numbers in-game.

- Specialties: Leadership, Garrison, Skill
- Troop type: Mixed (garrison-focused)

## Skills
- **Active:** Direct damage to up to 5 nearby enemy troops, with extra direct damage if the current target is a rallied army.
- **Passive:** Defense and health bonuses when the army contains at least 3 different troop types; while serving as garrison commander, garrison takes reduced damage.

## Recommended Usage
Dedicated city-defense pick for governors who are frequently rallied against; do not build him for any other purpose.

## Best Pairings
- **Heraclius** — best pairing; both are Leadership/Garrison commanders with strong synergy.
- **Gorgo** — solid alternative if Heraclius is unavailable, though better suited to open field generally.
- Any AoE commander as a fallback secondary.

## Equipment
- Garrison-defense equipment sets exclusively.

## Strategy Tips
- Only invest in Lapu-Lapu if you specifically need anti-rally city defense — his narrow role makes him a poor general-purpose investment.

## Strengths
- Strong, focused anti-rally garrison damage and defense.

## Weaknesses
- Explicitly weak/ineffective in open field, rallying, or flag-garrisoning roles.
