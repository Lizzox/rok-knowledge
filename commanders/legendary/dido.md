# Dido

## Overview
Dido is a Legendary Archer/Garrison/Support commander, a "Ranged Garrison Support" type built specifically to counter swarm/rally attacks with high single-target damage plus rally-wide skill-damage reduction.

## Statistics
> Verify exact numbers in-game — figures below are community-reported.

- Specialties: Archer, Garrison, Support
- Troop type: Archer

## Skills
- **Active — Burning of Xinye:** Damage factor ~2000 to a target troop; if the target is a rallied army, also reduces the skill damage of up to 5 nearby enemy troops by 30% for 3 seconds.
- **Passive — Weeping Queen:** +20% Archer defense, +5% damage to Infantry; if the army is pure Archer, takes 10% less normal attack damage.
- **Passive — Queenly Zeal:** +20% Archer attack.
- **Passive (garrison-specific):** While garrisoned in a Stronghold or own city, Archer units gain +20% health and can reduce incoming skill damage by 15% once every 10 seconds.

## Recommended Usage
Premier anti-swarm garrison Archer defender, especially strong against players facing heavy rally/swarm threats.

## Best Pairings
- **Heraclius** (Dido primary, Heraclius secondary) — the archer-garrison specialist configuration; rated the strongest anti-swarm setup.
- **Zhuge Liang** — great general synergy, though not itself a garrison commander.
- **Amanitore** — Amanitore provides damage while Dido supplies anti-swarm survivability.

## Equipment
- Defense and garrison-focused Archer equipment; anti-skill-damage gear amplifies her rally-countering niche.

## Strategy Tips
- Talent priorities: Impregnable and Impenetrable Fortifications in the Garrison branch for damage reduction.

## Strengths
- One of the strongest garrison options specifically against swarm/rally-heavy attackers.

## Weaknesses
- Zhuge Liang pairing, while good generally, performs weaker specifically against swarm scenarios compared to the Heraclius pairing.
