# Stephen III

## Overview
Stephen III is a Legendary Engineering/Versatility/Skill commander leading Siege units with ranged attacks, minor healing, and minor stacking buffs — designed as a strong pairing partner for John Hunyadi.

## Statistics
> Verify exact numbers in-game — figures below are community-reported.

- Specialties: Engineering, Versatility, Skill
- Troop type: Siege

## Skills
- **Active:** ~800 ranged direct damage (~1500 if enemy is in melee range); additional damage over time (~200 for 2s, ~350 if in melee range); basic attacks grant mighty healing (~300) for 2 seconds.
- **Passive:** -15% ranged counterattack damage taken; ~10% chance to gain +25% ranged normal attack damage for 3 seconds.
- **Passive:** Ranged basic attacks have a ~40% chance to gain +2% attack but -0.5% defense (stacking up to 15 times, up to +30% attack/-7.5% defense).
- **Expertise:** +10% skill damage, boosting both his own active skill and other commanders' active skills.

## Recommended Usage
Ranged Siege commander for open-field ranged combat, best paired specifically with John Hunyadi.

## Best Pairings
- **John Hunyadi** — designed to pair with him; strong combined skill damage, sustained attacks, and durability.
- **Gajah Mada** — also cited as a strong partner.

## Equipment
- Siege/Engineering equipment sets.

## Strategy Tips
- Max all skills including the second skill to realize full pairing value with John Hunyadi.

## Strengths
- Useful ranged damage/healing hybrid with a skill-damage-boosting Expertise applicable to the whole pairing.

## Weaknesses
- Kit is heavily reliant on the John Hunyadi pairing specifically for top performance.
