# Wu Zetian

## Overview
Wu Zetian is a Legendary Leadership/Garrison/Support commander widely rated among the best garrison-defense commanders in the game, combining a healing-damage hybrid active skill with what community sources call "probably the best skill for garrison in the game."

## Statistics
> Verify exact numbers in-game.

- Specialties: Leadership, Garrison, Support
- Troop type: Leadership (garrison-focused)

## Skills
- **Active — Mandate of Heaven:** Damage factor ~1000 to a single target; heals a portion of slightly-wounded troops (healing factor ~500). Should be maxed first before her second skill.
- **Passive (3rd skill):** Extremely high garrison damage output — rated among the best garrison skills in the game.

## Recommended Usage
Primary garrison defender against enemy rallies.

## Best Pairings
- **Yi Seong-Gye** — synergizes via YSG's damage-increase and rage-regeneration bonuses plus healing.
- **Yi Sun-Sin** — both defensive kits complement each other for city defense.
- **Constantine I** — both are strong defensive commanders capable of countering nukers.

## Equipment
- Garrison-defense and damage equipment sets.

## Strategy Tips
- Talent priority within the Garrison Tree: unlock Impregnable, Empty Fortress Stratagem, Impenetrable Fortifications, and Nowhere to Turn.

## Strengths
- Top-tier garrison damage plus meaningful self-healing.

## Weaknesses
- Kit is garrison-specific; limited value in open-field or gathering roles.
