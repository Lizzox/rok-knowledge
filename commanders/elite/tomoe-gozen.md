# Tomoe Gozen

## Overview
Tomoe Gozen is an Elite commander who performs better as a secondary commander than as a primary, offering an attack buff and rage generation that pairs well with a strong nuker.

## Statistics
> Verify exact numbers in-game.

- Specialties: Archer
- Troop type: Archer

## Skills
- **Active:** Increases attack of all units for the next 3 seconds.
- **Passive:** Gains additional rage when attacking the enemy; increases skill damage specifically when serving as secondary commander.
- **Passive:** Using her active skill reduces the target's damage for 2 seconds.

## Recommended Usage
Best as a secondary commander behind a strong nuker; if used as primary (mainly viable in the very early game), run her with an Archer march.

## Best Pairings
- Any strong nuker primary — her kit is explicitly designed to boost a primary commander's damage as secondary.
- Any Archer commander if run as primary in the early game.

## Equipment
- Archer-attack and rage-generation equipment sets.

## Strategy Tips
- Always level a skill to Skill Level 5 before adding another star.
- Talent trees: Archer (Arrows Rain, Razor Sharp, Full Quiver, Venomous Sting, Phoenix-Tail Arrows) or Attack (Burning Blood, Armored Joints, Fight to the Death).

## Strengths
- Useful early secondary-commander damage/rage support for a low-rarity investment.

## Weaknesses
- Primarily an early-game commander; outclassed once Epic/Legendary options are available.
