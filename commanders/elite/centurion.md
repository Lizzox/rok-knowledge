# Centurion

## Overview
Centurion is a generalist Elite gathering commander, useful as a well-rounded early gathering pick that pairs well with more specialized gatherers.

## Statistics
> Verify exact numbers in-game.

- Specialties: Gathering
- Troop type: Mixed (gathering-focused)

## Skills
- **Active — Bloody Battle:** Direct damage to a target (minor combat utility, not the focus).
- **Passive — Excitement:** +up to 3% troop defense.
- **Passive — Army Management:** Boosts gathering speed and load capacity.

## Recommended Usage
Early-game generalist gathering commander; prioritize leveling to ~20 and maxing his third skill (Army Management) for best gathering results.

## Best Pairings
- **Constance** or **Gaius Marius** — pairing with either specialized gatherer amplifies overall gathering efficiency.

## Equipment
- Gathering-speed and load-capacity equipment sets.

## Strategy Tips
- Talent priorities: Medical Aid, The More the Better, and Superior Tools within the Gathering tree for maximum gathering speed.

## Strengths
- Solid, well-rounded early gathering generalist.

## Weaknesses
- Purely a gathering commander with no meaningful combat value.
