# Constance

## Overview
Constance is a standout Elite gathering commander, valuable not just early but throughout the mid-to-late game for food and gold gathering — both increasingly critical resources as a kingdom matures.

## Statistics
> Verify exact numbers in-game. Recommended skill levels: 5-5-5-5.

- Specialties: Gathering
- Troop type: Mixed (gathering-focused)

## Skills
- Gathering-speed skills for food and gold specifically; max all four to 5-5-5-5 for full effect.

## Recommended Usage
Long-term gathering commander; since her value is entirely gathering-based, there's little reason to level her past what's needed for gathering gear (community sources suggest star level 30–40 is sufficient, with gathering equipment covering the rest).

## Best Pairings
- **Gaius Marius** — pairs well to maximize efficiency, forming a cornerstone of early resource management.

## Equipment
- Gathering-speed and load-capacity equipment sets.

## Strategy Tips
- No need to level her to 60 like a combat commander — her value doesn't scale the same way, so gathering equipment is a better investment than raw levels beyond a moderate threshold.

## Strengths
- Remains one of the most effective gatherers throughout the entire game, not just early on.

## Weaknesses
- Zero combat value; purely a gathering-only investment.
