# Gaius Marius

## Overview
Gaius Marius ("Reformer of the Legions") is one of the most efficient and easiest-to-build early-game gathering commanders, particularly strong for wood, stone, and food gathering.

## Statistics
> Verify exact numbers in-game. Base power: ~300.

- Specialties: Gathering
- Troop type: Mixed (gathering-focused)

## Skills
- **Passive — Founder of Empire:** Significantly boosts gathering speed.
- **Passive — Marius's Reform:** Increases troop load capacity, reducing trips to resource nodes.

## Recommended Usage
Dedicated early-game gathering commander, best assigned to food gathering given his skill emphasis, or wood/stone depending on need.

## Best Pairings
- **Šárka** or **Constance** — pairing with either maximizes gathering efficiency as a cornerstone of early resource management.

## Equipment
- Gathering-speed and load-capacity equipment sets.

## Strategy Tips
- Build entirely around the Gathering talent tree — no other investment is worthwhile for this commander.

## Strengths
- Extremely easy to build and highly efficient for early-game gathering.

## Weaknesses
- Zero combat value; purely a gathering-only investment.
