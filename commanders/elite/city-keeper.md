# City Keeper

## Overview
City Keeper is a basic early-game Infantry commander, primarily useful as a secondary providing simple Infantry stat buffs before better commanders become available.

## Statistics
> Verify exact numbers in-game. Recommended skill levels: 5-5-5-5 (easy to max since sculptures are cheap to obtain).

- Specialties: Infantry
- Troop type: Infantry

## Skills
- **Active:** Increases troop defense for 5 seconds (Defense Bonus scaling 3%/6%/9%/12%/15% by skill level).
- **Passive:** Increases Infantry health (scaling 1%/2%/3%/4%/5% by skill level).
- **Passive:** Increases Infantry attack.

## Recommended Usage
Early-game Infantry secondary for fighting barbarians; not intended for advanced/late-game play.

## Best Pairings
- Best paired with any stronger Infantry primary in the early game — his own kit is simple and easily replaced later.

## Equipment
- Basic Infantry equipment; not worth heavy investment.

## Strategy Tips
- Max all his skills early since sculptures are cheap and easy to obtain — there's little reason not to fully build him at the start of the game.

## Strengths
- Cheap, easy early-game Infantry support.

## Weaknesses
- Quickly outclassed; not viable for advanced gameplay.
