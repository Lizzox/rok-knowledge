# Markswoman

## Overview
Markswoman is an entry-level Elite Archer commander, most useful as a secondary in early combat and especially valued for her Peacekeeping talent tree, which sharply reduces the Action Point cost of attacking barbarians.

## Statistics
> Verify exact numbers in-game.

- Specialties: Archer, Peacekeeping
- Troop type: Archer

## Skills
- **Active:** Direct damage (low damage factor, roughly 50–100 depending on upgrade level).
- **Passive:** +2–10% damage against barbarians (scaling with level).
- **Passive:** +1–5% Archer attack, +1–5% Archer defense (scaling with level).

## Recommended Usage
Early-game barbarian farming via her Peacekeeping talent tree; not recommended for PvP.

## Best Pairings
- Any Archer commander as primary, with Markswoman as secondary.

## Equipment
- Action-point-reduction gathering/PvE equipment; Archer-attack gear if used as secondary in combat.

## Strategy Tips
- Talent priorities: Peacekeeping Tree (Quick Study, Insight, Trophy Hunter) can reduce Action Point cost per barbarian attack to as low as ~30; Archer Tree (Archer Mastery, Archer Defense, Archer Health) for combat stats.

## Strengths
- Excellent low-cost early barbarian farming via Action Point reduction.

## Weaknesses
- Poor PvP performance — avoid using her for that purpose.
