# Dragon Lancer

## Overview
Dragon Lancer is an Elite Cavalry commander whose main value is march speed, making him useful early on for map exploration, rune-gathering, and hunting resource gatherers rather than combat.

## Statistics
> Verify exact numbers in-game.

- Specialties: Cavalry, Mobility
- Troop type: Cavalry

## Skills
- **Active:** Direct damage plus extra damage over 2 seconds.
- **Passive:** +2–10% Cavalry march speed (scaling with level).
- **Passive:** +1–5% Cavalry attack and defense (scaling with level).

## Recommended Usage
Early-game mobility commander for map exploration, rune-gathering, and hunting gatherer marches; not effective for sustained PvP or PvE battles.

## Best Pairings
- **Lancelot** — an easy-to-upgrade Cavalry pairing whose passives directly stack march speed.

## Equipment
- March-speed and mobility-focused equipment sets.

## Strategy Tips
- Use the Mobility talent tree; don't expect combat performance from this commander — his value is entirely speed-based.

## Strengths
- Strong early-game map mobility for exploration and rune-carrying.

## Weaknesses
- Ineffective for real PvP/PvE combat; quickly outclassed.
