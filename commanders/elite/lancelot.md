# Lancelot

## Overview
Lancelot ("Knight of the Lake") is an Elite Cavalry commander whose main appeal is very high mobility, making him useful for rune-carrying, hunting gatherers, and quickly reaching map objectives rather than serious combat.

## Statistics
> Verify exact numbers in-game. Base power: ~300.

- Specialties: Cavalry
- Troop type: Cavalry

## Skills
- **Active:** Deals massive damage to a single target.
- **Passive:** Increases Cavalry march speed and attack.
- **Passive:** ~10% chance, when attacked, to reduce all damage taken on the next turn.
- **Passive:** When at ≤50% units remaining, increases all damage and march speed (comeback mechanic).

## Recommended Usage
Early-game mobility commander for rune-grabbing, gatherer-hunting, and fast map traversal.

## Best Pairings
- **Cao Cao** — shares the same talent tree, so either can be primary with similar results.
- **Dragon Lancer** (Elite) — an easy-to-upgrade Cavalry pairing, not top-tier but simple to build.

## Equipment
- March-speed and mobility-focused equipment sets.

## Strategy Tips
- Prioritize him specifically for speed-critical tasks rather than sustained combat, where he's outclassed by Epic/Legendary Cavalry commanders.

## Strengths
- Excellent early-game mobility for map objectives.

## Weaknesses
- Not competitive for sustained combat once better commanders are available.
