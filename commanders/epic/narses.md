# Narses

## Overview
Narses is an Epic Engineering/Versatility/Defense commander whose purpose is debuffing enemies and generating rage rather than dealing large direct damage himself.

## Statistics
> Verify exact numbers in-game.

- Specialties: Engineering, Versatility, Defense
- Troop type: Siege

## Skills
- **Active:** Grants troops 40 rage and a healing factor of ~250 in ranged mode, enabling faster skill cycling and debuff application.
- **Expertise:** Boosts his 3rd skill ("Western Wall of the Empire"), increasing rage granted from 40 to 60 and the healing factor from ~250 to ~400.

## Recommended Usage
Best used as a secondary commander given a weak personal talent tree; primary use only recommended after fully unlocking Expertise, and then mainly for open field.

## Best Pairings
- **Babur** — the only commander noted to have meaningful synergy with Narses; Babur's active skill also grants rage per second, reinforcing Narses's rage-support role.
- **Catherine de' Medici** — the default F2P Epic Engineering pairing partner.

## Equipment
- Rage-generation and Engineering equipment sets.

## Strategy Tips
- Do not invest heavily in his talent tree unless running him as primary — as secondary, his talents don't apply, and his kit's value (rage/healing) still comes through regardless.

## Strengths
- Useful rage-generation and healing support for Engineering marches.

## Weaknesses
- Weak personal talent tree and low direct damage; limited synergy options among other Engineering commanders.
