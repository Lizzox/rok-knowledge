# Joan of Arc

## Overview
Joan of Arc is the Epic base version of the commander later reworked into Legendary [[joan-of-arc-prime]]. She is a starting commander granted with the France civilization, valued as one of the best support/farming commanders in the early game, with no troop-type restriction.

## Statistics
> Verify exact percentages in-game.

- Specialties: Integration, Gathering, Support
- Troop type: Any (unrestricted)

## Skills
- **Active — Divine Revelation:** Elevates troop stats (health, defense, attack) and increases rage generation — regarded as one of the best skills any commander has.

## Recommended Usage
Early-game support/gathering commander usable in any troop-type composition; a strong starter investment for new France-civilization players.

## Best Pairings
- **Charles Martel** — defense and survivability.
- **Saladin** — adds Cavalry power and versatility.
- **Alexander the Great** — boosts attack and skill damage.
- **Aethelflaed** — potent support march with enemy stat reduction.

## Equipment
- Support/rage-generation equipment sets.

## Strategy Tips
- Talent build: the Support tree is her most potent, emphasizing rage generation and mixed-troop buffs.

## Strengths
- Completely troop-type-agnostic support kit; excellent starting-commander value.

## Weaknesses
- Eventually surpassed by her own Legendary "Prime" rework and other top-tier Legendaries.
