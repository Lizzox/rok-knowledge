# Baibars

## Overview
Baibars is a widely-recommended Epic Cavalry/Skill commander known for strong skill damage output relative to his rarity, making him a popular budget alternative/secondary to Legendary cavalry commanders.

## Statistics
> Verify exact damage factors/rage costs in-game.

- Specialties: Cavalry, Skill
- Troop type: Cavalry

## Skills
- **Active (unlock 1★):** Skill damage to target and nearby enemies.
- **Passive (unlock 2★):** Increases skill damage.
- **Passive (unlock 3★):** Increases attack/rage generation.
- **Passive (unlock 4★):** Further damage scaling.

## Recommended Usage
Strong early-to-mid game Cavalry primary or secondary for open-field and rallies before Legendary cavalry commanders are available.

## Best Pairings
- Paired with Cao Cao or Sun Tzu for a full budget-friendly rally comp.

## Alternative Pairings
- Substitutes reasonably for Alexander the Great in budget rosters.

## Equipment
- Skill-damage and cavalry-attack equipment sets.

## Strategy Tips
- A good long-term investment even after acquiring Legendaries, since he can serve as a strong secondary commander.

## Strengths
- Excellent damage-per-investment ratio for an Epic commander.

## Weaknesses
- Eventually surpassed by Legendary cavalry commanders at the top competitive tier.
