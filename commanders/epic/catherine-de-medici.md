# Catherine de' Medici

## Overview
Catherine de' Medici is a free-to-obtain Epic Engineering (Siege) commander, offering ranged damage plus a randomized support buff for nearby allied troops.

## Statistics
> Verify exact numbers in-game.

- Specialties: Engineering, Versatility, Skill
- Troop type: Siege

## Skills
- **Active:** ~500 direct damage at range, or ~900 direct damage if the enemy march is in melee range.
- **Passive:** Using an active skill grants up to 3 nearby allied/friendly troops a random +5% bonus to attack, defense, or health for 3 seconds.
- **Passive:** +15% Siege attack, +10% march speed.
- **Passive:** Ranged basic attacks have a ~30% chance to deal additional ranged damage each second for 2 seconds.

## Recommended Usage
Free F2P-accessible ranged Siege commander for early Engineering-focused marches.

## Best Pairings
- **Narses**, **Stephen III**, **Philip II**, **Gajah Mada**, **Gonzalo de Córdoba** — all viable Engineering partners.
- Notably: she is the only free Epic Engineering commander, making her the default F2P pairing option for Narses.

## Equipment
- Engineering/siege equipment sets.

## Strategy Tips
- As a free commander, she's an accessible entry point into Engineering-focused compositions before investing in Legendary Siege commanders.

## Strengths
- Free to obtain; solid ranged damage plus a useful randomized ally buff.

## Weaknesses
- Outclassed by Legendary Engineering commanders at higher play tiers.
