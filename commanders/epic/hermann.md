# Hermann

## Overview
Hermann is an Epic Infantry/Skill commander recognized for a kit favoring ambush-style burst damage and defensive utility, with a Legendary "Prime" rework (Hermann Prime) available as a stronger version of the same archetype.

## Statistics
> Verify exact percentages/rage costs in-game.

- Specialties: Infantry, Skill
- Troop type: Infantry

## Skills
- **Active (unlock 1★):** Skill damage, often stronger when ambushing/attacking first.
- **Passive (unlock 2★):** Increases defense.
- **Passive (unlock 3★):** Increases attack or damage reduction.
- **Passive (unlock 4★):** Further scaling.

## Recommended Usage
Infantry primary for open-field ambush plays and garrison defense.

## Best Pairings
- Paired with a rage-generation secondary to trigger his active skill more frequently.

## Alternative Pairings
- Hermann Prime (Legendary) as an upgrade path if available.

## Equipment
- Defense and skill-damage equipment sets.

## Strategy Tips
- Effective for players who favor ambush/ ganking playstyles on the open-field map.

## Strengths
- Good burst-plus-durability balance for an Epic Infantry commander.

## Weaknesses
- Less reliable in prolonged straight-up rally combat than dedicated tanks.
