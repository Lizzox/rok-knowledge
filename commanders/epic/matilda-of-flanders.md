# Matilda of Flanders

## Overview
Matilda of Flanders is an Epic Integration/Gathering/Defense commander from Britain, obtainable via Expedition and Tavern, offering strong resource-gathering bonuses. Community sources note she is terrible in PvP.

## Statistics
> Verify exact percentages in-game.

- Specialties: Integration, Gathering, Defense
- Troop type: Siege (gathering-focused)

## Skills
- **Passive:** +5% stone gathering speed, +3% food and wood gathering speed.
- **Passive:** +10% bonus resources upon completing gathering.
- **Passive:** +10% Siege unit health, +10% Siege unit defense.

## Recommended Usage
Dedicated gathering-march commander; not suited to combat roles.

## Best Pairings
- Any commander works reasonably as a gathering partner since no single commander offers a major additional gathering boost with her specifically.
- **Constance** — together grants +20% more gems from gathering.

## Equipment
- Gathering-speed and load-capacity equipment sets.

## Strategy Tips
- Talent priorities: Gathering Mastery (Wood, Food, Stone, Gold), Modified Axle, The More The Better, Armed Convoy, Superior Tools.

## Strengths
- Strong resource-gathering speed and bonus-resource generation; useful troop-capacity increase.

## Weaknesses
- Explicitly poor in PvP — a gathering-only investment.
