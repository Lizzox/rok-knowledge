# Eulji Mundeok

## Overview
Eulji Mundeok is an Epic Infantry/Garrison/Attack commander with an unusual talent-tree combination lacking a Skill tree entirely, focused instead on raw normal-attack power and defense reduction.

## Statistics
> Verify exact numbers in-game.

- Specialties: Infantry, Garrison, Attack
- Troop type: Infantry

## Skills
- **Active — Water Attack:** Damage factor ~750; reduces target defense by 30% for 2 seconds — a strong nuking skill.
- **Passive — Cold Welcome:** +5% defense when serving as garrison commander.
- **Passive — Battle of Salsu:** +10% Infantry attack and defense.
- **Passive:** ~10% chance, when assaulted, to boost his own troops' damage the following turn.

## Recommended Usage
Open-field Infantry attacker who values raw attack power; also usable as a garrison commander.

## Best Pairings
- **Sun Tzu** (Epic) — best pairing; buffs Infantry health by 10% and supplies the rage engine Eulji Mundeok's kit lacks, forming a full Infantry army.
- **Boudica** (Boudica secondary) — despite lacking direct troop-type synergy (Boudica is universal, not Infantry), her high single-target damage complements Eulji Mundeok's attack-focused open-field playstyle; Eulji Mundeok should remain primary.

## Equipment
- Attack and defense-shred equipment sets.

## Strategy Tips
- Talent priority (Attack Tree): Burning Blood (rage restoration for skill spam), Lord of War and Effortless (damage increases).

## Strengths
- Strong defense-shred nuking skill; unusual attack-focused kit without needing Skill-tree investment.

## Weaknesses
- Lacks a rage engine of his own — benefits significantly from a rage-generating partner like Sun Tzu.
