# Bjorn Ironside

## Overview
Bjorn Ironside is a well-regarded Epic Infantry/Skill commander, often used as a budget-friendly Infantry tank before players acquire Legendary tanks like Richard I or Charles Martel.

## Statistics
> Verify exact percentages/rage costs in-game.

- Specialties: Infantry, Skill
- Troop type: Infantry

## Skills
- **Active (unlock 1★):** Skill damage with a control or debuff effect.
- **Passive (unlock 2★):** Increases defense.
- **Passive (unlock 3★):** Increases health/damage reduction.
- **Passive (unlock 4★):** Further defensive scaling.

## Recommended Usage
Early-to-mid game garrison defender and Infantry open-field tank.

## Best Pairings
- Paired with Sun Tzu or Cao Cao for a durable, sustain-oriented Infantry march.

## Alternative Pairings
- Substitutes for Guan Yu/Richard I/Charles Martel in budget rosters.

## Equipment
- Defense/health equipment sets.

## Strategy Tips
- A solid long-term keep even after acquiring Legendary tanks, useful as a secondary garrison commander in a second city or alt account.

## Strengths
- Strong durability for an Epic-tier commander.

## Weaknesses
- Lower damage ceiling than Legendary Infantry tanks.
