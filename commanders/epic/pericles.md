# Pericles

## Overview
Pericles is an Epic Infantry/Support commander, easy to upgrade and recommended for new players — a durability-focused Infantry commander offering shields and march-speed utility.

## Statistics
> Verify exact numbers in-game.

- Specialties: Infantry, Support
- Troop type: Infantry

## Skills
- **Active:** Grants his troop a shield (damage factor ~700) for 3 seconds; the shield also increases Infantry march speed by ~20%.
- **Passive:** +10% Infantry attack, +10% Infantry defense.
- **Passive:** +10% Infantry health; reduces enemy troop march speed by ~10%.

## Recommended Usage
Early-to-mid game Infantry tank; good starter Epic for new players due to ease of upgrading.

## Best Pairings
- **Alexander the Great** — one of the most powerful pairings in the game, adding further defense/offense buffs.
- **Eulji Mundeok** (Eulji primary) — Eulji's Attack tree and -30% enemy defense active skill outperform a pure Support approach.
- **Bjorn Ironside** (Pericles primary, tank) — Pericles tanks while Bjorn Ironside nukes as secondary.

## Equipment
- Defense and shield-boosting equipment sets.

## Strategy Tips
- Talent priority: Support Tree first for Rejuvenate (rage), then Infantry Tree for stat scaling.

## Strengths
- Easy to upgrade, strong early-game durability, useful shield and march-speed utility.

## Weaknesses
- Outclassed by Legendary Infantry tanks in the late game.
