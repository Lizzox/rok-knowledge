# Scipio Africanus

## Overview
Scipio Africanus is an Epic Cavalry/Skill commander offering solid all-round cavalry skill damage, commonly recommended as a budget cavalry pick or secondary support commander.

## Statistics
> Verify exact percentages/rage costs in-game.

- Specialties: Cavalry, Skill
- Troop type: Cavalry

## Skills
- **Active (unlock 1★):** Skill damage to target and nearby enemies.
- **Passive (unlock 2★):** Increases attack.
- **Passive (unlock 3★):** Increases skill damage or rage generation.
- **Passive (unlock 4★):** Further scaling.

## Recommended Usage
Budget Cavalry primary/secondary for open-field and rallies before Legendary cavalry commanders are unlocked.

## Best Pairings
- Paired with Sun Tzu or Baibars for a cost-effective Cavalry comp.

## Alternative Pairings
- Substitutes for Alexander the Great or Attila in budget rosters.

## Equipment
- Skill-damage and cavalry-attack equipment sets.

## Strategy Tips
- Good stepping-stone commander while building toward a full Legendary cavalry lineup.

## Strengths
- Reliable, no-frills cavalry damage for an Epic-tier commander.

## Weaknesses
- Outclassed by Legendary cavalry commanders in high-tier competitive play.
