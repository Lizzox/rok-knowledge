# Diaochan

## Overview
Diaochan is an Epic Peacekeeping/Support commander explicitly described by community sources as a pure PvE commander — excellent for barbarian hunting and commander leveling, but not versatile in PvP.

## Statistics
> Verify exact numbers in-game.

- Specialties: Peacekeeping, Support
- Troop type: Mixed (PvE-focused)

## Skills
- **Active — Dance of Chaos:** Small direct damage (damage factor ~150) over 4 seconds.
- **Passive — Dance of the Silver Lotus:** Increases damage against barbarians and boosts EXP gain for both commanders in the pairing.

## Recommended Usage
Dedicated barbarian-farming and commander-leveling support commander; avoid for PvP.

## Best Pairings
- **Lu Bu** — thematic synergy but generally not recommended in practice since most players won't max Lu Bu or find better partners for him.
- **Cao Cao** — good damage/Cavalry stats plus complementary small healing.
- **Joan of Arc** — great PvP pairing on new kingdoms with stats, buffs, and healing, usable with any troop type.
- **Lohar** — strong barbarian-farming pair; both provide healing and fast EXP gain for new players.

## Equipment
- Support/healing equipment sets.

## Strategy Tips
- PvP talent build: focus on support and rage generation, useful when running 3+ troop types.
- PvE talent build: focus on Peacekeeping/Support for healing plus damage/EXP gain for commanders.
- Talent: Burning Blood grants +9 rage per normal attack, improving rage regeneration and skill uptime.

## Strengths
- Excellent EXP-farming and barbarian-hunting utility, especially valuable for leveling new commanders quickly.

## Weaknesses
- Explicitly not viable for PvP — a PvE-only investment.
