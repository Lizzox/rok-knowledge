# Kusunoki Masashige

## Overview
Kusunoki Masashige is an Epic Archer/Peacekeeping commander notable for a cleanse-plus-damage active skill, offering both AoE Archer damage and utility against enemy control effects.

## Statistics
> Verify exact numbers in-game — figures below are community-reported.

- Specialties: Archer, Peacekeeping
- Troop type: Archer

## Skills
- **Active:** Removes all negative/control effects from his troops and deals direct damage to up to 3 targets in a fan (damage factor up to ~800), plus additional damage each second for 2 more seconds.
- **Passive — Bravery and Wisdom:** +10% Archer attack and defense, strongest with a pure-Archer build.
- **Passive — Tragedy's End:** Small chance to strike the enemy for ~900 damage over 2 seconds.

## Recommended Usage
Two main builds: a Field Fight build maximizing PvP damage, or a Garrison build for defense — choose based on intended role.

## Best Pairings
- **Yi Seong-Gye** — excellent area-damage pairing, especially strong in Canyon.
- **Hermann** (Epic) — cheap, accessible early-game pairing with a similar kit and strong rage engine/damage reduction for open field.
- **Richard I** — provides continuous healing, keeping Kusunoki's Infantry-adjacent Archer march alive longer — ideal for defensive strategies or protecting a city in KvK.

## Equipment
- Skill-damage and archer-attack equipment sets.

## Strategy Tips
- His cleanse effect is uniquely valuable against control-heavy enemy comps — factor this into pairing/role decisions beyond pure damage output.

## Strengths
- Rare cleanse-plus-damage combo skill; flexible between Field Fight and Garrison builds.

## Weaknesses
- An Epic-rarity commander, eventually outclassed by Legendary Archer specialists in the highest tiers of play.
