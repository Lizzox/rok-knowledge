# Boudica

## Overview
Boudica is the Epic base version of the commander later reworked into Legendary [[boudica-prime]]. Her strength lies primarily in PvE barbarian/fort farming, with reasonable but not top-tier PvP performance on newer servers.

## Statistics
> Verify exact percentages in-game.

- Specialties: Peacekeeping, Skill
- Troop type: Mixed

## Skills
- **Active:** Deals massive damage to the target and reduces its rage.
- **Passive:** +25% damage against barbarians and other neutral units, +20% commander EXP gained from those targets.

## Recommended Usage
Barbarian/fort-farming commander; on new servers she can hold up reasonably in PvP, but she does not compete with strong Legendary commanders on older, more developed servers.

## Best Pairings
- **Lohar** — best PvE combo; both offer self-healing and bonus barbarian/neutral-unit damage plus EXP gain, especially strong early-to-mid game since both are easily obtainable Epics.
- **Minamoto no Yoshitsune**, **Cao Cao** — also cited as strong pairings.

## Equipment
- Rage-generation and PvE-damage equipment sets.

## Strategy Tips
- Talent build: start Peacekeeping Tree for Insight (reduces Action Point cost for barbarian attacks), then Rejuvenate in the Skill Tree.

## Strengths
- Strong PvE barbarian/fort farming; solid early PvP option on new servers.

## Weaknesses
- Not competitive against strong Legendary commanders on mature servers.
