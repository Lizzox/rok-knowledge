# Osman I

## Overview
Osman I ("Osman Gazi") is an Epic Leadership/Conquering/Skill commander celebrated for exceptional nuking capability and mixed-troop flexibility, considered one of the strongest Epic-rarity commanders.

## Statistics
> Verify exact numbers in-game — figures below are community-reported.

- Specialties: Leadership, Conquering, Skill
- Troop type: Leadership (mixed-troop capable)

## Skills
- **Active — The Empire Awakens:** Damage factor ~850 to target; should be maxed first.
- **Passive — Radiance of Bursa:** After attacking a city for 30+ seconds, +15% troop attack for 120 seconds.
- **Passive (4th skill):** +10% troop capacity, boosting overall offensive index.

## Recommended Usage
Rage-engine nuker for mixed-troop marches, particularly strong against cities in prolonged sieges.

## Best Pairings
- **Yi Seong-Gye** — a top pairing; YSG's damage amplification makes for a deadly combo.
- **Aethelflaed** — crowd control and AoE debuffs complement Osman's raging playstyle.
- **Genghis Khan** — pairing can effectively double combined offensive strength.
- **Julius Caesar** — buffs plus enemy debuffs form a fast, efficient kill combo.

## Equipment
- Skill-damage and rage-generation equipment sets.

## Strategy Tips
- Talent priority: Skill Tree first for Rejuvenate — grants 150 rage per active skill use (300 total across both commanders), instantly filling roughly a third of the rage bar.

## Strengths
- Exceptional nuking output for an Epic-rarity commander; strong city-siege scaling passive.

## Weaknesses
- Eventually surpassed by Legendary Leadership/Conquering commanders at the highest competitive tiers.
