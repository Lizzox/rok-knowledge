# Lohar

## Overview
Lohar is an Epic Integration/Peacekeeping/Support commander purpose-built for PvE — specifically barbarian hunting — offering healing, a large damage bonus against barbarians, and a substantial EXP boost for leveling other commanders.

## Statistics
> Verify exact numbers in-game — figures below are community-reported.

- Specialties: Integration, Peacekeeping, Support
- Troop type: Mixed (PvE-focused)

## Skills
- **Active — Overwhelming Force:** Damages a single target while simultaneously healing his own wounded troops.
- **Passive:** +35% damage against barbarians, significantly speeding up barbarian farming.
- **Passive:** +70% EXP gain — dramatically shortens commander-leveling time.

## Recommended Usage
Dedicated barbarian-farming commander for efficiently leveling other commanders through EXP gain.

## Best Pairings
- **Aethelflaed** (Aethelflaed primary) — best pairing; her AoE active skill hits multiple barbarians while providing health/attack/defense debuffs and bonus barbarian damage.
- **Boudica** — restores rage and heals troops simultaneously, boosting overall damage/healing output when farming barbarians.
- Generally: pair Lohar with any commander offering strong damage, ideally with healing as well, to optimize output.
