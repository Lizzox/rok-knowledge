# Belisarius

## Overview
Belisarius is an Epic Archer/Garrison commander valued for strong garrison-defense utility combined with Archer skill damage, making him a popular pick for defending cities against rallies. A Legendary "Prime" rework, Belisarius Prime, exists as a separate stronger commander.

## Statistics
> Verify exact percentages/rage costs in-game.

- Specialties: Archer, Garrison
- Troop type: Archer

## Skills
- **Active (unlock 1★):** Skill damage to attacking enemies.
- **Passive (unlock 2★):** Increases garrison defense.
- **Passive (unlock 3★):** Reduces damage taken while defending.
- **Passive (unlock 4★):** Further defensive scaling.

## Recommended Usage
Primary garrison defender against enemy rallies, especially effective versus Infantry-heavy attackers.

## Best Pairings
- Paired with another Garrison-specialty commander for maximum city defense.

## Alternative Pairings
- Belisarius Prime (Legendary) if available, for a stronger version of the same archetype.

## Equipment
- Defense and garrison-focused equipment sets.

## Strategy Tips
- Prioritize for players who are frequently rallied against and need a dedicated defensive commander rather than an offensive one.

## Strengths
- Strong, specialized garrison-defense utility.

## Weaknesses
- Limited value outside of garrison/defense contexts.
