# Keira

## Overview
Keira is an Epic Archer/Peacekeeping/Skill commander noted for a very high damage-amplification signature skill ("The Mutineer"), making her a strong budget Archer nuker.

## Statistics
> Verify exact numbers in-game.

- Specialties: Archer, Peacekeeping, Skill
- Troop type: Archer

## Skills
- **Passive — The Mutineer:** Increases damage by up to ~150% — her signature, most valuable skill to prioritize.
- **Passive (4th skill):** Increases damage by up to ~80% under certain conditions.

## Recommended Usage
Archer primary in a full-Archer march; benefits significantly from rage-generation secondaries.

## Best Pairings
- **Yi Seong-Gye** — first-choice pairing; benefits heavily from Keira's kit, especially her 4th skill's +80% damage bonus.
- **Sun Tzu** (Epic, secondary) — an all-Archer showstopper pairing; Sun Tzu's rage generation plus Keira's high damage factor.
- **Hermann** (Epic) — similarly strong Archer-Archer synergy with high rage potential and damage output.

## Equipment
- Skill-damage and archer-attack equipment sets.

## Strategy Tips
- Talent build: Archer tree is the clear best choice, since her third skill focuses heavily on Archer units, generating additional damage and rage.

## Strengths
- Very high personal damage amplification for an Epic-rarity commander.

## Weaknesses
- Requires a full-Archer composition and strong rage-generation partner to reach full potential.
