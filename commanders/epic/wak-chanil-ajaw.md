# Wak Chanil Ajaw

## Overview
Wak Chanil Ajaw is an Epic Integration/Gathering/Defense commander used exclusively for gathering — notably lacking a gold-gathering bonus, a meaningful limitation in the late game.

## Statistics
> Verify exact percentages in-game.

- Specialties: Integration, Gathering, Defense
- Troop type: Mixed (gathering-focused)

## Skills
- **Passive:** +10% unit capacity for her troop.
- **Passive:** ~20% chance, on landing or receiving a basic attack, to recover a portion of slightly-wounded units.

## Recommended Usage
Gathering commander for food, wood, and stone specifically — not gold.

## Best Pairings
- Any other gathering commander, e.g. **Joan of Arc** — no single partner offers a standout additional boost.

## Equipment
- Gathering-speed and load-capacity equipment sets.

## Strategy Tips
- Talent priority: Gathering tree first, with leftover points into Integration or Defense.
- Because she lacks gold-gathering bonuses, pair her primarily with Tier 1-4 resource farming rather than relying on her for gold, which matters more once Tier 5 units are in play.

## Strengths
- Solid unit-capacity and gathering utility for food/wood/stone.

## Weaknesses
- No gold-gathering bonus — a real gap in the late game when gold becomes the priority resource.
