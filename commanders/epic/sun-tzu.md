# Sun Tzu

## Overview
Sun Tzu is the classic Epic Leadership/Support commander — for years considered one of the best Epic-tier picks in the game due to AoE damage, damage reduction, health buffs, and rage generation all in one kit. A Legendary rework, [[sun-tzu-prime]], exists as a separate, stronger commander.

## Statistics
> Verify exact percentages/rage costs in-game.

- Specialties: Leadership, Skill
- Troop type: Leadership

## Skills
- **Active (unlock 1★):** AoE skill damage.
- **Passive (unlock 2★):** Reduces damage taken and increases health.
- **Passive (unlock 3★):** Increases rage generation.
- **Passive (unlock 4★):** Increases skill damage.

## Recommended Usage
Excellent budget/early-game rally leader and general-purpose support commander; remains useful even into the mid/late game as a secondary if Sun Tzu Prime isn't available.

## Best Pairings
- Paired with strong Cavalry or Archer damage-dealing primaries as a rage/defense-boosting secondary.

## Alternative Pairings
- Cao Cao (similar archetype) as an alternative if available.

## Equipment
- Rage-generation and defensive equipment sets.

## Strategy Tips
- One of the best "first Epic to invest in" recommendations for new players due to broad utility across rally, garrison, and open-field roles.

## Strengths
- Extremely well-rounded kit for an Epic-rarity commander; excellent F2P value.

## Weaknesses
- Outclassed by Sun Tzu Prime and top Legendary supports at the highest competitive tiers.
