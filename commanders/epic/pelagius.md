# Pelagius

## Overview
Pelagius is an Epic Cavalry/Garrison commander offering strong nuking with built-in rage restoration, plus enough garrison utility to double as a defensive commander.

## Statistics
> Verify exact numbers in-game.

- Specialties: Cavalry, Garrison
- Troop type: Cavalry

## Skills
- **Active:** Deals up to ~900 damage to the enemy and restores a decent amount of rage, extending nuking uptime.
- **Passive — Founder of Asturias / Expertise — Oath of Asseuua:** Reinforce his identity as a Cavalry-focused leader.
- **Third/Fourth skills — Retaking Lost Territory / The Mutineer:** Support a secondary garrison-commander role.

## Recommended Usage
Budget Cavalry nuker for open field, or a serviceable garrison defender if needed.

## Best Pairings
- **Cao Cao** (Cao Cao primary) — both Cavalry specialists with complementary defense/attack passives.
- **Baibars** (Epic) — adds AoE damage and march-speed slow alongside +20% Cavalry attack.
- **Boudica** — considered the best pick; generates rage while reducing enemy rage, adds healing, and reduces enemy attack — synergizing with Pelagius's own healing factor.
- **Minamoto**, **Genghis Khan**, **Belisarius** (Epic) — also solid alternatives.

## Equipment
- Skill-damage and rage-generation equipment sets.

## Strategy Tips
- His rage-restoring active skill makes him a strong pick for players who want consistent nuking uptime without heavy rage-generation equipment investment.

## Strengths
- Strong nuking with built-in rage sustain; flexible between offensive Cavalry and garrison roles.

## Weaknesses
- As an Epic commander, eventually surpassed by Legendary Cavalry specialists at the highest tiers.
