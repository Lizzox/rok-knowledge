# Imhotep

## Overview
Imhotep is an Epic Archer/Garrison/Support commander from Egypt, considered unusually powerful for Epic rarity — he deals little direct damage himself but provides an insane amount of debuff utility, especially when serving as a secondary commander with his Expertise unlocked.

## Statistics
> Verify exact numbers in-game.

- Specialties: Archer, Garrison, Support
- Troop type: Archer

## Skills
- **Active — The High Priest (best when Expertised):** Increases damage taken by up to 5 targets by 30% and reduces their rage by 50 per second. Requires Imhotep to be the **secondary** commander to take effect.

## Recommended Usage
Secondary support/debuff commander for KvK 1/2/3 and general rally support, rather than a primary damage dealer.

## Best Pairings
- **Hermann** — Hermann's rage reduction and silence stack with Imhotep's own rage-denial for near-total enemy shutdown; generally recommended over Kusunoki due to higher combined damage.
- **Kusunoki Masashige** — alternative pairing that clears negative effects on the march, though lacks Hermann's march-speed benefit.
- **Joan of Arc** (Epic) — can work as primary alongside Imhotep as secondary.

## Equipment
- Support/rage-denial equipment sets.

## Strategy Tips
- Must be run as secondary commander and ideally with Expertise unlocked to realize his full debuff value — as primary, his kit underperforms significantly.

## Strengths
- Exceptional rage-denial and damage-vulnerability debuffs for an Epic-rarity commander.

## Weaknesses
- Low personal damage output; full value requires secondary-slot usage and Expertise investment.
