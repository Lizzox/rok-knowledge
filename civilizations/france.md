# France

## Overview
France is regarded as probably the best field-fighting civilization, thanks to a large hospital healing-speed bonus that meaningfully reduces healing speedup consumption — especially valuable in longer wars where sustained combat activity would otherwise drain speedup stockpiles quickly.

## Bonuses
- +20% hospital healing speed

## Best For
Players prioritizing open-field fighting and sustained war participation, where healing throughput is a real bottleneck.
