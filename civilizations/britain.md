# Britain

## Overview
Britain is a dependable Archer-focused civilization, with an attack bonus that boosts ranged troop damage in both PvP and PvE content, alongside faster troop training.

## Bonuses
- Boudica sculptures (bonus acquisition)
- Increased troop training speed
- Archer attack bonus

## Best For
Archer-primary accounts wanting a civilization bonus that directly reinforces their troop-type specialization.
