# Germany

## Overview
Germany is frequently cited as the best civilization for post-KvK, long-term play, since its bonuses (training speed, action point recovery, cavalry attack) remain consistently relevant across every game phase rather than front-loading value into the early game.

## Bonuses
- +5% troop training speed
- +10% action point recovery
- +5% Cavalry attack
- Teutonic Knight special unit

## Best For
Players optimizing for long-term account value across the full game lifetime, and Cavalry-primary accounts specifically.
