# Maya

## Overview
Maya's standout bonus is +10% gold gathering speed — valuable since gold is the most critical, hardest-to-obtain resource in the game (no passive production). Its combat bonuses (Archer defense, normal damage) are comparatively minor and mostly benefit ranged-focused players.

## Bonuses
- +5% Archer defense
- +3% normal damage (all troop types)
- +10% gold gathering speed

## Best For
Gathering-focused accounts specifically valuing gold throughput; a weaker pick for players prioritizing Infantry or Cavalry combat, since it provides no bonuses for those troop types.
