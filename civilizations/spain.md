# Spain

## Overview
Spain offers a substantial passive resource-production bonus alongside Cavalry defense and bonus EXP from barbarian/neutral-unit kills, making it strong for both economy-focused play and commander leveling via barbarian farming.

## Bonuses
- +20% resource production
- +5% Cavalry defense
- +10% EXP gained from defeated barbarians and neutral units

## Best For
Economy-focused accounts and players who farm barbarians heavily for commander leveling.
