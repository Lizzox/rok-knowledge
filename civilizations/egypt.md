# Egypt

## Overview
Egypt combines an Archer attack bonus with a damage bonus specifically against rallied objectives (flags, forts, cities) — excellent for players who continuously rally objectives, but the objective-damage bonus provides no value in open-field combat.

## Bonuses
- +5% Archer attack
- +5% damage to rallied troops (flags, forts, and cities)
- +1.5% research and building speed

## Best For
Players whose primary activity is rallying objectives rather than open-field combat — a poor fit if open-field fighting is your main focus.
