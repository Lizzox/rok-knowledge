# Rome

## Overview
Rome excels at defending cities and structures, with an infantry defense bonus that directly toughens garrison marches, plus solid food-gathering and march-speed bonuses for general economy.

## Bonuses
- +5% Infantry defense
- +10% food gathering speed
- +5% troop march speed

## Best For
Players prioritizing garrison defense and Infantry-focused play, or those who want a balanced economy/defense hybrid.
