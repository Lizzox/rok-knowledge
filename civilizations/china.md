# China

## Overview
China is widely cited as the strongest all-round pick for new players, combining a meaningful early-game economy bonus with Sun Tzu — regarded as the strongest Epic commander in the game — as a free starting commander.

## Bonuses
- +5% building speed
- +5% action point recovery

## Best For
New players and anyone prioritizing fast early-game City Hall progression, since the building-speed bonus is most impactful during the early stages when construction queues dominate playtime.

## Starting Commander
Sun Tzu — see `commanders/epic/sun-tzu.md`.
