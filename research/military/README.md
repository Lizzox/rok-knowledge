# Military Research

Detailed strategy content lives in [`guides/economy/military-research-guide.md`](../../guides/economy/military-research-guide.md), covering Infantry, Cavalry, Archer, and Siege research priorities plus T5 prerequisites.

This folder is reserved for future machine-readable per-technology data (exact research costs, times, and stat bonuses per node) once a verified, current source is confirmed — RoK's research tree is periodically rebalanced and a hardcoded per-node database would go stale quickly without a live sync.
