# Crystal Technology Overview

## Overview
Crystal Technology is a separate research tree unlocked during the Season of Conquest phase of KvK, funded by a resource called Crystals (distinct from standard gold/food/wood/stone) rather than the base game's normal research resources. It layers additional, season-specific combat and economic bonuses on top of a governor's existing base-game research.

## How It Works
- Crystals are earned through Crystal Mine production, completing Bastion quests (structures that unlock when your alliance/coalition controls a nearby building), and various other Season of Conquest activities.
- The Crystal Mine and Research Center must be upgraded (community sources cite level 25) to maximize crystal production speed and unlock higher-tier Crystal Technology nodes.
- Crystal Technology nodes span both economic (crystal production, resource bonuses) and war (troop-type-specific combat bonuses) categories, mirroring the base game's Economic/Military research split but with season-specific effects.

## Best Upgrade Order
General community consensus: **economy before war**. Build up Crystal Mine production and economic Crystal Technology first to establish a sustainable crystal income, then pivot to war technology once your primary troop type is confirmed for the season's combat needs.

## Crystal Spending Priorities
- Crystal Mine and Research Center upgrades to level 25 first, to maximize the rate at which everything downstream can be funded.
- Within war technology, focus on your account's single primary troop type rather than splitting investment — Crystal Technology rewards specialization the same way base-game military research does.

## F2P Optimization
- Chain barbarians consistently to help fund crystal/resource needs alongside standard Crystal Mine production.
- Prioritize cost-saving Crystal Technology nodes (which reduce the crystal cost of further research) early, since they compound value for the rest of the season.

## Whale Optimization
- The Premium Season Crystal Supply bundle (community-cited price ~$4.99) boosts Crystal Mine production speed by 50% and grants a daily crystal supply (~60,000 cited) — purchasing this on Day 1 of the season is specifically recommended to capture its full-season value, since its benefit compounds daily from the point of purchase.
