# Crystal Technology — Early Season

## Overview
The opening weeks of a Season of Conquest set the trajectory for the rest of the season — early Crystal Mine and economic-technology investment compounds daily, so mistakes here are costly to correct later.

## First Priorities
1. Upgrade the **Crystal Mine** and **Research Center** toward level 25 as quickly as possible — this maximizes crystal production speed and unlocks higher research tiers earlier.
2. Purchase the Premium Season Crystal Supply bundle (if spending) on Day 1 specifically — its daily crystal bonus compounds from the purchase date, so delaying the purchase permanently loses value.
3. Begin completing Bastion quests as soon as your alliance/coalition controls a nearby bastion-unlocking building — these are a steady supplementary crystal/favor source.

## Resource Management
- Don't spend crystals reactively — plan the first 2-3 Crystal Technology node purchases in advance so early income isn't wasted on low-value nodes.
- Chain barbarians consistently during this window; barbarian-clearing remains a solid supplementary resource/crystal source for F2P and low-spend players specifically in the early season.

## Growth Strategy
- Focus entirely on economic Crystal Technology during this phase — war technology matters far less before your kingdom's combat window opens, and economic nodes compound value the longer they're active.
- Confirm your account's primary troop type early (in coordination with alliance/kingdom leadership) so you know which war-technology branch to pivot to once the early-season economic push is complete.
