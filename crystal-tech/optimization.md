# Crystal Technology — Mid & Late Season Optimization

## Mid Season: Technology Optimization
- By mid-season, your economic Crystal Technology base should be largely established — shift remaining crystal income toward your confirmed primary troop type's war-technology branch.
- Reassess whether your kingdom's combat needs (rally-leading, garrison defense, open-field) match your current war-technology allocation, and adjust before committing further crystals to a mismatched branch.

## Mid Season: War Preparation
- Coordinate with alliance/kingdom leadership on which war-technology nodes matter most for your kingdom's specific upcoming combat objectives (structure defense vs. offensive pushes) rather than optimizing purely for personal preference.
- Continue Bastion quest completion — it remains a steady crystal/favor source throughout the season, not just early on.

## Late Season: Final Upgrades
- Prioritize completing any war-technology nodes directly relevant to your kingdom's final combat push over starting new economic investments — economic nodes have less time remaining to pay off their investment by late season.
- Spend down crystal stockpiles rather than hoarding them into the season's end, since unused crystals typically don't carry meaningful value into the next season/reset cycle.

## Late Season: Maximum Efficiency
- Focus every remaining crystal on your account's single most impactful missing node (usually a capstone war-technology bonus for your primary troop type) rather than spreading thin across multiple partially-completed branches.

## Which Technologies Matter
- Universal war-technology bonuses (troop-wide attack/defense/health) generally outperform narrow situational nodes for most accounts.
- Crystal-cost-reduction economic nodes matter most early, since their value is time-compounding — a cost-reduction node purchased late in the season has little time left to pay for itself.

## Which Technologies Are Traps
- Highly situational nodes (narrow troop-type or scenario-specific bonuses) purchased before your kingdom's actual combat needs are confirmed — wait for coordination with alliance leadership before committing crystals to niche nodes.
- Late-season economic investment, which has insufficient remaining season duration to pay off relative to a direct war-technology purchase.

## Recommended Paths
1. Economic foundation (Crystal Mine/Research Center to 25, core economic nodes) — early season.
2. Primary-troop-type war technology — mid season, once combat needs are confirmed.
3. Capstone/highest-impact remaining node — late season, spending down remaining crystal stockpile.
