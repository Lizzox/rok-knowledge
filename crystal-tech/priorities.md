# Crystal Technology Priority Checklist

## Overview
A condensed priority order combining the early/mid/late-season guidance from [[crystal-tech-beginner]] and [[crystal-tech-optimization]] into a single reference checklist.

## Priority Order (All Accounts)
1. Crystal Mine → level 25
2. Research Center → level 25
3. Core economic Crystal Technology nodes (crystal-cost reduction first, since they compound)
4. Confirm primary troop type with alliance/kingdom leadership
5. Primary-troop-type war technology nodes
6. Bastion quest completion (ongoing, throughout the season)
7. Capstone/highest-impact remaining node (late season, spend down remaining crystals)

## F2P Priority Adjustments
- Lean harder on barbarian-chaining and Bastion quests as crystal sources, since the Premium Season Crystal Supply bundle isn't available.
- Prioritize cost-reduction economic nodes even more heavily, since every crystal has to come from active play rather than a purchased daily supply.

## Whale Priority Adjustments
- Purchase the Premium Season Crystal Supply bundle on Day 1 of the season without delay.
- Consider pushing war-technology nodes earlier than the standard "economy first" sequencing if your kingdom's combat window opens sooner than average, since whale-level crystal income can sustain both tracks simultaneously.
